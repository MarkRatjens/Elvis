**Locations**: Streets and alleys of The Pale, market square, Graceland.

**Tone and Voice Sentence**: "The Elvi patrol combed the way forward as assiduously as they would comb their hair."

**Plot Summary**:
- Chilo, divided in philosophy from Kleoboulos, reflects on his scepticism towards their universe's supposed decay, his preference for stability, and his pragmatic approach to sagely interventions. This ideology underlines his foil relationship with the enigmatic prophecy facilitator.
- Tony and Sarah, escorted by a commando squad of Elvi, navigate deeper into The Pale toward Graceland amid deteriorating urban conditions. Amid rising tension, Sarah reveals the Sages’ shocking proposition to impregnate her, confounding Tony and unnerving their Elvi escorts, who regard Sarah as potentially holy.
- The Sages covertly follow, struggling to maintain subtlety while passing through the bustling crowds preparing for the Vegas Show, one of the most significant celebrations in the Ghetto. Acutely aware of the growing density and spectacle of Elvi culture, the Sages decide to blend in.
- The group encounters a savvy market vendor selling Elvi attire, insisting on traditional jumpsuits and capes for propriety's sake. The Sages reluctantly comply, stripping in public to don their Elvi-themed outfits. Though unsettled by the peculiar customs, they adopt the garb while receiving epinephrine auto-injectors under the guise of medical precaution, ostensibly required for entry to the event.
