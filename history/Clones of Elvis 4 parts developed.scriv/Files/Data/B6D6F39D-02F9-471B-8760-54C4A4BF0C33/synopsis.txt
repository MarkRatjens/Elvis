**Locations**: Sarah's townhouse in Eloise's compound; the parlour.

**Tone and Voice Sentence**: "Follow the earthquakes, Detective."

**Synopsis**:
Under Hayley’s firm questioning, Sarah reveals critical insights into the mysterious Gyges Disc, admitting she had possession of it before handing it over to Eloise. She details its strange effects, including causing an accident that she initially dismissed as a van hitting a pothole but later attributed to the disc’s disruptive properties. Sarah implies the disc is linked to localized earthquakes and expresses guilt over relinquishing it without understanding its full power. Hayley draws connections between the disc, its previous handlers—including Tony and Travis Teh—and the broader conspiracy involving PAPER and Eloise. As Sarah reluctantly recounts key events, including Johnny Stoat’s cryptic remarks about the disc, the narrative deepens around this enigmatic device, its dangers, and its ties to the unfolding prophecy.
