**Locations**: Sublime Point hover-pad; the city; streets near Travis Teh’s neighborhood.

**Synopsis**:
Under Eloise’s stringent conditions, Hayley ventures to the city with a Shakti Enterprises hover-copt, careful surveillance, and "expendable dentist assassins" in tow. Her investigation leads her to Stasis Anomalies Investigator Stockwell (“Chip”), a gruff and evasive Fridge Force agent tied to the Chuglingham residence, which neighbors Travis Teh’s alleged suicide site. Confronting Chip with call-out logs and connections to the Preservation Secrets Act, Hayley implies Fridge Force involvement in both the Chuglinghams’ mysterious disappearance and Teh’s death. Chip stonewalls with recited corporate lines, but Hayley’s pointed questions about national security and violations related to stasis tech visibly unsettle him. While Chip withdraws, warning Hayley she’ll hear from Fridge Force, her probing clearly implicates the agency in a deeper conspiracy surrounding stasis anomalies, national security, and covert maneuvers.
