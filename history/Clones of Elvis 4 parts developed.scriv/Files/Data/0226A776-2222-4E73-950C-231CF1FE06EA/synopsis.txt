**Locations**: Sarah’s parlour in the Elvi mansion.

**Tone and Voice Sentence**: "The multitude of camels shall cover thee."

**Synopsis**:
Sarah's day is disrupted by the arrival of Three Sages purportedly bearing gifts for her unborn child. Their explanation for their tardiness involves the malfunctioning Travelator’s "curse," instigating a barrage of convoluted, pseudo-scientific explanations. Amid growing tensions, Sarah demands to know which of them was responsible for the forced foetal implantation. The Sages defensively rationalize the procedure’s necessity and invoke prophecies of “The One After the One,” framing her child as central to a grand, apocalyptic vision of universal renewal. Their wild discussions incite frustration, chaos, and arguments about identity, religion, and the nature of prophecy. Sarah declares her intent to abort the pregnancy, dismissing their reasoning. The Sages finally present their questionable offerings: a two-dollar coin for gold, a half-used tube of analgesic for myrrh, and a bottle of toilet cleaner for frankincense.
