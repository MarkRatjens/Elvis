**Locations**: Elvi mansion, surrounding manicured grounds.

**Tone and Voice Sentence**: "Tony was a mother-fucking hero. Thank you very much."

**Plot Summary**:
- Rumors of Sarah’s pregnancy spread across the mansion staff, reaching the Elvi collective and external operators, escalating to fervent adoration for Tony. Despite his detainment, Tony is hailed by the Elvi as a hero and protector of the "Holy Momma," solidifying his cultural status.
- Sarah awakens to the absence of the usual tourist commotion and is instead alarmed by new military-like activity surrounding the mansion. Observing from her window, she sees battalions of Elvi parading on the lawns, with tanks rolling up the driveway.
- Alarmed but determined, Sarah rushes outside to confront the scene, unprepared for Specialist 5 Tulsa McLean’s calm explanation. He reassures her that the "headquarters," as he now describes the mansion, is secured for her protection, including her baby, with the surplus military presence.
