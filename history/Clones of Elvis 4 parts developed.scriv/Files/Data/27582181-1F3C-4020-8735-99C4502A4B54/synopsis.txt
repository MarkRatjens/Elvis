### Synopsis

**Location:** A Cloudborne street under the oppressive Layer, transitioning to Tony’s van in a gritty dead-end alley.

Tony, dejected and disheartened after his emotionally turbulent encounter with Sarah, trudges back to his cleaning van, immersed in self-pity and lamenting his life beneath the Layer. His wayward cart collides with a dead body sprawled on the pavement, an incident that briefly disrupts his brooding. While socially invisible pedestrians bypass the scene, Tony’s street-smart survival instincts kick in, prompting him to rifle through the corpse’s belongings for anything of value. His pilfering is interrupted by coroner workers, whom he hastily evades, pocketing a peculiar, coin-like object that momentarily flickers in color. After securing his findings, Tony retreats to his van, brushing off the incident as he attempts to move forward with his dreary existence.
