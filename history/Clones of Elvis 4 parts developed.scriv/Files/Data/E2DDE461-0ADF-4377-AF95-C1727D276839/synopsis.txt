**Locations**: Memphis Mafia Bar (in the Ghetto).

**Tone and Voice Sentence**: "For Johnny, the disc was superior, in every way, to a light saber."

**Plot Summary**:
- Johnny arrives and immediately expresses distaste for the Memphis Mafia Bar’s setting, sparring verbally with Sarah while exuding Airborne elitism. His arrogant demeanor contrasts sharply with Sarah’s tense pragmatism.
- Sarah reveals the "disc" to Johnny, who, after sarcastic dismissals, becomes captivated when he witnesses its rainbow shimmer. Despite Sarah's attempts to downplay its significance, Johnny recognizes the artifact as a possibly mythical "Gyges Disc."
- Delving into legend, Johnny warns Sarah about the disc’s potential powers, such as temporal dislocation and unexplained phenomena. His alarm intensifies when Sarah admits bending and handling the disc, recounting strange events (like the van crash) afterward.
- Johnny, initially panicked by PAPER’s involvement, shifts gears upon realizing the disc's significance. Seeing it as a unique discovery, he envisions leveraging its power to elevate himself, positioning Sarah as his reluctant sidekick in his newfound pursuit of fame and glory.
