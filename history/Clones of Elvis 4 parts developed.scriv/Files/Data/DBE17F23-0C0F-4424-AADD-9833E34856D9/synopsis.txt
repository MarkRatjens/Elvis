**Locations**: Government cabinet meeting in a high-security boardroom.

**Tone and Voice Sentence**: "Planes. Apparently, the Elvi know how to sky-dive."

**Synopsis**:
In a continuation of the Sunday cabinet meeting, Piotr Dunhilton shifts focus to addressing the growing threat of the Elvi, exacerbated by their audacious parachute infiltration of St. Hedges Cathedral. Cabinet members propose self-serving exploitation plans for the Elvi, including forced conscription, commercialization with tourist attractions, and property development schemes over the Ghetto. Dunhilton, despite the synergies of these ideas, remains determined to prevent others from profiting at his expense and maintains the focus on broader control measures.

Gerry Hardman updates the assembly on the Elvi's sky-diving capabilities and their resourceful acquisition of planes from junkyards, further humiliating the Airborne government's defense forces, whose hover-copters failed to intercept the fixed-wing aircraft. Dunhilton's frustration peaks as he contemplates the Elvi's unprecedented coordination and the embarrassing failures of Airborne security. While Gerry hints at intelligence initiatives (including the virus project) targeting the "Holy Momma," the session underscores the deepening unease over the Elvi's evolving sophistication and challenges to Airborne authority. Dunhilton contemplates consolidating more portfolios under Gerry to reinstate order but hesitates, wary of Gerry's increasingly unchecked power.
