**Locations**: Tony's penthouse above Jamison Tower; bathroom and change room; reception area with the Elvi staff.

**Tone and Voice Sentence**: "You are indeed the Great Teacher. And it seems we have been in stasis for much longer than we might have ever guessed."

**Synopsis**:
Newly settled in Jamison Tower, Tony explores his luxurious surroundings, refreshing himself in an opulent bathroom and dressing room before meeting his newly assembled Elvi staff. Presented by Fisher, the staff includes personal assistants, entertainment heads, and even a sommelier, all Elvi and Elvine former "Grayling Corporation units." Their interaction initially puzzles Tony, as they do not refer to him as "Great Teacher" until he politely insists, signaling his uneasy transition into a position of power within this enigmatic new world.

As introductions unfold, Tony discovers a shocking truth: the Elvi staff have been living in a figurative and literal stasis since the death of their previous benefactor, Jamison Hardy. They are unaware of their emancipation generations earlier and still regard themselves as the property of their superiors. Horrified, Tony decides to take action to awaken and free the remaining staff members, allegedly kept in stasis in the building’s basement. Declaring his intent to accompany Fisher, Tony redials the High Elvis for guidance, setting the stage for his next step in navigating this morally fraught inheritance.
