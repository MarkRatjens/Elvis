**Synopsis**:
After Sarah's departure, Tony spirals into a deep depression, spending days in the ruins of the mansion, refusing sustenance and solitude save for the rare cheeseburger. He retreats into isolation, obsessing over the absence of Sarah and the unborn “Little Clinton,” whom he nostalgically credits as his muse. On the seventh day, the High Elvis intervenes, relocating Tony to a new residence—a lavish recording studio and apartment on the edge of the Ghetto.

Despite the luxurious amenities and accommodations provided for the "Great Teacher’s" creative resurgence, Tony remains stagnant, haunted by his loss and unable to produce lyrics. The Elvi Parliament, invested in ensuring Tony’s influence continues to pacify their kind and inspire peace, has lofty hopes for his output. However, their faith in him is unshaken, even as Tony grapples privately with crippling creative block and the void left by Sarah and her child.
