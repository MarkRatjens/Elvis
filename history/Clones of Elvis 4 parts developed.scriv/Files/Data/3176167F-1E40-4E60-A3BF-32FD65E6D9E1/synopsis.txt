**Locations**: Government cabinet meeting in a high-security boardroom.

**Tone and Voice Sentence**: "Now get out. Both of you get out now, or you’ll both be hugging pillows."

**Synopsis**:
Piotr Dunhilton convenes an emergency Sunday cabinet meeting to address recent crises, including the Scarlet Shower Surgeon case and the disruptive resurgence of the Elvi. Gerry Hardman confidently reports the case as resolved, announcing that Haloren Smythe, the implicated killer, was slain during a scuffle and confirming the weapon's connection to previous crimes. Tensions rise as the ministers debate the political implications of the Elvi's growing unity and influence, exacerbated by their recent disruption of Airborne events and their alarming allegiance to the "Holy Momma," Sarah Jacobson.

Dunhilton shifts the meeting tone dramatically by introducing Item Two: an unexpected cabinet reshuffle. The Director of Freedom, Heston, is forced to resign, and the Commissioner of Elvi Rights is summarily dismissed, both ousted under the thinly veiled threat of public and lethal disgrace. Gerry Hardman is appointed acting Director of Freedom and permanent Director of Elvi Rights, consolidating a newfound political clout that draws the wary attention of the remaining cabinet members. While toasts and congratulations mask deep-seated insecurities, Dunhilton and Defence remain the only figures unaffected and quietly watch as Gerry’s ascendancy reshapes the balance of power within Ulyssia’s government.
