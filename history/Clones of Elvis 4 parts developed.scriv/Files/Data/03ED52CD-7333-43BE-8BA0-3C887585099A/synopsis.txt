**Locations**: Eloise Shakti’s downtown office; various Liechtenstein assets including a cheese factory in Balzers.

**Tone and Voice Sentence**: "Activate your cheese-based cover operation."

**Synopsis**:
Following Alexander’s departure, Eloise sets her plans into motion, instructing a Swiss contact, Heinrich, to fabricate a convincing corporate operation for VorpalTech AG in Liechtenstein to occupy Alexander. Heinrich scrambles to prepare within a narrow timeframe, leveraging unconventional assets, including a repurposed cheese factory, condemned restaurants, and a failing VR startup. Eloise emphasizes the need for convincing bureaucratic and technological facades to satisfy Alexander’s shallow understanding of physics while preventing real investigation into the company’s nonexistence.

To ensure sufficient time for setup, Eloise orchestrates delays in Alexander’s flight through contrived "mechanical issues" and fanciful disruptions like ostrich migrations. Heinrich, though incredulous, mobilizes resources to brand the cheese factory as a cutting-edge research facility involving quantum technology and "data carrier cheese." Eloise, confident in her contingencies and Alexander’s tendency to remain distracted by surface-level grandeur, concludes arrangements with an air of bemused exasperation, ensuring the charade will sustain until her broader plans necessitate Alexander’s recall.
