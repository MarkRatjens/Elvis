**Locations**: Eloise Shakti's limousine; a luxurious residential compound named Sublime Point; the futuristic palace; adjacent well-appointed townhouses amidst native bushland.

**Tone and Voice Sentence**: "An advanced civilisation, Sarah mused, who conquered the universe by shaming other worlds into submission for their inadequate feats of architecture."

**Synopsis**:
Eloise escorts Sarah, Kathy, and Hayley via limousine to her Sublime Point compound, an isolated, high-security estate amidst restored native bushland. Upon arrival, the passengers marvel at the futuristic, structurally ambitious glass-and-metal palace, a breathtaking statement of Eloise's wealth and influence. While Eloise proceeds to the palace, Sarah and her companions are directed towards luxurious townhouses.
