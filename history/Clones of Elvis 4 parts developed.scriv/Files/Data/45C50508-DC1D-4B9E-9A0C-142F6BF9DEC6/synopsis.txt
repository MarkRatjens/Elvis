**Synopsis**

**Locations**: A run-down inn in The Pale.

**Tone and Voice Sentence**: "Tony hoped there would be sex and Sarah knew there would be none"

**Plot Summary**:
- Due to crowded accommodations from the Vegas Show, Lonnie secures Sarah and Tony a single room in a dingy inn. Though Tony harbors hope for intimacy, an exhausted Sarah is confident no such interaction will occur, and they both quickly fall asleep.
- Under the effect of Kleoboulos’ potion, Sarah enters a vivid, sensual dream, only to awaken in horror as the Gyges Disc, hidden in her bra, comes alive as a parasitic creature feeding on her chest.
- Tony springs into action, prying the disc from Sarah amidst panic. The disc snarls physics with its strange properties, shaking the room upon being flung away. Both shaken and fearful, they dread the artifact’s power but reject abandoning it, knowing its danger will ensure Agent Jones’ unrelenting pursuit.
- After an uneasy return to sleep, Sarah awakens at dawn, defeated and overwhelmed. She wrestles with both the immediate terror of the Gyges Disc and PAPER’s chase, and deeper fears spurred by a vivid dream of Kleoboulos. In this dark vision, the sage, garbed in Elvi attire, disturbingly leaves Sarah with the haunting impression of something being implanted within her.
