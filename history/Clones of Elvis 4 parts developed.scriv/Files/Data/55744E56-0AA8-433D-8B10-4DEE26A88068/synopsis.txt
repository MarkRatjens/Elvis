The Johnny secretly calls Tony to enlist him to get the Gyges discs. He wants Tony to use his influence with the Elvi to go on a mission (to execute Eloise’s idea of stealing from empty mansions in the mountains).

Tony, reluctant at first, sees an opportunity to enlist The Johnny to help see Sarah.

The High Elvis will only agree to the plan if Tony can write new tunes and, for that, he agrees Tony needs to be in the presence of the One after the One (Sarah’s unborn baby).

Johnny agrees if Tony comes to Sublime Point with the required discs, he will smuggle him in to Sublime Point.
