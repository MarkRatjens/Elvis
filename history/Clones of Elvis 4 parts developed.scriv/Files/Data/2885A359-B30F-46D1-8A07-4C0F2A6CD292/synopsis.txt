**Locations**: Memphis Mafia Bar; New Graceland worship complex; the streets of the Ghetto; secure government facility.

**Tone and Voice Sentence**: "The swivel of the hip leads straight to the fires of HELL!"

**Synopsis**:
Pastor Rex Humble, a once-mediocre Elvis impersonator, rises to power as the spiritual leader of the Holy Rollers, shepherding the gospel-infected Elvi through a fervent religious transformation. His fiery sermons blend evangelical zeal with mutated cultural interpretations of Elvis, creating a doctrine both compelling and absurd. Iconic rituals such as the "Great Sideburn Sacrifice" and sermons against "Seven Deadly Shimmies" galvanize his followers, while his controversial stances on issues like modesty for Elvines and condemnation of the Evolutionaries alienate the unconverted.

Humble's popularity explodes with the launch of his televised ministry, "Heavenly Hits with Pastor Humble," spreading his fiery gospel beyond the Ghetto and fueling the construction of "New Graceland," a grand worship complex. However, his rhetoric grows increasingly dangerous as he weaponizes faith against the Evolutionaries, framing them as apostates and sanctioning violent "rescue missions," escalating the conflict within the Elvi community.

While Pastor Humble thrives in his newfound influence, the High Elvis and the Sages look on in horror, struggling unsuccessfully to counter the growing religious fervor. Meanwhile, Agent Jones, listening to bootleg sermons in his government office, begins to show subtle signs of being affected by the infectious hymns—a chilling reminder of the schism's reach beyond the Ghetto. As Humble’s influence grows unchecked, the once-unified Elvi kingdom succumbs further to division and fanaticism.
