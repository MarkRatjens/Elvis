**Locations**: The kitchen in Eloise Shakti’s Sublime Point compound; the stasis fridge.

**Tone and Voice Sentence**: "WARNING: TAMPERING WITH STASIS TECHNOLOGY INVALIDATES THE WARRANTY OF YOUR LIFE."

**Synopsis**:
Struggling to sleep in the disconcerting luxury of her room, Hayley descends to the kitchen and reflects on Kathy’s earlier musings about stasis technology. Her curiosity piqued, she begins investigating stasis fridge maintenance records, uncovering an unusual connection to Travis Teh’s murder—an entry tied to a resident named Chuglingham. Overcome with suspicion, Hayley impulsively examines the stasis fridge’s interior, ignoring multiple warnings both literal and figurative. Upon breaching a sealed component, she inadvertently triggers an ear-shattering alarm and flashing strobes. Within moments, a squad of heavily armed paramilitaries branded with Shakti Enterprises insignias storms in, weapons drawn, leaving Hayley caught red-handed.
