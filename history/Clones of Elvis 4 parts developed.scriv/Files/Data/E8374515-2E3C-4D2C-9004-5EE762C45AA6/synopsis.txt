**Locations**: The Elvi mansion; the house parlour; the mansion grounds.

**Tone and Voice Sentence**: “I’d rather be loved by a pustulant braised squid than you.”

**Synopsis**:
Sarah receives another delivery of unwanted gifts from Alexander Shakti, now escalating to include vibrators and muffins. While venting her frustrations alongside Kathy, who casually plays with the items, Sarah wrestles with the unsettling presence of Captain Denver, an Elvi bodyguard, and rumours of increasing Elvi political organization. Overwhelmed, Sarah contacts Alexander, whose smug and relentless romantic overtures drive her to anger. The conversation devolves into insults, culminating with Sarah furiously hurling her tablet into the fireplace as Alexander’s infuriating persistence continues unabated.
