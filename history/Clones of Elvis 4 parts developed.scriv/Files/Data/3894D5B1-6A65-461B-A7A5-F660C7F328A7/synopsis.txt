**Locations**: Various Ghetto venues; Tulsa McLeans' militarized patrols; ruined recording studios; newly divided Elvi communities.

**Tone and Voice Sentence**: "The harmonies of Gospel are just too powerful for an Elvis to resist."

**Synopsis**:
Agent Jones’s deployment of gospel records across the Ghetto quickly spirals into chaos, igniting a schism between the newly converted “Holy Rollers” and the secular, science-favoring “Evolutionaries.” While the Holy Rollers embrace gospel fervor, staging revivals and forcibly "baptizing" dissenters in fountains, the Evolutionaries retaliate with rock anthems and Richard Dawkins audiobooks, escalating the divide into an ideological war. 

The Tulsa McLeans split their allegiance, with converted factions repurposing tanks into mobile crucifixes blaring deafening gospel music, while Evolutionists counter with their own modified vehicles. Violent clashes, midnight raids on Elvine dormitories, and coercive prayer sessions underscore the zealotry of the Holy Rollers, while the Evolutionaries struggle to defend their secular vision of Elvi unity. Attempts by the High Elvis and the Sages to mediate only reveal the depth of the Elvi susceptibility to religious fervor, rooted in their cloned heritage.

Meanwhile, Ulyssian executives, including CEO Dunhilton and Gerry Hardman, observe the chaotic fallout from the safety of sanitized conference rooms. Dunhilton naively celebrates the "success" of the operation, dismissing warnings about the unintended sociopolitical consequences.

In the Ghetto, amidst the rubble of recording studios and tainted jukeboxes, remnants of the Evolutionary faction gather in secret, vowing to fight back. Whispered reverence for the “Great Teacher” and the "Holy Momma" spread, highlighting their roles as potential figures of salvation. The fractured Elvi community becomes a battleground of extremes, where the fight for identity intertwines with manipulation and a dangerous cultural vacuum left by the sabotage. Faith manifests in myriad forms, leaving both sides entrenched and a deeper mystery surrounding the Gyges influence unresolved.
