**Locations**: Tony’s old apartment building; surrounding streets.

**Tone and Voice Sentence**: “The furniture had been rearranged by someone with strong opinions about feng shui and a personal grudge.’"

**Synopsis**:
Hung-over and emotionally frayed, Tony attempts to return to his old apartment after the reception. Guided by muscle memory and stubborn determination, he struggles through his hangover-induced haze, resorting to risky climbing antics on the building’s exterior when his door lock fails to recognise him. After an unfortunate fall resulting in injuries, Tony ultimately gains access to what he discovers is no longer his home—his apartment has been revamped and occupied by new tenants following his eviction.

The intrusion prompts an angry confrontation with the new resident, forcing Tony to flee. Along the way, a young bystander returns a tablet thumb drive that supposedly fell from Tony’s pocket. Instinctively pocketing the mysterious item, Tony imagines an unseen link between it and his fragmented thoughts of Sarah and past events. Despite the ordeal, his brief return home punctuates his ongoing struggle with dislocation, identity, and unresolved ties to his former life.
