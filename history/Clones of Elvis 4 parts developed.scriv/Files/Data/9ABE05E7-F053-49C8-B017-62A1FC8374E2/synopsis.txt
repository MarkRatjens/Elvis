**Locations**: Memphis Mafia Bar (in the Ghetto).

**Tone and Voice Sentence**: "We’d rather like to know, if it isn’t too much trouble, that is. Sarah. Could we get you pregnant?"

**Plot Summary**:
- Sarah is approached by Kleoboulos Windus and his two attendants, eccentric self-proclaimed Sages clad in ridiculous wizard-themed onesies adorned with absurd celestial designs. They introduce themselves as representatives of the "Seven Sages Corp.," though their group has dwindled to three due to "artistic differences."
- While Sarah sceptically fields their humorous, meandering explanations about prophecy fulfillment and their peculiar work, Johnny is sidelined, growing increasingly frustrated as his self-perceived importance is ignored.
- The Sages reveal an overwhelming interest in the Gyges Disc, their awestruck behavior confirming its immense significance beyond mythology. They ultimately declare they need Sarah—branded as "perfect" and "invincible"—for a "sacred mission."
- The encounter culminates in hilarity and shock as Kleoboulos formally and absurdly asks if Sarah would consider becoming pregnant as part of their mysterious prophetic designs. Sarah is left stunned and exposed, suspecting the Sages know more about her than they let on.
