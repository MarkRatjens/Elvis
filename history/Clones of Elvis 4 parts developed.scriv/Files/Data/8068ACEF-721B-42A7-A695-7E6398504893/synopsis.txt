**Synopsis**

**Locations**: Stickett Inn bar, side alley, Avi's studio apartment.

**Tone and Voice Sentence**: "Some people didn’t know how to die according to his needs."

**Plot Summary**:
- Halo, anger dissipating, shifts his focus at the Stickett Inn to Avi, a detached and alluring stranger. Observing her demeanor and arousal, he approaches, and their mutual attraction leads to a passionate encounter in a side alley before heading to her nearby studio.
- Their intimacy devolves into a deadly confrontation when Avi, a covert operative, attempts to use her agency-issued particle pistol against Halo. Halo overpowers her, killing her with his gruesome weapon, the Point of Order. However, her death does not satisfy his fetishistic expectations, leaving him frustrated.
- Discovering Avi's likely agency mission and reckoning with the potential risks of her investigation and pursuit, Halo panics. He resolves to clean the scene professionally and seek a "do-over" to assuage his fury.
