**Locations**: Secure laboratory beneath Tramp Tower; Gerry Hardman’s corner office; Bent Street protest site.

**Tone and Voice Sentence**: "The unified movement of just hours ago had been effectively divided along precisely the lines it had sought to erase."

**Synopsis**:
Facing a swelling protest outside Tramp Tower, Gerry Hardman authorizes the deployment of the TMinus-Virus, a genetically engineered pathogen designed to incapacitate the Elvi. The virus is released through modified ventilation units, targeting Elvi genetic markers while causing only mild symptoms in others, ensuring plausible deniability under the cover of a fabricated "bacterial outbreak" narrative. 

As media allies broadcast misinformation and Health Department vehicles cordon off the area, the virus begins ravaging the Evolution Elvi within the mixed crowd. Panic spreads as protesters realize the sickness is selective. Initial solidarity among Airborne, Cloudborne, and Elvi crumbles as Health Department enforcers separate the ill Elvi from their unaffected allies, dismantling the movement’s unity.

From his office, Gerry observes the scene with calculated detachment, updating reports and receiving praise from CEO Dunhilton, who lauds the operation’s precision and its role in reinforcing Ulyssia's social hierarchy. Despite Dunhilton’s approval and the operation's tactical success, Gerry remains stoic. He logs the virus deployment and watches as the once-vibrant protest winds down into chaos, with Evolution Elvi performers and leaders loaded into quarantine vehicles, their discarded instruments symbolizing the abrupt silencing of their movement. Efforts to “sanitize” the area and restore public order signal the stark efficiency of social control, as the Elvi protesters face a fate designed to eradicate their influence entirely.
