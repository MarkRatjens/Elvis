**Locations**: The mansion; the mansion’s parlour.

**Tone and Voice Sentence**: "I just gave a piece of infected biotech to the most feared weapons manufacturer in the Airborne Meritocracy of Ulyssia."

**Plot Summary**:
- Following his explosive rescue by the Elvi, Tony is brought to the mansion, where he is escorted to meet Sarah. Their reunion is dramatic and heated, as Sarah immediately punches Tony, accusing him of causing her pregnancy and repeatedly deceiving her.
- Tony vehemently denies Sarah’s accusations, claiming he would never harm her, while expressing confusion over his own mysterious regenerative abilities. Sarah confronts him about the horrors she witnessed during his televised tortures, highlighting his seemingly supernatural recovery from brutal injuries.
- Amid their argument, the conversation spirals toward the enigmatic Gyges Disc. Tony and Sarah connect its strange properties—earthquakes, physical transformations, and other incidents—to their current predicaments, particularly Tony’s resilience and Sarah’s pregnancy. Both suspect the influence of the disc and vaguely recall the role of the cryptic Sages in their chaotic journey.
- Sarah admits to recently handing over the disc to "Johnny," aligning him with Eloise Shakti’s network. Tony processes the gravity of the situation as Sarah internally laments her action, realizing she might have delivered dangerous biotech to the Airborne Meritocracy’s foremost weapons dealer.
