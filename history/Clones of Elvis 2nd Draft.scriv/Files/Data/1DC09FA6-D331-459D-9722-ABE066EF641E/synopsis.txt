**Locations**: Graceland amphitheater and surrounding streets in The Pale.

**Tone and Voice Sentence**: "By the time the second chorus opened, she was singing with the best of them."

**Plot Summary**:
- Escorted by a squad of Elvi soldiers, Sarah and Tony arrive at Graceland, a sprawling, mud-trodden field surrounded by vibrant bars, buskers, and crowds of Elvi and Cloudborne. The electric atmosphere builds as the crowd prepares for the Vegas Show—a celebration rooted in the emancipation and cultural vitality of the Elvi clones.
- Lonnie explains the tension among the Elvi on the topic of lyrics, likening it to their unique blend of divine inspiration and chaos, which often leads to conflict in their musical gatherings.
- Sarah and Tony are seated as the stage lights announce the start of the show. The event begins solemnly with the crowd reciting the "Oath of the Warranty," a humorous creed of rules and reverence for the legacy of music and behavior "after midnight."
- The Vegas Show commences, and Sarah is swept into a powerful sensory experience—glittering performances by jewel-encrusted, karate-practicing Elvi in intricate orchestration and dazzling theatrics. Engulfed in the music, she loses herself in its ecstatic embrace.
- A moment of mood shift occurs as the lights dim, and Sarah is captivated by a single raw, emotional solo, showcasing unmatched vocal skill and vulnerability. This song moves Sarah to tears and instills in her a profound sense of love, grace, and human connection, unlike anything she has ever experienced.
