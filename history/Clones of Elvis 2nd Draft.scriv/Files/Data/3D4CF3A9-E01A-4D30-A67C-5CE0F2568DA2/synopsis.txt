**Locations**: Laboratory wing at Sublime Point; experimental research area.

**Tone and Voice Sentence**: "Accelerating inevitability."

**Synopsis**:
The Johnny leads a tour of the Sublime Point research facilities, presenting recent advancements in decoding the Gyges Disc's enigmatic properties. Accompanying him are the Sages (Kleo, Solo, and Chilo) and a reluctant Chip Stockwell. Dr. Quark, visibly apprehensive, demonstrates pulsing concentric patterns attributed to corresponding universes with variable gravitational constants, spurring intense interest from Chilo, who provocatively suggests incorporating temporal adjustments into the research.

Chip, alarmed by the proposal, warns of catastrophic risks tied to temporal calibration, referencing confidential Fridge Force incidents. Though his objections are dismissed by The Johnny, who orders the modifications, Dr. Quark begins preparations, intrigued but cautious. Behind the scenes, tension rises as Solo confronts Chilo, accusing him of deviating from their shared plan, but Chilo cryptically insists he's merely hastening inevitable outcomes.

Realizing the grave implications of the changes, Chip retreats to his office, deciding to warn Sarah Jacobson of the escalating dangers. Meanwhile, the lab's trajectory reflects a reckless convergence of ambition, ignorance, and underlying manipulations by the Sages. Chilo's enigmatic intentions loom ominously over the unfolding experiment.
