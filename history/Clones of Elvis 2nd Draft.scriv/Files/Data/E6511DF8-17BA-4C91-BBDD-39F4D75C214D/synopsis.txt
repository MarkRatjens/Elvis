**Tony Activates the Army**

From his penthouse atop Jamison Tower, Tony watches the breaking news coverage with growing alarm. Military vehicles surround Sublime Point, helicopters circle like mechanical vultures, and government spokesmen deliver carefully worded statements about "neutralizing terrorist threats." Sarah is in there. Little Clinton is in there. The people who've become his unlikely family are trapped.

His secure phone buzzes—the High Elvis, voice tight with controlled fury.

"Great Teacher, you're seeing this?"

"I'm seeing it," Tony confirms, never taking his eyes off the screen. "What's our response?"

"We're mobilizing everything. Every Elvis, every Elvine, every tank we can roll. But it's not enough—they've got professional military units, quantum suppression fields, orbital support." The High Elvis pauses. "We need more than what we have."

Tony's gaze shifts to the basement access panel. Dozens of Grayling Corporation storage containers still wait down there, the legacy of Jamison Hardy's vast collection. An army in boxes, waiting for activation.

"I might have something," Tony says. "Give me thirty minutes."

**In the Basement**

Tony descends to find Fisher and the Jamison Tower staff already at work. Word has spread through the building's service network—the Holy Momma is in danger, and every resource must be deployed. Cartons are being opened with systematic efficiency, activation codes entered, and newly conscious Elvi are being oriented and sent upstairs for briefing.

"We've cleared most of the Entertainment Units," Fisher reports, consulting his tablet. "Approximately two hundred activated so far. But sir..." He gestures toward the far corner, where seven larger cartons remain untouched.

Tony approaches, reading the labels with growing amazement. The marketing graphics are unmistakable: figures in black suits, sunglasses, stern expressions. "Grayling Corporation: Security & Protection Unit: Ajent Jones Model." Each carton bears colorful starburst identifiers—"With Blue Tie!" "With Black Tie!" "Now With Pink Tie!"

"They made clones of Agent Jones?" Tony asks incredulously.

"Apparently for executive protection services," Fisher explains. "The records indicate these were part of a specialized security line that never went to market. Some kind of licensing dispute with the original subject."

Tony stares at the cartons, his mind racing. Agent Jones—the PAPER operative who'd hunted them, tortured him, terrorized Sarah. The man whose gospel-induced breakdown had marked a turning point in the religious war. Now, an entire squad of his clones waited in storage.

"Activate them," Tony decides.

**The Awakening**

The seven cartons hiss open simultaneously. Seven identical figures sit up in perfect synchronization—tall, lean, cold-eyed men in impeccable black suits. Blue tie, black tie, pink tie, and four others in various corporate color schemes. They move with the same predatory grace, the same calculating stare.

But as they focus on Tony, something unexpected happens. Instead of the original Jones's barely contained rage, these clones display professional deference. Their programming is different—protection, not aggression. Service, not domination.

"Sir," they say in unison, their voices identical yet somehow warmer than the original. "Awaiting instructions."

Tony grins, the absurdity of the situation not lost on him. "Well, boys, looks like we're going to war. How do you feel about taking on the government that created your template?"

The lead clone—Blue Tie—tilts his head thoughtfully. "Our primary directive is protection of designated assets. If the government threatens those assets..." He pauses, his expression shifting into something that might almost be a smile. "Then the government becomes the problem we solve."

As the cleanup continues and the newly activated clones head upstairs for orientation, Tony pats one of the empty cartons affectionately. The whole situation is so surreal, so perfectly in keeping with the cosmic joke his life has become.

"Maybe after this is all over," he muses to himself, "I can form a band with you guys. How about we call it ENCHANTED and the Ajent Jones Clones."

In the distance, the sound of Elvi war songs begins to echo through the tower as Tony's unlikely army prepares to march on Sublime Point. The government wanted a fight. Now they're about to get one they never saw coming.
