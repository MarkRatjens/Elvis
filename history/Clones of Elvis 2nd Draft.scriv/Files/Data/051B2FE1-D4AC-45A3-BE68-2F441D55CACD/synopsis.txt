**Locations**: Piotr Dunhilton's dining room; virtual conference call linking Dunhilton, Gerry Hardman, and the Director of Freedom.

**Tone and Voice Sentence**: "How the tax do you lose a body?"

**Synopsis**:
Piotr Dunhilton convenes a tense briefing with Gerry Hardman and the Director of Freedom to address the fallout from the previous evening. The discussion centers on the Scarlett Shower Surgeon, revealed to be Haloren Smythe, a rogue PAPER agent implicated in the Stickett Inn bombing and deeper black ops missions involving the neutralization of heirs to hidden billionaire assets. Smythe’s dual role as investigator and potential assassin adds complexity, particularly when linked to another rogue PAPER agent, Avi Grubler, who orchestrated the Stickett Inn bombing. Dunhilton is frustrated to find two PAPER agents working cross-purposes within the same covert operation.

The situation is compounded when Gerry reveals that Smythe's body vanished en route to the morgue, alongside the ambulance drivers, whose identities appear to be fraudulent and connected to PAPER. As Dunhilton processes the overlapping failures—rogue agents, missing bodies, and the infiltration of societal ceremonies by Elvi tensions—his exasperation grows. The revelation that Smythe may still be alive and the sheer dysfunction within PAPER’s operations leave the meeting unresolved, amplifying Dunhilton’s concerns about stability and control across Ulyssia.
