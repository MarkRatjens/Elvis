### Synopsis

**Location:** A hyper-stylized dystopian TV studio transitioning to the streets, preparing for chaos in the Pale.

Alexander Shakti, an incompetent police lieutenant and media celebrity, hosts another cynical episode of his reality show, *Candid Criminal*. Despite its pomp and calculated drama—complete with fabricated evidence and rigged execution scenarios—Alexander’s disdain for both his role and his audience seeps through. The centerpiece of the show, a grotesque execution contraption dubbed "The Wheel of Death," functions as both macabre spectacle and social commentary. However, the show is abruptly curtailed as riot cops storm the studio to whisk Alexander away to a fresh crisis: a bomb attack in the chaotic Pale district. As the situation unravels en route, the dismissive Alexander realizes his presence at the blast site is less about investigation and more a PR maneuver to rubber-stamp the police's actions.
