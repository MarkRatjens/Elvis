In the pre-dawn darkness, a massive government operation unfolds around Sublime Point. Gerry Hardman's TRAMP forces approach from the north through established access roads, while Dunhilton's off-the-books mercenary units position themselves along the cliff faces and helicopter landing zones. A third contingent—regular Ulyssian Defence Force units ostensibly conducting "training exercises"—forms an outer perimeter, ensuring no civilian interference and providing plausible deniability.
The siege represents the culmination of months of careful planning following the Elvi's theft of Gyges Discs from Blue Mountains mansions. Gerry has leveraged his position as head of TRAMPs and PAPER agent to frame the operation as necessary action against "dangerous experimental weapons," while Dunhilton has called in favors from private military contractors. Unlike previous improvised assaults, this is a coordinated military action with advanced electronic warfare capabilities, satellite support, and specialized equipment designed to counter Eloise's reality-manipulation abilities.
Inside the Sublime Point facility, Eloise stands at the panoramic windows of her executive suite, watching thermal imaging displays that reveal the encircling forces. Her usual composure shows hairline cracks as she realizes the scope of what's arrayed against her. The energy dampening fields, quantum interference generators, and reality stabilization matrices being deployed represent technology specifically developed to counter her abilities—technology that suggests government research into weapons has advanced far beyond her expectations.
