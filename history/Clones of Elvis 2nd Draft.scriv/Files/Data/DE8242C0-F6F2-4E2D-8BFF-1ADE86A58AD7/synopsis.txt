**Locations**: Tony’s recording studio; the Elvi lounge; surrounding fan-filled areas.

**Tone and Voice Sentence**: "Evolve me tender, Evolve me true, the Dawkins dream fulfilled."

**Synopsis**:
Tony and the Elvi delve into discussions about Richard Dawkins, whom the Elvi reverently dub “The One.” As the group fixates on the philosopher’s principles, they reinterpret his anti-religious stance as a quasi-religious prophecy warning against powerful institutions like the Airborne. Discussion swiftly escalates to collective fervor, with the Elvi drawing imaginative and ideological connections between Dawkins’ philosophy, evolutionary biology, and their own engineered existence. 

Their awe spills into a musical interlude as they spontaneously adapt Tony’s famous earlier lyrics into a new rendition, “Evolve Me Tender,” blending their worshipful homage with existential themes. The moment culminates in an accidental injury to Tony during their impassioned celebration, leaving him incapacitated but cemented further as their spiritual figure. Through this blend of rapturous song and philosophical exploration, the Elvi and Tony edge closer to a confluence of identity, purpose, and their evolving role within the prophecy.
