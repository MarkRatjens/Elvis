**Locations**: Sarah's townhouse bedroom in Eloise Shakti's Sublime Point compound.

**Tone and Voice Sentence**: "The burden of your scorn is a weight I am willing to bear."

**Synopsis**:
Sarah, restless and emotionally drained, is visited by Kleoboulos who intrudes stealthily into her room. Offering apologies for his role in her forced pregnancy, Kleo assures Sarah that Tony played no part in it and remains innocent. He frames her circumstances as central to a prophecy predicting an era of enlightenment through her child, where science and reason prevail. Despite her anger, Kleo subtly convinces Sarah to consider Eloise’s offer and entertains the idea of marrying Alexander as a symbolic arrangement consistent with the prophecy. Sarah pushes back, demanding answers about the significance of her child and the fate of the Elvi. Kleo insists her child will herald progress and suggests that she herself will be integral in ensuring the Elvi's continued perseverance, claiming it aligns more with her character than the prophecy. Their interaction shifts Sarah's turmoil toward a tentative acceptance of her importance in this grand narrative, though she ends the encounter unresolved and overcome with emotion. When she regains her composure, Kleo is gone without a trace.
