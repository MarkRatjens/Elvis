**Locations**: Hayley's residence; Police Department system; ENCHANTED Cleaning data records.

**Tone and Voice Sentence**: "Hayley loved coincidences, because they mostly weren’t."

**Synopsis**:
Hayley focuses on ENCHANTED Cleaning and uncovers records connecting the business to phoney invoices and suspicious payments, including contracts with the Department of Traffic Movement and Property Security for “Deep Clean: Crime Scene” services. Delving further, she discovers overlapping connections between Anthony Chantara, Alexander Shakti, and Sarah Jacobson on the day of the Stickett Inn bombing, as well as concerning details surrounding the suicide of an employee, Travis Teh. Sarah’s documented injuries from the bombing and her inexplicably rapid recovery raise more questions, especially as all three figures converge repeatedly in Hayley’s mounting investigation.
