**Locations**: Outside the mansion in the Ghetto; the guarded gates.

**Tone and Voice Sentence**: "Tony? He’s a pussycat."

**Synopsis**:
Hayley confronts Dr. Logan, who confirms she is attending a patient inside the mansion. Through their exchange, Hayley learns that Tony Chantara has become revered by the Elvi as "The Great Teacher," having written lyrics that replace violence with belief in self-worth. Dr. Logan reveals that Sarah Jacobson is her patient but offers no insights into her condition, though the situation intensifies as Elvi guards sing Jacobson’s praises. Desperate for direct contact with Tony or Sarah to corroborate her findings and stop the operation, Hayley pleads to no avail, as Dr. Logan remains noncommittal. The tension peaks when a PAPER gunship descends ominously from above the Layer.
