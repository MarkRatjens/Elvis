**Locations**: Sarah's high-rise apartment; the luxurious foyer.

**Synopsis**:
Hayley reluctantly risks visiting Sarah's exclusive apartment, wary of alerting Koalemos to her movements. Navigating the architectural brutality forced upon the Cloudborne community below, Hayley gains access through a concierge distracted by a tablet game, learning there has been no police activity at the location. Inside, Sarah’s apartment is unnaturally pristine and devoid of habitation signs, as though it has been staged for display. However, a single peculiar item—an inexpensive six-pack of pink fairy-themed boxer shorts—contrasts sharply with the otherwise flawless presentation, leaving Hayley to ponder its meaning.
