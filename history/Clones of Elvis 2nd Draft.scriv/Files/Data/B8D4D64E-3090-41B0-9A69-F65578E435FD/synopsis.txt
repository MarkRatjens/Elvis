### Synopsis

**Location:** A bustling city street in the midst of morning rush hour.

Rick and Dick, two mischievous operatives who moonlight as "ambulance punks," combine their shady professions (covert surveillance, booby traps, and general malfeasance) with their knack for macabre humour. On a routine gig involving a body reported in the street, they engage in procrastination and gallows humour. Sitting in their souped-up ambulance, they enjoy meat pies and coffee while observing the indifference of the crowd toward the corpse in the street. When an unfortunate cleaner stumbles over the body, their day is made, and their hearty laughter causes a mess in their vehicle.

**Voice and Tone Highlight:**
*Rick and Dick were guys who broke into houses and placed covert listening devices and cameras—and occasionally, bombs and other hilarious booby traps.*
