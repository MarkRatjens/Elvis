**Synopsis**

**Locations**: Airborne hospital, Tony’s Cloudborne tenement, streets of deteriorating Cloudborne neighborhoods.

**Tone and Voice Sentence**: "These are a few of my ghastliest things."

**Plot Summary**:
- Tony and Sarah are separated after the explosion, sent to distinct hospitals reflecting their class divides: Sarah to a luxurious Airborne facility where she recovers quickly, Tony to a decrepit Cloudborne clinic where treatment is negligent.
- Sarah, fatigued by her surroundings and determined to repay Tony's rescue, checks herself out against medical advice. She locates Tony, disheveled and in pain, and insists on bringing him home.
- In an amusing and chaotic journey through run-down Cloudborne streets, Sarah steals a wheelchair, enlists reluctant aides, and contracts a ramshackle ground cab, immersing herself in Tony’s dilapidated world.
- From Tony's apartment, Sarah nurses him, enduring the shabby conditions while introducing Airborne comforts, like proper clothing and advanced pampering. Through her dedicated care, Tony begins recovering physically and emotionally.
