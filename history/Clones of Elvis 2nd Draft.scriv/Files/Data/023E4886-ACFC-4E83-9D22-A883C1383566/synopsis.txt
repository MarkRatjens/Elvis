### Synopsis

**Location:** *The Blue Shade*, an Elvis-themed karaoke bar in the Ghetto quarter of the Pale—a decrepit, edgy district where only the reckless dare to venture.

Sarah, an Airborne socialite who thrives on discomfort and defying expectations, invites Johnny—a reluctant, awkward acquaintance—to join her for an unconventional celebration. Eschewing the luxury typical of her elevated status, she drags him to a seedy Elvis karaoke bar, where patrons pay homage to the King in ostentatious outfits. Johnny, out of his depth, fumbles between his germophobic awkwardness and reluctant infatuation with Sarah, while she revels in the absurdity of the situation. Drinks are ordered (a bizarre cocktail named "Death in a Bathroom"), karaoke ensues (featuring hilariously warped Elvi lyrics to *The Wonder of You*), and Sarah thrives in the chaos, as Johnny succumbs to his rising dread, hoping only to survive the night.
