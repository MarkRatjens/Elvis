**Locations**: Tony’s penthouse atop Hardy Tower; dimly lit living area with simulated “Mountain View” windows.

**Synopsis**:
Tony’s opulent yet solitary existence, marked by excess and emotional stagnation, devolves further as he isolates himself in his penthouse. Haunted by unresolved feelings for Sarah and guilt over Halo’s conflicting roles in his survival and the attack on Sarah, Tony avoids the outside world and drowns in simulated distractions. News of Sarah’s "holy joint venture" exacerbates his despair as he obsesses over the fading vibrancy of his once sought-after lifestyle.

His monotonous exile is interrupted when Fisher announces the arrival of three Elvi delegates clad in ceremonial jumpsuits. The visitors, sent by the High Elvis, implore Tony to headline the "Rock Against Revival" festival, emphasizing the concert’s significance in resisting the cultural infiltration of Gospel music in the Ghetto. Tony dismisses their flattery and firmly declines, declaring his retirement from songwriting.

Unwilling to accept his rejection, the Elvi reveal their "preparedness" with unexpected aggression. A swing from a gilded pool cue knocks Tony unconscious, leaving his role in the movement’s cultural battle uncertain as the scene ends on this jarring note.
