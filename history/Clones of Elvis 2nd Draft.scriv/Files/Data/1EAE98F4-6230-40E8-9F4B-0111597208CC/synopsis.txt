Location: Partliament House of the Elvi
Scene: The High Elvis, Tony and other Elvi are meeting about what to do about the Gospel Music Pandemic. Tony pleads that this is well beyond his pay grade (but in a manner more typical of Tony). The High Elvis exhorts Tony as the Great Teacher to join with him to meet the threat. He believes that reason in more powerful than religion and if they can counter the spread of gospel music with a more effective spread of Evolutionary Music, they will win the day. “Not only do we need turn the Elvi away from the the evil of religions, we need to list the whole of Ulyssia to cause” (or something like that).”

The High Elvis proposes and outdoor music festival to be held, not in the the Ghetto or even the Pale, but the relatively pristine grounds of the Domain (which are normally off limits to Cloudborne and especially Elvi). The others in the meeting are astonished at the High Elvis’s audacity.

The sticking points is how can they afford to to book the grounds. Tony offers to pay for it… ‘it’s all just beer money to me now” and they discuss asking Sarah to persuade Eloise to apply the required political force to get regulatory approval.

The High Elvis then turns to Tony and orders him to write new material for the festival. Tony, suffering imposter syndrome and longing for Sarah and Little Clinton doubts he can ever write again, He’ll need to be closer to Sarah to perform. 

The meeting is interrupted by Tony’s device ringing.
