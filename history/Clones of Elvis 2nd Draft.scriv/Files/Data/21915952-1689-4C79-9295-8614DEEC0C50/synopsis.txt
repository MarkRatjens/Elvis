**Locations**: Mansion parlour.

**Tone and Voice Sentence**: "You are a genius, Detective Inspector Hayley Lanning, a genius!"

**Synopsis**:
Hayley interviews Tony Chantara, uncovering his perspective on the investigation. Tony denies involvement in stealing documents from Travis Teh’s office and clarifies his role as a lyricist adored by Elvi for uniting them with his songs. Tony also dismisses paternity claims regarding Sarah Jacobson’s child. After the interview, Alexander Shakti lavishes Hayley with exaggerated praise, framing her investigative work as a sensationalist narrative for television. Alexander imagines leveraging the investigation to expose governmental corruption, particularly targeting PAPER, while expanding his own media empire. Alexander’s self-serving theatrics leave Hayley disengaged but wary, concluding with him expressing relief over Tony’s exoneration, claiming they are “very good friends.”
