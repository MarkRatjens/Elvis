**Locations**: A street in Cloudborne, a ground cab, Memphis Mafia Bar (in the Ghetto).

**Tone and Voice Sentence**: "Jesus Taxing Christ! The Johnny tried to hold Sarah’s hand!"

**Plot Summary**:
- After surviving their ordeal, Sarah and Tony leave the van in favour of a cab ride, with Sarah reluctantly offering directions to a dirty, seedy bar on the edge of the Ghetto. Tony, amused and confident, takes her to the heart of the Ghetto instead, where the Memphis Mafia Bar awaits.
- At the bar, staffed by cheeky characters and surrounded by a lively but run-down atmosphere, the dynamics of their mismatched worlds are on full display. Sarah cautiously scans for surveillance, while Tony indulges in the chaos of Elvi impersonators and cultural gatekeeping.
- Sarah produces the mysterious "disc," its strange rainbow sheen appearing once more, and they discuss its implications. Sarah blames herself for their predicament, while Tony reassures her—but the escalating dangers force them to acknowledge Tony cannot safely return home.
- Sarah contacts Johnny, a tech genius and clear Airborne elitist, to investigate the artifact. Unwilling to be seen associating with a Cloudborne, she dismisses Tony to another table, leaving him to bitterly observe her interactions with Johnny, culminating in jealous frustration.
