**Locations**: Tony’s apartment, fire escape, street, Tony’s van, crash site.

**Tone and Voice Sentence**: “You hit a pothole.” Sarah, containing her fury, enunciated the accusation slowly and precisely.

**Plot Summary**:
- Sarah and Tony's apartment hideout is compromised when Agent Jones tracks them down, storming in and assaulting Tony while demanding a mysterious "disc."
- As Jones overpowers Tony, Sarah finds a case of beer and uses it to knock Jones unconscious, allowing them to escape down the fire escape to Tony’s van amidst palpable tension.
- During a chaotic car chase, Tony evades PAPER agents with amateurish driving, deflecting Sarah’s interrogation about the mysterious disc. Eventually, Sarah finds the "disc," a leathery object Tony took from Emil Kalheri’s corpse, which inexplicably shimmers with a rainbow effect under certain light.
- Examining the object's curious properties, Sarah inadvertently triggers a van crash with a kebab cart. After escaping unscathed, the two assess the damage, finding inexplicable signs at the crash site.
