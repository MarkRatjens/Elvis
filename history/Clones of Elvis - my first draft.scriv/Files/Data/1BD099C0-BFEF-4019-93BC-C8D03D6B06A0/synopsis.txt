**Locations**: The dining room in the mansion.

**Tone and Voice Sentence**: "Seems like it’s the men who are campaigning for the little monster the most."

**Synopsis**:  
Tony joins Sarah and Kathy for a lavish dinner of absurd culinary offerings brought by devout Elvi pilgrims. Amidst the bounty, Captain Denver comments on the Elvi's growing reverence for Sarah and Tony. Sarah’s cravings and the unsettling presence of bizarre dishes underscore her frustration and unease. The conversation shifts to the future of Sarah's unborn child, Little Clinton, with Tony expressing reverent, almost mystical visions of the child’s potential to bring enlightenment. However, Kathy sharply critiques male entitlement, and Sarah reveals her lingering trauma over the forced foetal implantation, voicing doubts about carrying the child to term. The dinner concludes with Sarah reflecting on the loss of her past autonomy and considering her options for the pregnancy.
