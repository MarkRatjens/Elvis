**Locations**: Hayley’s apartment, pedestrian bridges, urban transit, Police Central office.

**Tone and Voice Sentence**: "All of life is a show, it’s merely a matter of how well you broadcast yours."

**Plot Summary**:
- Hayley begins her bleak commute from her drab, perpetually shadowed apartment above the Layer to her office at Police Central. She navigates inconvenient transit and imposed spending algorithms, offering a slice of dystopian routine contrasting against the larger narrative’s escalating tension.
- At Police Central, Hayley’s grim, cockroach-laden environment reflects her strained focus on the Nightclub Vampire case. Her focus is interrupted by the entrance of Alexander Koalemos, whose grandiose and performative personality immediately clashes with Hayley’s no-nonsense detective attitude.
- Alexander, obsessed with framing life as a narrative, imposes his arrogant, broadcast-themed worldview on Hayley. He derails the interaction with ridiculous metaphors, dismissive commentary, and self-aggrandizing rhetoric, framing himself as an "executive producer" in charge of solving the case. 
- The two spar verbally, Alexander revealing deeper motives as he fixates on Sarah Jacobson, whom he believes is central to the broader conspiracy and terrorist activity. His obsession bleeds into his dismissive handling of Tony as a suspect and Hayley as an investigator, exposing his egocentric tendencies.
- Hayley resists Alexander’s attempts to dominate and marginalize her in the investigation, standing firm against his performative worldview and casting doubt on the simplistic conclusions he is attempting to shape for his self-serving narrative.
