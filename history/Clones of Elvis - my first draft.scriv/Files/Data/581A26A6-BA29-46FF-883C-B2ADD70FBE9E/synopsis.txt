**Locations**: Government Square; a secure conference room.  

**Tone and Voice Sentence**: "We must consider the worst possible outcome: the entire population of the Elvi contaminated by religion."

**Synopsis**:  
A high-security meeting convenes over the attack on the Truth building, focused on the Elvi’s  breakout of Tony.  The CEO, directors, and commissioners at the meeting debate the fallout, particularly the Elvi’s exposure to religion, which violates their warranty. The group, alarmed by the potential of widespread religious contagion, considers several extreme measures, including containment, extermination, or accidental "tragedies." Gerry presents a discreet proposal to resolve the problem, leading the CEO to dismiss everyone but him for a private discussion.
