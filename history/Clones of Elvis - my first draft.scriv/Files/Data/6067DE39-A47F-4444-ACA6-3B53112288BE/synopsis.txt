**Locations**: Sarah’s parlour in the Elvi mansion.

**Tone and Voice Sentence**: "Save the Dawkins or we’ll blow you away."

**Synopsis**:  
Captain Denver summons Elvi guards and a performance group into the parlour, where the performers deliver a threatening musical number demanding Sarah carry her child to term, tying its birth to their apocalyptic prophecies. Their song concludes with a chaotic looting of the dinner table, leaving Sarah and Kathy stunned. Captain Denver presents Sarah with an official decree from the newly established Elvi Parliament, confining her to the mansion until the birth of her child, referred to as the "esteemed Dawkins, the Once After the One.” After adding his autograph on the back of the decree, Captain Denver departs, leaving Sarah bewildered and chilled by the escalating situation.
