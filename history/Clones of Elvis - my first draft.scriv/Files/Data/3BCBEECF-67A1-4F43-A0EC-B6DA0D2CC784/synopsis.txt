**Locations**: Eloise’s main office suite; Sarah’s meeting room.

**Tone and Voice Sentence**: "I have a Gyges Disc … another Gyges Disc."

**Synopsis**:  
Johnny Stoat meets Sarah in her new role as head of Eloise Shakti’s "Project Kebab," attempting to leverage his possession of another Gyges Disc to reclaim a position of influence. Despite his boastful demeanor and vague declarations of understanding the disc’s functioning, Sarah outmatches Johnny with sharp, strategic questioning. She challenges his competency by showing images of his disastrous lab explosion and interrogates the true source of his new disc.

While Johnny reluctantly agrees to work under Sarah’s leadership, he reveals critical insights about the Gyges technology, including the necessity of activating the discs in pairs for stability. Sarah, meanwhile, is deeply unsettled by an image from the destroyed lab that strikingly resembles patterns from her recurring nightmares, intensifying her sense of personal entanglement with the project. Though she hires Johnny, Sarah remains skeptical of his efficacy, warning that any deceit will result in both professional and personal dismissal.
