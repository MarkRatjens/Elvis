**Locations**: Johnny's facility; containment bunker; labs and control room.

**Tone and Voice Sentence**: "Five-second bursts at 500 GeV, commencing in thirty seconds."

**Synopsis**:  
From his facility, Johnny oversees an experiment involving a disc placed under a gamma radiation emitter in a containment bunker. The disc, central to Johnny’s covert Gyges Project, undergoes initial tests, during which Johnny cruelly orchestrates a false alarm that terrifies the technicians. Following the prank, proper testing resumes, yet results yield no measurable phenomena, leaving Johnny frustrated. His determination to achieve breakthrough insights persists despite these setbacks, as the emitter's gamma bursts seemingly have no effect on the mysterious disc.
