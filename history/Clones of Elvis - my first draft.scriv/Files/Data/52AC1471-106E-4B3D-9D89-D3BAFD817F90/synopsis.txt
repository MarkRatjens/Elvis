**Locations**: The Stickett Inn’s bombed site, and an improvised outdoor bar on the street.

**Tone and Voice Sentence**: “‘He never did any of those things.’”

**Synopsis**:  
Hayley investigates the bombed remains of the Stickett Inn and encounters Tony’s friends at outdoor bar, where they dismiss accusations against Tony, describing him as harmless and fixated on cleaning tasks. Their banter shifts to Halo, boasting about his success with women. Hayley leverages the casual camaraderie to trick Major into letting her download images of Halo from his phone.
