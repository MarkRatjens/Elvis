**Locations**: Jamison Hardy’s penthouse above Jamison Tower.

**Tone and Voice Sentence**: "We’ve been keeping your proportion of the world’s worth steady at 18.1935%."

**Synopsis**:  
Tony, disoriented and shaken, meets Fisher, an impeccably dressed Elvi who serves as the formal steward of Jamison Tower and its legacy. Fisher informs Tony that by passing through the mysterious Rose Door, his DNA confirmed him as the sole heir to the vast wealth and estate of Jamison Hardy, a once-mighty Airborne billionaire. Tony, overwhelmed by the revelation that he now holds exactly 18.1935% of the world’s wealth, struggles to reconcile his newfound status with his Cloudborne roots.

Fisher, professionally aloof, accommodates Tony's disbelief and attempts to orient him to the extravagance of his new life. He offers Tony fresh clothes and a tour of the penthouse, highlighting the luxurious lounge, panoramic views, and traces of Jamison Hardy’s opulent tastes. Caught between disbelief and tentative acceptance, Tony begins to glimpse the magnitude of his inheritance while remaining skeptical of this sudden elevation. The encounter underscores Tony’s mental dislocation and foreshadows the heavy implications of his claim to Hardy's immense fortune.
