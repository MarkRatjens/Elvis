**Locations**: The Blue Shade nightclub.

**Tone and Voice Sentence**: "For the blunder / The blunder of you."

**Synopsis**:  
Johnny revisits the Blue Shade nightclub, still obsessed with Sarah and haunted by past memories. Amidst the lively crowd celebrating a newfound unity inspired by Tony's Great Teacher persona, Johnny adopts a detached and bitter attitude, dismissing their reverence. After mocking the new order of Elvi harmony, Johnny receives a shocking series of messages on his tablet. Initially summoned to meet Eloise Shakti, he briefly panics, fearing the end of his career. A follow-up cancellation message offers momentary relief, only to be crushed moments later when another message from Shakti Enterprises' Human Resources informs him of his termination, leaving Johnny reeling amidst the nightclub's jubilant atmosphere.
