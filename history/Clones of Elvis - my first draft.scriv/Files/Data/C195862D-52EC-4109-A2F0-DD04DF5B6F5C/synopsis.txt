**Locations**: Hayley’s office, Alexander Koalemos’ communication line.

**Tone and Voice Sentence**: “People became stupider wherever Alexander Koalemos went."

**Plot Summary**:
- Detective Superintendent Hayley Lanning investigates the incompetence and influence of Lieutenant Alexander Koalemos, uncovering his role as a tool for the powerful to dismiss cases that should not be scrutinized. She is particularly dedicated to reclaiming her *Nightclub Vampire* case, now hijacked by Koalemos.
- The two engage in a frustrating phone conversation where Hayley reveals five new murders occurred while Tony Supaporn, the framed main suspect, was in custody. Koalemos disregards the evidence in favour of shaping a "more compelling narrative."
- Koalemos embraces his detachment from reality, speaking in terms of "plot twists" and "scripts," while dismissing the murders as simple elements of a grander, fictionalized conspiracy in which Tony remains the main culprit, ignoring all rational arguments.
- Despite Hayley’s attempts to expose Koalemos’ absurd logic, she is met with his blithe arrogance and a persistent refusal to engage with legitimate detective work, leaving her further convinced of the destructive rot within the Ulyssian police system.
