### Synopsis

**Location:** Sarah's pristine, high-tech Airborne office transitioning briefly to a clinic, then following her trail into the depths of the gritty Pale district.

In her first official day in the new office, Sarah encounters a foreboding agent investigating the mess left behind by the explosive demise of her predecessor, Travis Teh. The sinister visitor, wielding a particle pistol and a snakelike disposition, shows an unsettling interest in the cleaner, Tony. Sarah, using her antique yet highly capable holoscreen, uncovers ominous gaps in Tony’s digital footprint, a void indicative of something—or someone—actively erasing his presence. After piecing together the possibility that she inadvertently sicced this operative on Tony via a rumor, Sarah becomes conflicted yet reluctantly resolved to warn him. 

Following her probing, Sarah ventures into the Pale—a district brimming with shady karaoke bars and Cloudborne outliers—to investigate Tony's whereabouts. Unearthed clues from a nightclub usher her to the unsettling Stickett Inn, where Tony is accompanied by unsavory "bodyguards." However, her arrival coincides with a sudden and violent explosion, leaving her role as a potential savior or bystander dangling amidst the chaos.
