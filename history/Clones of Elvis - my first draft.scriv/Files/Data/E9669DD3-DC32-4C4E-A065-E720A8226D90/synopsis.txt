**Locations**: Eloise’s main estate building; a grand dining room with a panoramic view.

**Tone and Voice Sentence**: "Heaven knows, I can handle the government, the police and even PAPER, but Fridge Force?"

**Synopsis**:  
Eloise confronts Sarah, Kathy, and Hayley over the stasis fridge breach, revealing the potential fallout with Fridge Force under the draconian Refrigeration Secrets Act. Initially scolding Hayley for her unauthorized tampering, Eloise allows the tension to hang, exercising control over the room. In response, Sarah declares her intent to marry Alexander, integrate with Eloise's domain, and secure Little Clinton as Eloise's sole heir. This decision shocks Kathy and Hayley, but Sarah insists it represents her seizing control rather than capitulation.  

As negotiation terms unfold, Sarah demands Kathy as her lifelong personal physician and Hayley as her Head of Security. Eloise eventually concedes, revealing she had anticipated these requests, which align with her vision for Sarah's role in the Gyges Disc project. Hayley, taking her impending security role seriously, identifies her first priority: neutralizing Fridge Force, revealing both a calculated focus on Eloise's interests and a clever pivot to underscore her value within the new arrangement. Eloise, displaying a rare mix of approval and delight, seals the discussion with a symbolic call for champagne and Devonshire tea.
