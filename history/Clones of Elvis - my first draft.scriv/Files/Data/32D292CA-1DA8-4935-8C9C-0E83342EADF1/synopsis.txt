**Locations**: Sarah’s private quarters at Sublime Point; the High Elvis’s headquarters in the Evolution Elvi district; Tony’s penthouse in Hardy Tower.

**Tone and Voice Sentence**: "You are the vessel, Great Teacher!"

**Synopsis**:  
As the TMinus-Virus ravages Elvi populations, Sarah, Kathy, and Hayley uncover evidence suggesting that Tony possesses an immunity to the virus—a side effect of the TPlus-Virus within him. This newfound immunity extends to those with whom Tony has been… intimate, creating a ripple effect that could save countless lives. Armed with this revelation, Sarah leads the trio to the High Elvis, who enthusiastically interprets Tony’s immunity as divine intervention through evolutionary selection.

Both practical and opportunistic strategies emerge as plans are made for a mass-targeted response. The High Elvis envisions "The Great Humperdicking Festival of Evolutionary Advantage," framing the organized spread of Tony’s immunity as sacred communion, cloaking promiscuity in ritualistic fervor. Sarah conflicts between pragmatic intervention and alarm over the cultish opportunism, particularly as she reflects on her own inadvertent connection to the TPlus-Virus and its potential implications.

Meeting Tony at his penthouse, the group presents the findings, surprising him with his unintentional role as the centerpiece of this immunity effort. Though initially skeptical, Tony reluctantly agrees to collaborate in organizing a systemized response, supported by the Elvisettes he has previously "blessed." As the High Elvis exuberantly plans the mass gatherings under the guise of evolutionary ritual, Sarah privately grapples with the irony of Jamison Hardy’s virus becoming a tool for salvation amidst the chaos of an escalating Elvi religious war—each step leaving her further entangled in a surreal fight for the survival of her people.
