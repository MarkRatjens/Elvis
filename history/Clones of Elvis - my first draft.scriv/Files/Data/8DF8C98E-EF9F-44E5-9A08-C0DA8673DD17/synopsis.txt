**Locations**: Outside and inside the mansion in the Ghetto; surrounding skies with PAPER gunships.

**Tone and Voice Sentence**: "I have the tactical advantage," Agent Jones said and fixed a smile across his face.

**Synopsis**:  
Agent Jones descends from a PAPER gunship, weapon drawn on Hayley and Kathy outside the mansion gates. As tensions rise, the Elvi guards maintain a protective stance, declaring Tony Supaporn their revered "Great Teacher" and Sarah Jacobson as "Holy Momma." Hayley confronts Jones with assertions of PAPER’s role in the tangled investigation, including the murder of Travis Teh, but Jones remains resolute in his intent to apprehend Jacobson and Supaporn, branding them state threats. 

Alexander Koalemos interrupts, elevating tension with theatrical arrogance, positioning the standoff as part of his television production. His provocations enrage Jones, who calls in a swarm of PAPER gunships, threatening escalation. Kathy defuses the situation by inviting Hayley and Alexander inside alongside Elvi guards, leaving Jones to command his forces ominously as a sense of impending action looms.
