**Locations**: The ruins of the mansion, particularly the staircase and the surrounding grounds.

**Tone and Voice Sentence**: "All it really means is just / Let your Momma go."

**Synopsis**:  
Crushed by Sarah’s departure and the growing bond between her and Alexander, Tony collapses into despair, retreating into the ruins of the mansion. He sits in a trance-like state atop the stairs, flashing between reveries of Sarah, the unborn Little Clinton, and haunting lyrics resonating in his mind. Surrounded by concerned Elvi, Tony initially resists their attempts to reach him, steeped in grief and creative barrenness.

Tony slowly finds his voice again, crafting a bluesy twelve-bar tune that expresses both his personal anguish and a message of release. The words, directed as much at himself as at the Elvi, call for acceptance and unity, urging them all to let Sarah, the "Holy Momma," grow into her destined role. His soulful performance stirs the Elvi, who gather en masse, first to listen, then to join in the song.

The mansion transforms into a sacred space filled with music and shared purpose. The Elvi weep, cheer, and unite in harmony, resonating with Tony’s transformative moment.
