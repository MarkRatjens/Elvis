**Locations**: Dunhilton's high-level corporate office; various lab sites referenced including the one under the Pale and Jamison Tower.

**Tone and Voice Sentence**: "Greatness never comes without greed."

**Synopsis**:  
In a tense status briefing, Ulyssia CEO Piotr Dunhilton learns from Gerry Hardman that the deadly TMinusVirus (dubbed by Dunhilton as the "Parramatta Virus") has been successfully recovered from a lab beneath the Pale, while the TPlusVirus ("Gummy Bear Virus") remains unaccounted for, potentially still contained beneath Jamison Tower. The conversation reveals increasing pressure on Gerry to expedite the weaponization of the virus within three months to suppress the Elvi, whose newfound unity, armed strength, and rising wealth threaten Airborne supremacy.

The discussion touches on the survival and eventual deaths of long-entombed scientists Hermann and Anstey, whose corpses displayed mysterious preservation traits. As Dunhilton fixates on plans to deploy the virus against the Elvi by Melbourne Cup Day (leveraging the timing to disguise the deaths), Gerry is forced to toe the line, mindful of the dire consequences of failure. The exchange underscores the ruthless greed and paranoia driving Ulyssia’s corporate elite in their efforts to maintain dominance over the Cloudborne, particularly the Elvi and their disruptive rise to power through cultural and economic success.
