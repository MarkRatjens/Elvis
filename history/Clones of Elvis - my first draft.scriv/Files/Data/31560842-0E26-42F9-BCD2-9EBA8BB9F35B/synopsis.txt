**Locations**: St. Hedges Cathedral, within the nave.

**Tone and Voice Sentence**: "Sweet Salt and Vinegar Jesus Chips, doesn’t anyone read a warranty card any more?"

**Synopsis**:  
The elaborate marriage ceremony of Sarah Jacobson and Alexander Koalemos takes place under the auspices of the Very Arch Bishop d’Molesto at St. Hedges Cathedral. The ritual, steeped in capitalist scripture from the New Ulyssian Market Bible, glorifies the Market’s dominion and frames the union as a "holy joint venture." While Alexander recites his vows with ostentatious sincerity, Sarah’s terse delivery and informal tone betray her personal reservations.

The ceremony is given an unintentional twist when the exuberant Elvi in attendance proclaim Sarah as the “Holy Momma of the New Church of the One After the One," launching into song and celebratory chanting. Their overt displays of devotion prompt visible outrage from CEO Dunhilton, who berates the Very Arch Bishop for exposing the Elvi clones to religious concepts, threatening their warranty compliance. In the midst of this tension, Sarah’s prominence grows as a central figure of Elvi adoration, fully solidifying her unintended status as their revered leader.
