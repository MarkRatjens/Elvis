**Locations**: Government Square's Secure Summit Center; Memphis Mafia Bar (via surveillance footage); Elvi Parliament.

**Tone and Voice Sentence**: "We are now at war with Sublime Point."

**Synopsis**:  
In a high-stakes cabinet meeting, CEO Piotr Dunhilton learns that the TMinus-Virus is failing to eliminate the Elvi, with recovery rates inexplicably spiking. Surveillance footage reveals the rise of "The Humperdicking," a wildly unorthodox and highly effective immunity-spreading ritual among the Elvi. Compounding matters, Sarah Jacobson’s recent visits to key Elvi locations appear to coincide with the virus's neutralization, implicating her—and possibly Eloise Shakti—in what Gerry Hardman identifies as the deployment of the classified TPlus-Virus.

As Gerry explains, the TPlus-Virus—a regenerative counter-agent to the TMinus-Virus—was Jamison Hardy’s dangerous invention, supposedly eradicated but now spreading via Tony Hardy, the "Great Teacher," who unknowingly acts as its carrier. With the Elvi leveraging this viral promiscuity and planning a massive concert, "Rock Against Revival," immunity is projected to reach critical mass within days.

Realizing Eloise Shakti’s likely involvement in deploying the TPlus-Virus to sabotage government plans, Dunhilton declares war on Sublime Point and orders full mobilization against her. Meanwhile, Gerry, quietly satisfied, anticipates the collision of forces as part of his own concealed agenda, ensuring he remains a pivotal figure in the unfolding power struggle.
