**Locations**: Eloise's Sublime Point compound; the road to the main building; a narrow stairway leading to a subterranean holding cell.

**Tone and Voice Sentence**: "Sarah was beginning to think they’d descend all the way to the floor of the Jamison Valley—or hell, whichever came first."

**Synopsis**:  
Escorted in their pyjamas by armed guards, Sarah, Kathy, and Hayley are marched from their townhouse to Eloise's imposing palace. The journey begins under the harsh glare of the sun and concludes with a disheartening descent, spiralling down several levels into a stark, utilitarian holding cell. The Three Sisters slump onto the sole bunk, silently processing their situation. Contrary to Eloise's expectations of discord, Hayley expresses remorse for their predicament, blaming herself for escalating tensions. Sarah and Kathy reject her self-blame, with Kathy acknowledging her “no free lunch” rant as the spark for their ill-fated actions.

Eloise, monitoring the scene via her tablet, is surprised by their cohesion and subdued reaction, finding no animosity or fractures to exploit among them. Though anticipating more dramatic fallout, Eloise eventually dismisses the surveillance, deciding after a brief intermission to have the three women brought back up.
