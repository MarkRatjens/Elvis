**Locations**: Inn bathroom, streets of the Ghetto, outside the Ghetto.

**Tone and Voice Sentence**: "In the Ghetto, it was never a question of whether you took half-and-half, sugar or amphetamines; it was a question of how much."

**Plot Summary**:
- Tony wakes to arguing buskers outside, feeling uncharacteristically revitalized. Discovering his burns healed and his body in peak form, he gleefully examines himself in the mirror, discovering a bold new sense of invincibility.
- Overwhelmed by his newfound vigor, Tony decides to channel his energy into a task he deems worthy—getting Sarah a coffee. Despite his earnest attempts, the peculiarities of Ghetto coffee culture thwart his plans; the overly indulgent preferences of the Elvi clones make finding a simple brew impossible.
- Tony’s determination leads him to wander further afield, eventually losing direction and unknowingly exiting the Ghetto. Before he can realize his mistake, he is suddenly ambushed and forcibly taken by unknown assailants into a shiny black suburban, cutting short his mission.
