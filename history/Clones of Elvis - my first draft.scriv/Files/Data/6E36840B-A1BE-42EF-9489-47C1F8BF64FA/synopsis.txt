**Locations**: Various HIBReLab facilities; Western Desert; the Pale district; Jamison Tower in the financial district.

**Tone and Voice Sentence**: "The economy needs an underclass to have long, drawn out illnesses and die horribly. And expensively."

**Synopsis**:  
Dunhilton tasks Gerry with locating the long-hidden HIBReLabs facility containing the Parramatta Virus—a cure-all that threatens the lucrative chronic illness economy. Gerry’s team begins excavation at low-risk sites in the Western Desert, progressing systematically through geographically easier locations while uncovering new lab sites along the way. Each facility proves deeply buried and painstaking to access, constructed with reinforced ultra-high-performance concrete to preserve contents during excavation. As weeks pass, only two potential locations remain: one under the impoverished Pale district, infamous for its unforgiving environmental conditions, and the other beneath Jamison Tower in the financial district. With growing trepidation, Gerry confronts the likelihood that the virus is housed in the most precarious Pale site, along with potential remnants of ancient scientists.
