**Synopsis**

**Locations**: Various urban locations, Truth interrogation facility.

**Tone and Voice Sentence**: "Agent Jones watched, as if from the comfort of a private box at the opera."

**Plot Summary**:
- Agent Jones traces an ambulance linked to a suspicious death but discovers the orderlies provided false identities, triggering his paranoia about a potential internal conspiracy within PAPER.
- The orderlies, Dick and Rick, are apprehended and taken to the ominous Truth interrogation facility. Strapped into advanced "experience sheaths," they are subjected to psychological torment designed to extract information, despite their attempts to cooperate.
- Against objections from clinicians, Agent Jones demands escalating mutilation, such as the severing of fingers and threats of genital torture, to encourage the orderlies' compliance, illustrating his sadistic tendencies.
- Under duress, Dick and Rick reveal a crucial detail: a Eurasian male cleaner associated with SupaTones Cleaning at the center of suspicions. This discovery aligns with previous sightings of a cleaner near key incidents, hinting at a deeper conspiracy.
- Dissatisfied and bored with the interrogation, Agent Jones leaves with newfound leads but no remorse for the destruction he inflicted.
