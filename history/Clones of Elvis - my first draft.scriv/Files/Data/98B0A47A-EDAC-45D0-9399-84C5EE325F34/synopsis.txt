**Locations**: Sarah's quarters in the Elvi-controlled mansion.

**Tone and Voice Sentence**: "Bombing survivor, PAPER survivor, nightclub riot survivor, Vegas Show survivor …"

**Plot Summary**:
- Sarah confronts the escalating surrealism of her situation, realizing her obsessive behaviors and sickness reveal a pregnancy she initially dismisses as impossible. Kathy, her effervescent but astute doctor, presses Sarah for details, uncovering layers of confusion, panic, and anger.
- Haunted by fractured memories of recent events, Sarah wrestles with theories for her condition—blaming Tony as a possible attacker before shifting suspicion onto the enigmatic sages and their prophetic manipulations. Kathy supports Sarah as she processes this maze of doubts, offering calm reassurance amid Sarah's spiraling accusations and misgivings.
- Fueled by Kathy's revelation that the Elvi already regard her as the "Holy Momma," Sarah connects dots between their cult-like deference, the prophecy, and her inexplicable role in a shifting power dynamic. Despite her self-doubt, glimpses of Sarah’s sharp instinct and resilience surface, bolstered by Kathy's affirmation of her importance.
