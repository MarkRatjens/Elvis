### Synopsis

**Location:** An executive office in an Airborne tower, above the toxic Layer—a space reeking of cleaning fluids after a graphic, shotgun-induced suicide.

Tony, a down-on-his-luck cleaner from the Cloudborne underclass, is dealing with the aftermath of a particularly gruesome suicide when he meets Sarah, a commanding Airborne woman stepping into her new office. Clad in ripped overalls exposing poorly chosen cartoon fairy boxers, Tony’s comedic and awkward attempts at small talk only punctuate the social and financial divides between them. Sarah, sharp and unapologetically dominant, subtly mocks him but also wields intrigue, discovering Tony’s links to homicide investigations through his cleaning gigs. Their exchange oscillates between biting humor and subtle power-play, layered with unspoken attraction (on Tony’s end) and Sarah’s growing curiosity about her own rumored significance. Their interaction ends with Tony leaving embarrassed but fascinated, while Sarah, triumphantly locking her new office, reflects on her upward trajectory and Tony’s fairytale underwear with amusement.
