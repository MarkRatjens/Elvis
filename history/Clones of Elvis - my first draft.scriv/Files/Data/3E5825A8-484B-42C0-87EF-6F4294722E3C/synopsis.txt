**Locations**: Tony’s penthouse at Hardy Tower; dining area, living room, master suite threshold.

**Tone and Voice Sentence**: "You don’t understand what you’re—"

**Synopsis**:  
Tony stages an extravagant evening to impress Sarah, orchestrating every detail, from lavish décor to a fourteen-course meal, in a bid to woo her back. Sporting a radiant vitality and newfound confidence, Tony captivates Sarah with his charm, though his opulent lifestyle and ostentatious antics evoke both amusement and unease in her. As the evening unfolds, Tony’s humor, boyish enthusiasm, and vulnerability rekindle their chemistry, leading to an intimate moment of connection.

However, as Tony leads Sarah toward his master suite, her discomfort escalates into visceral revulsion. Her sudden aversion is tied to the legacy of Jamison Hardy, whose wealth Tony now commands, and the man’s dark history haunts her. Stricken by the reality of what Tony represents, Sarah breaks away, citing her pregnancy to excuse her swift departure. 

A misunderstanding unfolds as Tony, feeling rejected, defensively declares his intention to return to a hedonistic lifestyle with the Elvines. His revelation—that his interactions seem to rejuvenate and beautify his partners—horrifies Sarah, who perceives this as evidence of something monstrous within Tony, fueled by his connection to Jamison Hardy’s fortune. Fleeing in tears, Sarah leaves Tony bewildered and alone, his charmed wealth and charisma hollow comfort as he realizes he may have crossed a line he can’t see or understand.
