**Synopsis**:  
Agent Jones fixates on Gerry Hardman, meticulously constructing a web of supposed connections between Gerry, Eloise Shakti, and conspiratorial movements tied to the Gyges Disc and the broader Elvi phenomenon. Operating from a state of apophenic paranoia, Jones uses cocktail napkins to outline his suspicions and concoct theories of grand unified evil, branding Gerry as "Jihad Man." Jones’ internal monologue reveals his stewing distrust, especially regarding Gerry’s unexpected influence over Ulyssia's CEO and PAPER’s seeming vulnerability. Amid this mounting tension, Jones resolves himself to uncover Gerry's role—real or imagined—as central to the sprawling chaos surrounding the prophecy, Sarah Jacobson, and the technologically coveted Gyges Disc.
