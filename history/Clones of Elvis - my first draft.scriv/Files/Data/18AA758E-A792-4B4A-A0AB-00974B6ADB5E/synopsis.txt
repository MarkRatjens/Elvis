**Locations**: Bomb site of the Stickett Inn; surrounding district.

**Tone and Voice Sentence**: "‘National security costs extra.’"

**Synopsis**:  
Gerry Hardman secures a deal with Ahmed Siddig, owner of the bombed Stickett Inn site, to lease the property under the guise of a national security operation. Siddig, financially desperate, agrees in exchange for Gerry’s promise to influence his uncooperative insurance company. To maintain cover, Gerry’s operation uses workers, branding, and equipment identical to the original crews. Excavations proceed unnoticed by most, blending seamlessly into the urban environment. However, Agent Jones, following detailed intel and drone footage, identifies police officers posing as construction workers and notes evidence of tunneling and explosives, leading him to suspect a major clandestine operation is underway and attributing its orchestration to "Jihad Man."
