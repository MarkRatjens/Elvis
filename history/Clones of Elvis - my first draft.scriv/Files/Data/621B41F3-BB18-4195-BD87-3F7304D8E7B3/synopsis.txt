**Locations**: Mansion parlour.

**Tone and Voice Sentence**: “I’m considering making your child my sole heir.”

**Synopsis**:  
Eloise Shakti’s arrival at the mansion culminates in tense exchanges, where her domineering presence overshadows Alexander, Hayley, and the Elvi guards. After dismissing the guards, Eloise turns to Sarah, asserting authority while probing into Sarah’s connections with Clinton Dawkins' child. Kathy provides fabricated DNA analysis referencing Dawkins as Sarah’s unborn child’s father. Eloise, seemingly convinced, shocks everyone by proposing to adopt Sarah’s child as her sole heir. Eloise further strengthens the tension by asking Sarah to marry Alexander, just as Agent Jones attacks the mansion.
