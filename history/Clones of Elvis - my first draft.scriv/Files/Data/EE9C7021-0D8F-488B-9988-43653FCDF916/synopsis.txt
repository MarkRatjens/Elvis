**Locations**: Secure conference room, Government Square.

**Tone and Voice Sentence**: "We must consider the worst possible outcome: the entire population of the Elvi contaminated by religion."  

**Synopsis**:  
Gerry presents his solution for dealing with the Elvi clones exposed to religion, a violation of their operational warranty. He reveals classified intelligence and proposes deploying a genetically targeted virus created by past research to eliminate the Elvi clones. CEO Dunhilton authorizes Gerry to oversee the operation in coordination with Defense, also granting him control of PAPER intelligence. As the gravity of the potential virus's impact is assessed, Gerry includes the revelation of a secondary, unintended virus with self-optimizing properties.
