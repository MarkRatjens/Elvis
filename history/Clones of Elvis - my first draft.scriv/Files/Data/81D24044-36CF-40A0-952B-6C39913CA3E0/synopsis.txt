**Locations**: Agora Symposium Bar; VIP section; makeshift pankration ring in the open room.

**Tone and Voice Sentence**: "The time for half-measures has passed. When Sarah's waters break, I will ensure the discs are at maximum resonance."

**Synopsis**:  
In the overly thematic Agora Symposium Bar, Chilo, Solo, and Kleo meet under the banner of "Seven Sages LLC" to discuss the escalating stakes of Project Kebab. Reclining in the ostentatiously kitschy VIP section, tensions escalate between the trio as Chilo insists on the transformative necessity of a cataclysmic event to bring forth the new Dawkins, while Solo and Kleo advocate for restraint, preservation, and measured evolution.

As the conversation deepens, Chilo reveals his intent to align the Gyges Discs at maximum resonance during Sarah’s labor, effectively triggering a collapse of dimensional barriers and initiating a reborn universe in the child’s image. Solo and Kleo vehemently oppose his deterministic vision, which dismisses free will and incremental change, framing it as a dangerous gamble with the existing reality.

Rising in mutual confrontation, the discussion transitions into an unorthodox physical altercation, where centuries-old philosophies of restraint, ambition, and balance are argued through the archaic combat art of pankration. As patrons cheer, mistaking the clash for bar entertainment, the three men’s struggle symbolizes the broader cosmic stakes tied to their conflict. The ambiguous outcome leaves unresolved whether Chilo’s unchecked ambition—or Kleo and Solo’s resistance—will shape the fate of Sarah’s unborn child and the multiverse it may call into existence.
