**Locations**: Eloise Shakti’s downtown office.

**Tone and Voice Sentence**: "Safe travels, darling. Do try not to accidentally film the end of the world as we know it while you’re away."

**Synopsis**:  
Eloise Shakti deftly maneuvers her son Alexander into an "opportunity" to lead Shakti Enterprises’ newly acquired VorpalTech AG in Liechtenstein. Framing the assignment with layers of incentives—including experimental "Quantum Reality Lenses" capable of filming alternate dimensions and mysterious multidimensional anomalies—she entices Alexander under the guise of artistic and professional advancement. Alexander, initially resistant, becomes enamored by the allure of power and creativity, particularly upon being presented with embossed business cards signaling his new, fabricated title.

Eloise further appeals to Alexander’s vanity and paranoia, suggesting the relocation as a means to protect him from political threats posed by Piotr Dunhilton and to keep him occupied while Sarah’s pregnancy unfolds. Sufficiently baited, Alexander reluctantly agrees, bargaining for creative control and an open expense account. Eloise subtly maintains the upper hand, concealing the true motivations behind the assignment while ensuring Alexander is preemptively removed from the local spotlight, preventing any impulsive disruptions as her broader plans unfold. Their transaction concludes with Eloise’s calculated reassurances and a parting warning not to unintentionally document catastrophe while exploring dimensions.
