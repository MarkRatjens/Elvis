**Locations**: Sarah’s suite in Eloise Shakti’s Sublime Point compound.

**Tone and Voice Sentence**: "Tony. Mr Fairy Boxers."

**Synopsis**:  
In the aftermath of Eloise’s proposition, Sarah wrestles with the implications of marrying Alexander and the Faustian bargain it represents. Awakening from fitful sleep and disturbing dreams, including marrying Alexander dressed as a burger clown, Sarah finds herself confronting her own reflection, confessing her inner turmoil and anguish aloud. The allure of Eloise’s monumental wealth and influence tempts her Airborne-instilled ambitions, promising a meteoric rise above the Layer. However, the price—aligning her future and her unborn child with Alexander and Eloise’s designs—leaves her hollow and conflicted.

Reflecting on her rebellious past of consorting with the Elvi for both thrill and defiance, Sarah considers their transformation under Tony's influence as the Great Teacher, sparking international acclaim. Simultaneously, doubts plague her regarding her baby’s paternity. Despite Kathy's evidence suggesting genetic manipulation tied to a long-dead scientist, Sarah's thoughts linger on Tony. Torn between her disdain for Alexander and her unresolved sentiments toward Tony, Sarah concludes she belongs fully with neither. The unresolved question of her own desires and identity hangs heavily as the night deepens.
