**Locations**: Sarah’s townhouse in Sublime Point.

**Tone and Voice Sentence**: "… alongside all of them Tony was a the one true knight in somewhat tarnished armour.”

**Synopsis**:  
Johnny Stoat arrives unannounced at Sarah’s townhouse under the pretense of checking on her welfare, bringing awkward and ill-considered gifts like stress balls with motivational slogans and a calming orchid. While outwardly expressing concern for Sarah’s health and suggesting she "step back" from the Gyges Disc project to rest, Johnny covertly attempts to position himself as the project’s de facto leader. His clumsy maneuvering, however, is met with Sarah’s sharp wit and unflinching resolve.

Feigning a concession, Sarah deftly manipulates Johnny into taking on the unenviable and ambiguous task of finalizing the Phase Four configuration parameters and delivering a comprehensive report to Eloise. By the time Johnny realizes the depth of his new responsibilities, he is halfway out the door, too late to reverse the situation. 

Alone, Sarah reflects on the encounter with a mix of amusement and disgust, recognizing Johnny's transparent opportunism as yet another example of men posturing for dominance. Her thoughts inevitably drift to Tony, whose uncalculated sincerity stands in stark contrast to Johnny’s plotting.
