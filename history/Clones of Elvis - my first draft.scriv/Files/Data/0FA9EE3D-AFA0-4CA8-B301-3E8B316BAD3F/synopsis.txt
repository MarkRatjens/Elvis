**Locations**: Laboratory wing of Sublime Point; Gyges Disc testing facility.

**Tone and Voice Sentence**: "It is as the prophecy predicted: And the power of the light shall be bestowed upon the keepers of time and space in the likeness of a pentagram."

**Synopsis**:  
Sarah forces Chip Stockwell to participate as the Sages and The Johnny resume experiments with the Gyges Discs. The Johnny unveils progress: the generation of complex, multiverse-spanning script patterns observable through the calibrated temporal matrix—a validation that the discs are channeling coded transmissions. Chilo and Kleo cryptically theorize that the discs serve as vessels for inter-universal communication, while Chip raises grave concerns about the dangers of amplifying their power, referencing catastrophic events like the Rotherhithe Stasis Collapse.

Eager to appear visionary, The Johnny impulsively suggests expanding the scale of the experiment by incorporating additional discs, a recommendation enthusiastically encouraged by the Sages despite Chip’s intensifying warnings about the risks and legal violations involved. Chilo manipulates The Johnny’s ego by contextualizing the plan within prophetic terms, invoking imagery of power and pentagrams. Caught between mysticism and faux-scientific innovation, the group aligns on a dangerous configuration of five discs.

Chip, exasperated and increasingly aware of the recklessness before him, resolves to bring his concerns directly to Sarah, though his attempts to convince the group fall on deaf ears. The Johnny, basking in the grandiosity of his perceived leadership, cements the plan as prophecy and personal triumph, oblivious to the perilous path unfolding around him.
