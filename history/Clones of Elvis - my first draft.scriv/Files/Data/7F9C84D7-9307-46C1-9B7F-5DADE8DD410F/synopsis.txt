**Locations**: Bent Street outside Tramp Tower; inside Tramp Tower’s executive offices and Gerry Hardman’s corner office.

**Tone and Voice Sentence**: "Linguistic abominations! In my day, protesters at least had the decency to use proper grammar when undermining authority."

**Synopsis**:  
The Domain concert catalyzes a rapid escalation of unrest as thousands of Airborne and Cloudborne youth converge on Bent Street, outside Tramp Tower, transforming a disorganized protest into an unprecedented movement demanding systemic change. Dubbed “Get Bent Street,” the protest becomes a unique mix of cultures and classes, with communal kitchens, collaborative music performances, and shared ideals. Makeshift banners and slogans like "Transparency For The 1%" and "Tax The Rich Or Eat Them" challenge Ulyssia’s rigid social and economic hierarchies.

The Evolution Elvi’s infectious tunes, now reworked with politically charged lyrics, become rallying anthems. Protesters adopt the refrain from "Piglicious Minds," blending critiques of police brutality, corporate surveillance, and inequality, bellowed loudly enough to rattle the corporate police executives watching nervously from Tramp Tower. Attempts to decode "piglicious" spark both confusion and frustration within the Airborne elite, further highlighting the disconnect between the ruling class and the protesters below.

As the protest grows, news outlets provide split-screen broadcasts juxtaposing the vibrant rebellion with Pastor Humble’s evangelical revival meetings, symbolizing the cultural and ideological fractures gripping Ulyssia. By nightfall, the protest solidifies its rituals and unity, projecting images of the High Elvis’s Domain concert onto nearby buildings and chanting anti-oppression slogans like "Praise the Holy Momma" and "Praise the Dawkins."

From his office, Gerry Hardman grapples with the evolving complexity of deploying the TMinus-Virus, designed specifically for Elvi but now likely to harm Airborne and Cloudborne youth participating in the movement. As the protesters settle in for the night, unified across class lines like never before, Gerry updates the deployment plan with a note on the unexpected "social mutation" the movement has created, signaling the profound and unpredictable challenges ahead.
