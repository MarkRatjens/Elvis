**Locations**: Tony's bedroom in the recording studio apartment.

**Tone and Voice Sentence**: "Great influencer, great teacher, great sandwich-maker … I’m none of those things."

**Synopsis**:  
Tony’s depression deepens in the days after his relocation, leaving him reclusive and apathetic, haunted by his separation from Sarah and Little Clinton. One night, he is visited by Solo Athena. Solo insists Tony is central to fulfilling an important prophecy and urges him to overcome his despair, assuring him he will reunite with Sarah in time. Skeptical at first, Tony becomes momentarily invigorated by Solo’s declaration and seeks clarity about Sarah’s situation. Though Solo avoids direct answers, his admission of responsibility for Sarah’s pregnancy and pledge to reunite them reassures Tony, leaving him resolved to wait for destiny even as he dismisses the apparition to reclaim his rest.
