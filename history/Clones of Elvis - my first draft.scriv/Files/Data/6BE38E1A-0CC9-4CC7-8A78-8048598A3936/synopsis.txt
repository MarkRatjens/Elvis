**Synopsis**:  
Hayley digs into the case of Sukrit Supaporn (Tony), whose existence is strenuously concealed across all data networks. Encountering invasive barriers to records, she presses through privacy-violating authentication methods and even resorts to illicit data piracy. Analyzing Supaporn’s media appearances reveals staged injuries and raises suspicions he may not be real. When cross-referencing locations between the Stickett Inn bombing and the Nightclub Vampire killings, she identifies a pattern resembling a ring with the Stickett Inn centrally located, intensifying her doubts about official narratives around Supaporn and the broader case.
