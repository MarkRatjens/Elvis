**Locations**: HIBReLabs facility beneath the Pale; adjacent bomb site; bioweapon bunker area.

**Tone and Voice Sentence**: "If we put so much as a crack in any of the Airborne real estate over that area, we’ll all be retired."  

**Synopsis**:  
At a HIBReLabs status meeting, Gerry Hardman outlines plans to excavate the final site suspected of housing the Parramatta Virus, located beneath the Pale and near the foundation of exclusive Airborne residential towers. A junior team member presents a bold alternative: tunneling laterally from a nearby nightclub bombing site, minimizing risks to the Airborne infrastructure. The bomb site, now a large excavation, offers easy access to the necessary depth with fewer obstructions, expediting the operation. Gerry, quickly claiming the idea as his own amid group tensions, begins considering this lateral excavation strategy as the primary approach to protect Airborne interests while fulfilling his search mandate.
