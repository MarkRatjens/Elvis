**Locations**: Corridor outside restaurant amenities; service elevator; Gravity Well restaurant.  

**Tone and Voice Sentence**: "That mother-taxer deserves all he got."  

**Synopsis**:  
At the Gravity Well restaurant, Halo, intent on orchestrating Sarah’s abduction, ambushes her in the amenities corridor. Using professional abduction tactics, he forces her into a service elevator, preparing to relocate her to a sinister "safe house" he has meticulously fitted for his gruesome plans. However, Sarah, realizing the stakes, channels both physical skill and maternal determination to fiercely resist. 

A brutal fight ensues within the confines of the elevator, where Sarah uses creative and relentless defenses to outmaneuver Halo, ultimately disarming him and holding his own weapon—his infamous Slice of Life—against him. Hayley and Tony, alerted by Sarah’s absence, race to the scene, joined by Elvi and Eloise’s entourage. As Hayley confronts Halo, a chaotic scuffle ends with a mortal injury dealt to Halo, seemingly accidental, involving his trademark weapon. 

While paramedics and suspicious figures whisk Halo’s lifeless body away, Tony, emotionally shattered, takes possession of a thumb drive handed to him by Halo in his dying moments, containing cryptic secrets. Meanwhile, Alexander exploits the scene’s drama for a live broadcast, humorously undercut by Eloise, reigning in her son and ensuring no further rash actions or media missteps worsen the situation. Sarah stands resilient amid the chaos, supported by Kathy and Hayley, her resolve forged deeper by the encounter.
