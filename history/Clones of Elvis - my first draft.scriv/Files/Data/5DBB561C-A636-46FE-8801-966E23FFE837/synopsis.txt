**Locations**: Kitchen in Eloise Shakti’s Sublime Point compound; the stasis fridge.

**Tone and Voice Sentence**: "There’s no such thing as a free lunch."

**Synopsis**:  
While preparing sandwiches, Kathy launches into a tirade against technological conveniences, particularly stasis technology, emphasizing that every innovation comes with hidden consequences. Suspecting Gyges Discs might power stasis fridges, she grows increasingly manic, challenging Sarah and Hayley to consider the potential dangers of seemingly perfect technology. 

Her skepticism peaks when she irrationally attempts to dismantle the fridge to prove her theory, prompting a chaotic scramble as Sarah and Hayley try to intervene. The struggle devolves into a farcical tussle, ultimately tiring everyone out and leaving them sprawled on the fridge floor in an awkward tangle.
