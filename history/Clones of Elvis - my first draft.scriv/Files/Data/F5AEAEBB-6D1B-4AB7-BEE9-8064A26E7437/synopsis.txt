**Locations**: Sarah's bedroom in the Elvi mansion.

**Tone and Voice Sentence**: "It’s coming to get me and I can’t stop it. It’s coming to get us all."

**Synopsis**:  
Waking from another intense nightmare, Sarah struggles to breathe and recover, alarmed by a recurring sense of dread and helplessness tied to her pregnancy. Kathy intervenes with care, monitoring Sarah’s rising blood pressure and offering comfort while expressing concern. Sarah describes her visions of a fractal-like pattern etched into a window, radiating malevolent, ancient indifference and exerting an irresistible gravitational pull. Haunted by the feelings of inevitability and doom, Sarah reveals her fear that her unborn child, Little Clinton, is somehow responsible for transmitting these harrowing dreams, leaving both women shaken.
