**Locations**: Small private airstrip; Sublime Point; Alexander’s temporary command center in the Cloudborne District; Agora Symposium Bar; surveillance van.

**Tone and Voice Sentence**: "Candid Criminal began by exposing the absurdities of everyday life. It will end by documenting the transformation of reality itself."

**Synopsis**:  
Returning discreetly to Ulyssia under the guise of an extended European honeymoon, Alexander Shakti covertly establishes a command center, fixating on investigating Sarah’s pregnancy, the mysterious Sages, and unsettling activities at Sublime Point. Discovering the Sages' obscure academic connections and cryptic ties to "The Ephesian Prophecy," Alexander grows alarmed, realizing they are manipulating Sarah and her unborn child for catastrophic ends tied to universal rebirth.

Alexander deploys surveillance operations, eventually tracking the Sages to the Agora Symposium Bar. Observing through concealed means, he overhears their heated discussion of Gyges Discs, dimensional barriers, and a reality-altering event involving Sarah’s child. The debate descends into a surreal physical confrontation, underscoring the high stakes of their conflicting interpretations of the prophecy.

Shaken but resolved, Alexander frames their apocalyptic schemes as the perfect finale for his media career, "Candid Criminal: Apocalypse Edition," plotting to expose their plans to the public. Determined to save Sarah and their son amidst reality-threatening stakes, Alexander’s media ambitions converge with uncharacteristic selflessness as he orchestrates a broadcast designed to unveil the Sages’ actions and confront the cosmic transformation they plan to ignite.
