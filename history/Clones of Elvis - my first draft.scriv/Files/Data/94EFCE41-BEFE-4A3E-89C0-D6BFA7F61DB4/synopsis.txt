**Synopsis:**  

**Location(s):** Office tower in Ulyssia (200+ floors above the Layer) → Chapelle Boogie Bar (nearby 24-hour establishment)  

**Signature Tone Sentence:**  
*"‘Tax you!’ Emil cursed his balls. ‘You won’t get the chance to kill me!’"*  

**Plot Progression:**  
- **Emil Kalheri**, having just executed Travis Teh per **Special Agent Jones'** Blank Contract, revels in his power (even pausing to urinate/victory-strut in the victim’s opulent bathroom) before abandoning his mission to get drunk.  
- At the **Chapelle Boogie Bar**, Emil flaunts his designer suit and drains his consumer account on tequila while musing about the disc he stole—a seemingly worthless object that briefly glints and coincides with an unexplained earthquake.  
- His drunken parody of Agent Jones ("I’m a stupid Special Agent, whose first name is also Ajent") is cut short when he collapses from a heart attack—a grim punchline to his defiance of both the contract and his terminal cancer.  

**Key Exposition/Internal Reflection:**  
- Emil’s nihilistic glee stems from trading his life for financial security for his family (Peggy, Becky, Roger), though a fleeting wave of sorrow hints at unresolved guilt.  
- The disc’s inexplicable properties (bendable yet resilient, crocodile-hide-like texture) and its possible link to the earthquake tease larger conspiracy.  
- Emil’s death—unheroic, unrelated to his contract or cancer—underscores the story’s satirical edge: in this dystopia, systemic neglect (unaffordable healthcare) is deadlier than assassins.  

**Themes Highlighted:**  
- The absurdity of power (Emil’s fleeting dominance vs. ignoble death)  
- Bureaucratic violence (Blank Contracts as casual murder-for-hire)  
- Class satire (designer suits as access keys, tequila as last rebellion)
