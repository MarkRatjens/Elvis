Sort of a job performance review for Agent Jones.  The new director of "Proactive Euthenasia and Pre-emptive Reciprocity" Damien Hardman flexing his muscles a bit.  This meeting shows how tenuous an agent's employment (and life expectancy) is.  (Then sets up Agent Jones to interrogate the operatives (who picked up the Emil Kalheri's body) to assist in identifying Cracker.  Which in turn sets up Agent Jones visiting Cracker at home and torturing him.  Then Sarah and Hayley turn up to speak with Cracker and then Shakti turns up to arrest Cracker.  It is meant to be farcical - Spys, Cops and More cops turn up to speak with Cracker, all for different reasons, but all pretty much at the same time.)
