Introduction to the incompetent Lieutenant Shakti - I like the idea of this character bumbling his way through life, always trying but never quite getting there.  Another example of a privileged person being granted the keys to the kingdom.  His brain does not function correctly it seems, as his brain would not accept brain-dumps - "Monkeys retain information better".  He eventually joins the police - a complete humiliation for his mother and father.  Later you find out that the rank of "Lieutenant" does not even exist and it was created just for him so he would not be taking orders from the 'cloudborn'.

Shakti is only good at reading off a cue card and is placed in the public affairs division - and then has his own TV show - Random Camera.  All this is a bit of a wank and didn't really go anywhere.  So it can be re-cycled or dumped.  There are a couple of further mentions of Random Camera later in the book.  Random Camera is a trope-fest.
