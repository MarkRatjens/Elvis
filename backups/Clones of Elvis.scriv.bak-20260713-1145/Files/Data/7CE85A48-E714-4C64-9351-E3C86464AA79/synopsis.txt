On the VIP platform raised in front of the engineering tower, two thrones wait -- one for the Holy Momma, one for the Great Teacher. Sarah occupies hers; Chanta's stays conspicuously empty, he's spending the day carousing with bands and Elvisettes in the green room rather than face her. Aunt Kathy and Aunt Hayley have their own seats alongside -- a couple since "After the Barbecue," and a look passes between them across the chaos below. Tethered overhead, presiding over the whole platform, floats Colonel Tom Porker -- a pig in a cigar and an oversized Stetson, the crowd's own "Tax the Tom Parkers!" joke made literal and airborne. Sarah watches the crowd she'd hoped, today, to disappear into. Just after cocktail hour, Eloise arrives -- she has tracked Sarah's small escape, says nothing about it, has her own chair (the "Ermine Muller") set down beside the throne, and sits. No reproach, no cross word: the having-been-found is the entire message. One contained flicker crosses Sarah before she puts her face back to the crowd she can no longer vanish into.
