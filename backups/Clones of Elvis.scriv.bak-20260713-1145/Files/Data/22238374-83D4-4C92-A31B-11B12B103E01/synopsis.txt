**Locations**: The "Truth" studio, a highly controlled torture and interrogation set.

**Tone and Voice Sentence**: "Interrogation and torture at nine sharp."

**Plot Summary**:
- Tony begins his grimly routine day in captivity, cycling through the dehumanizing morning schedule designed to break him physically and mentally. His ordeal culminates in another session on the set of *Curb Your Individualism*, where he endures torment at the hands of Special Agent Ajent Jones and the show’s production crew.
- Just minutes into the session, explosions erupt, and chaos ensues. The studio walls crumble as a tank barrels into the scene.
- Specialist 5 Tulsa McLean of the Elvi battalion emerges dramatically from the tank, clad in a camouflage poncho and cape. Declaring allegiance to the "Holy Momma," he announces to a bewildered Tony that the Elvi have come to rescue him in Sarah’s name.
