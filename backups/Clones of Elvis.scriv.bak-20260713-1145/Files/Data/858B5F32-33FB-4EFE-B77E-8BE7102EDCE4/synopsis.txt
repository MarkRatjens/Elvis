### Synopsis

**Location:** A sleek cruiser transitioning to an industrial necropsy room within Sunny Contemplations Funeral Home, juxtaposed against shadowy PAPER operations and corrupted ambulance hacks.

Special Agent Ajent Jones, an operative of ominous disposition, stews in fury over his encounter with Sarah, whose confident defiance infuriates him—challenging his deeply ingrained misogyny and his twisted view of the world. His rage centers on a missing object of critical importance: the Gyges Disc, once possessed by Emil Kalheri and presumably sought by Sarah as well. Seeking answers, Jones investigates Emil’s body at the mortuary, observing the gruesome yet methodical dissection process underway. Despite combing through Emil’s effects and reviewing security footage, the disc is absent. Drawing on clues and logical deductions, Jones concludes that the disc must have been taken by corrupt ambulance operatives involved in Emil’s delivery, setting his sights on yet another trail of treachery and intrigue.
