**Locations**: An exclusive Airborne café; Johnny's table.

**Tone and Voice Sentence**: "Johnny had always dreamed of getting what he deserved, especially richly."

**Synopsis**:
Chilo tracks down a despondent Johnny sulking at a luxurious Airborne café following his failed Gyges disc experiment. Introducing himself as a "Prophecy Facilitator," Chilo approaches Johnny with a mix of mystical charisma and wry condescension, confronting him about the recent disaster at the Shakti Research facility. Johnny, initially defensive and alarmed, listens warily as Chilo explains the unstable nature of Gyges disc experiments, likening them to a small black hole and revealing their propensity to generate uncontrollable energy phenomena.

Offering hope, Chilo reveals that a binary configuration of two synchronised discs could stabilize the reaction, creating a cosmic energy exchange likened to the infinity symbol. He tantalizes Johnny with the possibility of achieving a breakthrough. Finally, to seal the deal, Chilo dramatically produces another Gyges disc from his pocket and slides it across the table like a token of fate. The stunned Johnny is left with the means to continue his experiments, now potentially unlocking the key to harnessing limitless energy—or catastrophic failure.
