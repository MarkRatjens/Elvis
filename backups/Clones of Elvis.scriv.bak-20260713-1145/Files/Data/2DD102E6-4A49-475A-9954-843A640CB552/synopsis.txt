**Locations**: The Chuglinghams' suburban backyard patio; surrounding garden and fire pit; the walk from Sublime Point townhouses.

**Tone and Voice Sentence**: "Your lazy, authoritarian, punitive actions put in train a series of events that led directly to where I am today. Where my family is today."

**Synopsis**:
At the behest of Sarah and under Eloise Shakti’s lingering authority, Chip reluctantly attends a barbecue hosted by the Chuglinghams at their home near Sublime Point. The encounter is fraught with tension, as Chip, now working for Shakti Enterprises, struggles to reconcile his past actions with the Chuglinghams' unexpected hospitality. Bala Chuglingham oversees a chaotic barbecue operation, hilariously mishandling an expensive grill, while Sarah, Hayley, Kathy, and the Sages contribute to a surreal stew of cultural exchange, awkward conversation, and veiled barbs.

As the meal evolves into a shared, albeit uneasy, gathering, Padmala Chuglingham takes subtle, pointed digs at Chip’s history while telling him to relax, exuding an aura of strained civility. Her passive remarks highlight the contrast between the Chuglinghams’ current idyllic living conditions and the trauma of their past experiences with Fridge Force. The Sages add their distinctively archaic flair by reinterpreting the barbecue through the lens of dramatic rituals, both confusing and amusing the other guests.

Later in the evening, after the children are enthralled by stargazing and flames at the fire pit, Bala confronts Chip in private. Bala’s polite façade fractures as he reveals his lingering bitterness, paired with an unexpected gratitude for the inadvertent series of events that led his family to their current haven under Eloise’s protection. The scene ends with Chip visibly unsettled, caught between guilt, self-defense, and a fleeting moment of introspection as the barbecue's laughter and warmth carry on in the background, alienating him further from the group.
