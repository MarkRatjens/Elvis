**Synopsis**

**Locations**: Bombed urban street, riot control cruiser interior.

**Tone and Voice Sentence**: “He obsessed over probabilistic calibration like a chef seasoning a soup.”

**Plot Summary**:
- Alexander arrives at the bomb site aboard a riot control cruiser, observing the demolition and chaos on the ground while internally frustrated by his subordinate role. 
- Constable Sman diligently leverages his “demographic-sensitive culling” expertise, targeting crowds with precision to maintain a statistically equitable tally in his killing spree—although his motives often seep into personal biases. Panic and despair ripple through the street as casualties mount.
- When Sman targets a final victim matching his criteria, Alexander recognizes Tony among the chaos and stumbles upon Sarah's lifeless-seeming body. Mesmerized by her appearance, he hesitates, torn between his duty and personal connection.
- Acting impulsively, Alexander disrupts Sman’s attempt to kill Tony. Exploiting their confrontation, Alexander ensures Tony survives for now, although at a personal and professional cost. His insubordination escalates tensions among those aboard.
