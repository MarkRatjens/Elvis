**Locations**: Back seat of a 1965 Cadillac Fleetwood; streets of the Elvi Quarter; the under-construction concert venue for the Rock Against Revival festival.

**Tone and Voice Sentence**: "Because if we lose this battle for hearts and minds, the Elvi might never recover."

**Synopsis**:
Tony awakens in the back seat of a luxurious Cadillac, disoriented and nursing a throbbing head, only to find himself seated next to The High Elvis. As the car glides through the streets of the Elvi Quarter, Tony observes a shocking transformation—once a haven for rock 'n' roll revelry, the district is now deeply divided, with traditional Elvi establishments sharing uneasy borders with new evangelical centers promoting "Salvation Through Song." The High Elvis reveals that a government-deployed gospel virus has fractured the Elvi community, instigating a schism between adherents of science and evolution and a growing faction of religious converts led by Pastor Rex Humble, a former mediocre impersonator turned fervent preacher.

The High Elvis explains that the upcoming Rock Against Revival festival is a desperate attempt to unify the Elvi through music and counter the rise of superstition. Despite Tony’s protest that he hasn’t written anything new in months, The High Elvis insists on his pivotal role, reminding Tony of the influence his lyrics once wielded across the Elvi.
