**Locations**: Graceland amphitheater, surrounding fairgrounds.

**Tone and Voice Sentence**: "You want to call my son Dick Hitler?"

**Plot Summary**:
- After the Vegas Show's triumphant conclusion, Sarah is snapped from her euphoria and joins Tony, who confronts the Sages with escalating rage. Recognizing them despite their Elvi disguises, Tony physically attacks the trio, tossing Kleoboulos and sending Chilo sprawling.
- Kleoboulos, maintaining his composure, calmly refocuses on Sarah, asserting that recent events confirm the fulfillment of their prophecy. Their bizarre claim insists on Sarah becoming pregnant to ensure the universe's destruction and rebirth into a more "perfect" one.
- Sarah, flustered but resilient, counters their manipulations with sharp wit and biting sarcasm, ridiculing their plan and exposing its absurdity. The Sages' increasingly cryptic and nonsensical responses only deepen the surreal nature of the encounter.
- Chilo oversteps by explaining the baby's role as a herald of the apocalypse, earning rebukes from Kleoboulos and further ridiculing from Sarah, who mockingly names the potential child "Dick Hitler."
- Amid the absurd debate, Solo briefly departs to fetch drinks, during which Chilo discreetly spikes the refreshments with a concoction indicative of ulterior motives, setting up potential consequences for Sarah and Tony.
