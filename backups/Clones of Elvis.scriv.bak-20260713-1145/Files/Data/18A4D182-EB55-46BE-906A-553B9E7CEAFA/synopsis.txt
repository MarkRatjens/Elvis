Synopsis
The Elvi hold their concert on the Domain — the meritocracy's own ground-level turf, not up high — and half a million Cloudborne and Airborne end up on the same lawn. Kathy stays close to Sarah backstage, the one person there who sees the woman under the Holy Momma. Hayley, the outsider cop, takes the whole thing in with a clear eye and reads the trap sitting inside the triumph. Chanta never goes on — he sits it out in the green room, sees Sarah pass through, and keeps his distance. During his last set the High Elvis brings Sarah out as the Holy Momma and calls Chanta up to take a bow for the song; Chanta doesn't appear, and the show moves on without much fuss. Up on stage it's Sarah's first Vegas show all over again and bigger, but it aches with everything that's happened since — and the baby stirs in her. The set finishes on "Religious Minds," the crowd one voice. Dunhilton and Gerry watch it go out live and start bringing the virus forward. It ends on the High Elvis's chant to the Dawkins that nobody catches the irony of — and, in the Ghetto, Humble's own congregation quietly humming "religious minds."
