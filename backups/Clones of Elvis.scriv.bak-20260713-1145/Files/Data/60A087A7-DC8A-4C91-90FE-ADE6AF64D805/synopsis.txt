**Locations**: Memphis Mafia Bar & House of Worship; walk-in cooler; Gospel House pulpit.

**Tone and Voice Sentence**: "The very concept offends the sanctity of our gospel existence!"

**Synopsis**:
Pastor Rex Humble discovers that the Evolution Elvi's counteraction to the TMinus-Virus, the "Great Humpening," has infiltrated even his congregation. Stumbling upon an orgiastic immunity ritual in the walk-in cooler, he reacts with fury, condemning it as a spiritual abomination and rallying his Gospel Elvi against the practice. Declaring faith as their only shield against the virus, Humble forbids his followers from participating in the Evolutionaries' immunity-spreading techniques.

Despite Humble’s fiery opposition, the TMinus-Virus spreads rapidly among the Gospel Elvi, decimating their numbers. As sickness ravages his flock, many defect to the Evolutionaries, reluctantly forsaking their gospel principles to survive. Humble, however, clings to his crumbling ideals, refusing to compromise even as the virus claims him and his remaining loyalists. 

In his final moments, propped weakly in his pulpit, Humble muses on the inscrutable and ironic workings of the universe, his steadfast dogma faltering as he grapples with the possibility that Darwin, not divine providence, holds the key to survival. Alone in his neon-lit Gospel House, Humble succumbs to the plague, his congregation’s dissent and defeat underlining the triumph of evolving pragmatism over inflexible faith.
