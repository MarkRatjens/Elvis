**Locations**: Police briefing room, Agent Jones’ office.

**Tone and Voice Sentence**: "In the name of stability I destabilize. In the name of order I deliver chaos. In the name of life I bring death."

**Plot Summary**:
- During a briefing with the new police commissioner, Gerry Hardman, Agent Jones navigates typical formalities while harboring mounting suspicion toward Gerry, particularly regarding his sharp intellect, suspiciously stoic demeanor, and potential knowledge—or withholding—of crucial elements, like the Gyges Disc.
- These doubts carry over as Agent Jones retreats to his office, channeling his chronic anger through a ritual of therapeutic doodling. Sketching absurd, violent imagery, the act grants him meditative clarity, ultimately crystallizing his unshakable suspicion: “Gerry Hardman is a spy.”
- Unaware that both he and Gerry are PAPER agents due to unnecessary bureaucratic secrecy, Agent Jones’ thoughts spiral into paranoia. His initial scribbles escalate into feverish iterations, blending suspicion with catharsis until he unconsciously concludes with the ominous phrase: “Jihad man.”
- The sequence highlights Agent Jones’ volatility, institutional mismanagement, and disjointed internal structures, setting the stage for further confusion and volatile conflict between agents meant to represent the same agenda.
