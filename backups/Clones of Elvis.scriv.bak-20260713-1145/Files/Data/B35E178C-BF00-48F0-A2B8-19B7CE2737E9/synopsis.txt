**Locations**: Rose garden walkway; Jamison Tower wall; mysterious atrium inside the building.

**Tone and Voice Sentence**: "Tony resorted to his usual, final back-up plan: he did something stupid."

**Synopsis**:
Tony navigates a path to Jamison Tower, guided by cryptic instructions left on Halo's thumb drive. Stumbling upon a holographic rose etched into the building wall, his natural curiosity and growing desperation lead him to test the supposed clues. By instinct and blind persistence, Tony discovers that the rose—an enigmatic part of the structure—acts as a portal. After multiple hesitant attempts, he finally walks through the wall, entering a luxurious atrium filled with greenery and fountains. 

A brief, darkly comic interaction with a drunken, half-clothed bystander escalates into tragedy when the man misguidedly attempts to follow Tony through the portal, meeting a gruesome end as the wall re-solidifies. Tony reluctantly proceeds, his awe and fear heightened as he ventures deeper into the enigmatic tower, beginning to comprehend the peril and gravitas of the mysteries ahead.
