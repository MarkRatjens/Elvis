**Locations**: Johnny's containment bunker and control room.

**Tone and Voice Sentence**: "Full steam ahead, Mr Sulu."

**Synopsis**:
Johnny coordinates a new test on an enigmatic disc using a Shakti-engineered high-speed vertical lathe capable of spinning at over 30,000 RPM. Despite seismic tremors and the growing concerns of his technical team, Johnny, driven by hubris and a reckless sense of authority, pushes the machine well beyond safe operational limits. As the disc emits destabilizing energy, culminating in blue lightning bolts and destructive radiation, the testing chamber is ravaged, and technicians in the observation area are violently killed. The disc's destructive response halts the experiment before reaching its intended speed, leaving Johnny satisfied at having "learned something" about the disc despite the carnage and catastrophic failure.
