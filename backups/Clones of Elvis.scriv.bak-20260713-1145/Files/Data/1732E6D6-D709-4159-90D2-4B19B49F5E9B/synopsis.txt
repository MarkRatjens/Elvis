Tony surprises Sarah by knocking on her townhouse door at Sublime Point. What ensues is an awkward conversation.

Sarah begins by telling Tony he can’t be here, how did he get in? What does he want? Sarah is obviously still defensive about the way Tony treated her in the penthouse, although neither of them bring it up. Both of them are overcoming strong attraction of each other while Tony explains how Gospel Music has divided the Elvi and The High Elvis’ plan for a festival in Airborne grounds to spread the Evolutionary Elvi’s message, as inspired by The Dawkins and Little Clinton. He explains the need for Eloise’s influence to procure the venue and his need to be near Little Clinton so he can write new material. The Elvi have already kidnapped him once and he’s afraid of what the Elvi might do if he cannot oblige them.

“Just a few days, a cpuiple of days,” he pleads.

Sarah, against her better judgment cannot refuse him, and so she reluctantly allows him to stay.
