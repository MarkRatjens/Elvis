### Synopsis:

Tony’s home life is oppressive—his marriage to Andrea is over, marked by physical violence and animosity, while his shame over an earlier public blunder involving a body in the business district looms over him. Andrea, newly matched with a more prestigious partner through some random allocation scheme, mocks him in a pre-recorded message, further deflating Tony's sense of worth. Seeking solace, he heads to the Stickett Inn, where his friends—Major, Halo, and Abe—offer camaraderie, humor, and a distraction in the chaotic social dynamics of the club. 

The tone shifts to one of levity and tension as the group observes Halo's relentless pursuit of fleeting romantic conquests, before ending on the tantalizing note of Halo spotting a woman he believes to be "the one."
