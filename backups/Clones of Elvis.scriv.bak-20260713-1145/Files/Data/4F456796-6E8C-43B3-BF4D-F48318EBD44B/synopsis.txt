**Locations**: Tony's luxurious bedroom; the recording studio's balcony.

**Tone and Voice Sentence**: "Who is Tony Chantara? We want the Great Teacher."

**Synopsis**:
Buoyed from a mixture of Solo's promises and increasing attention from adoring Elvi, Tony exhibits a mix of indulgence and retreat. Surrounded by a group of Elvisettes, some becoming permanent fixtures in his bed and others camping on the balcony or even sneaking in uninvited, Tony grapples with the suffocating adulation that accompanies his status as the “Great Teacher.” Despite the superficial pleasures—affection, whiskey, and bespoke gold-threaded jumpsuits—Tony reflects on his misplaced longing for Sarah and an identity crisis centered on his growing detachment from his followers' idealized vision of him.

Even the adoring Elvisettes’ apparent physical perfection unnerves Tony, reinforcing the psychological weight of his elevated role. A moment of levity arises as he signs autographs for Elvi fans scaling the balcony with ropes, though their dismissal of “Tony Chantara” in deference to the "Great Teacher" further compounds his alienation. Struggling to maintain authenticity, Tony resigns himself to dressing the part, wearing his blindingly gold jumpsuit but rejecting the accompanying poncho, tacitly signaling his resistance to fully submit to the cult of his personality.
