**Locations**: St. Hedges Cathedral, a monumental Airborne landmark.

**Tone and Voice Sentence**: "Sarah Jacobson is the Holy Momma!"

**Synopsis**:
The ostentatious St. Hedges Cathedral hosts the heavily-publicized joint venture wedding of Alexander Shakti and Sarah Jacobson. The ceremony’s grandeur is disrupted by the unexpected arrival of the Elvi, carrying parachutes, filling the nave to capacity, and sparking a clash between young Airborne fans and conservative elites. The Elvi’s presence incites fervent reactions, culminating in widespread chants of "Holy Momma!" as sunlight transforms The High Elvis into a radiant figure. 

Amidst this chaotic reverence, Alexander panics, while Sarah’s mixture of fear and exhilaration betrays her gradual embrace of the Elvi's devotion. Dunhilton grows increasingly alarmed, recognizing Sarah’s emerging role as a unifier of the Elvi and growing in resentment toward her affection for the clones. The ceremony proceeds under the strained decorum of the Very Arch Bishop, who struggles to maintain dignity as The High Elvis occupies his throne, signalling the Elvi's unshakable rise in influence and Sarah’s pivotal position within their reverence.
