**Locations**: Hayley's workspace; virtual data systems; map locations like the Stickett Inn.

**Tone and Voice Sentence**: "Chantara lived nowhere, travelled nowhere, consumed no services legal or elicit, spent nothing, earned nothing, ate nothing, drank nothing, subscribed to nothing, never used a toilet, never had sex, was never healthy nor sick, never spoke, sang, coughed, bled, farted or, as far as the data could report, picked his nose."

**Synopsis**:
Hayley digs into the case of Anthony Chantara (Tony), whose existence is strenuously concealed across all data networks. Encountering invasive barriers to records, she presses through privacy-violating authentication methods and even resorts to illicit data piracy. Analyzing Chantara’s media appearances reveals staged injuries and raises suspicions he may not be real. When cross-referencing locations between the Stickett Inn bombing and the Scarlet Shower Surgeon killings, she identifies a pattern resembling a ring with the Stickett Inn centrally located, intensifying her doubts about official narratives around Chantara and the broader case.
