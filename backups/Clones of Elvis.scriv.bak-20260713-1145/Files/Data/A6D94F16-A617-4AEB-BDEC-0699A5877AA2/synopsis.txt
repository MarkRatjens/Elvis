**Locations**: The Quarter’s Travelator station in the Ghetto; monitored via Eloise’s surveillance.

**Tone and Voice Sentence**: "The key was to give up any expectation of when the Travelator would arrive."

**Synopsis**:
Eloise permits Kathy to undertake Sarah’s peculiar assignment despite assessing it as tangential and indulgent, though she covertly monitors her every move. Kathy travels into the Ghetto, where she meets The High Elvis and is greeted with the reverence of "Aunt Kathy." With a Tulsa Macleans squad in tow, armed and immaculately groomed, the group proceeds to a Travelator station to retrieve an awaited party. 

As they wait, Kathy and the Elvi attempt to navigate the enigmatic "Travelator Uncertainty Principle," which means the vehicle only arrives when it is no longer expected. Hours pass with little success, prompting the Elvi to sing an impromptu tune and their collective enthusiasm to wane. Just as the group decides to abandon their vigil, the Travelator seemingly materializes out of nowhere. From it emerge Kleo, Solo, and Chilo, who greet Kathy with the cryptic assurance, "We’ve been expecting you."
