**Locations**: Ulyssia Police Incorporated Headquarters; Gerry’s corroded corner office; Commissioner’s office.

**Tone and Voice Sentence**: "In a brief flash before anyone present felt compelled to speak, Gerry decided that, now he had met Piotr Dunhilton, CEO of Ulyssia, in the flesh, descriptions of him as a potato face were quite egregious, quite egregious indeed, to potatoes everywhere."

**Plot Summary**:
- Gerald "Gerry" Hardman navigates his mundane but mordantly humiliating day at Ulyssia Police Incorporated, from moving through labyrinthine corporate procedures to reflecting on his corrosion-surrounded office below the Layer. His career in administrative management is tightly engineered by omnipresent PAPER oversight, reinforcing the dystopian hierarchy through veiled threat.
- Summoned abruptly to the Commissioner’s office, Gerry faces blinding morning light and an unnervingly warm reception, leaving him off balance and deeply suspicious about the intentions behind the meeting.
- Inside, he encounters Piotr Dunhilton, the grotesque and pale Chief Executive Officer of Ulyssia, flanked ominously by Agent Jones of PAPER. To Gerry’s shock, Dunhilton announces the "retirement" of the previous Police Commissioner under grisly circumstances and informs him he is the new appointee—a decision laden with thinly-veiled menace and sealed with a handshake he cannot refuse.
- With Agent Jones tasked to "read him in," Gerry’s fate becomes entangled in the broader schemes of Ulyssia’s corporate-political machine, leaving him both in awe of his unexpected promotion and paralyzed by the dangers he knows it entails.
