**Locations**: Sublime Point laboratory facility ("Project Kebab"); Chip’s new townhouse.

**Tone and Voice Sentence**: "There’s no 'just' anything at Project Kebab, Stockwell."

**Synopsis**:
After reluctantly joining Shakti Enterprises, Stasis Anomalies Investigator Chip Stockwell acclimates to his new position within the advanced research initiative, "Project Kebab." Led by Sarah Jacobson and Johnny Stoat, the project focuses on experimental Gyges Disc applications far beyond Fridge Force's modest stasis technology uses. Chip is introduced to the Sages, who approach the technology with an intuitive, almost mystical lens, unsettling his pragmatic engineering mindset. 

Despite his scepticism and unease, Chip is tasked with evaluating and mitigating risks associated with the Gyges Discs, which Sarah explains create localized "micro-pockets of altered time." As Chip integrates into a routine involving the lab and his hyper-secure townhouse, Eloise ensures his compliance through covert surveillance and indirect control, emphasizing the stakes of his role within Shakti Enterprises.
