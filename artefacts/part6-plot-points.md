## 1. The rails — what is already fixed

### The destination

- **The novel's last line is "And that's when Sarah's waters broke."**
  (author 2026-07-30, [[review/ending-decisions-log]] §A). **Several Part 6
  conflict scenes end on this same sentence**, and its final occurrence is
  the novel's closing sentence. The birth itself never happens on the page.
- On that line **the prophecy is fulfilled** — Little Clinton ascends as The One
  After the One; everything after is the extended epilogue. **The Sages are
  correct**; the reconciliation between prophecy and delivery stays off page.
- Calendar (canon, year-free): conception 18 March; Rock Against Revival
  Saturday 10 November at 35 weeks; birth **25 December** at ~41 weeks — "when
  the night is right for it and not one hour before."
- **Sarah's invincibility may fade toward the birth** (TPlus/TMinus equilibrium
  burn-out) so she experiences it "more like a mortal woman — tire, bleed, feel
  it." Not to be asserted as permanent fact ([[arc-tracker/sarahs-biology]]).

### Standing rulings that shape Part 6 content

- **Part 6 braids** (author, 2026-08-08): no stretch of the part presents a
  huge chunk of a single arc. The strand chains in §2 are **ordering
  material**, never chapter shapes — chapters are cut across several strands
  at once. Sharpened 2026-08-10: **chapters pair scenes from different
  character arcs**, as the earlier parts do ("Gee, I Bruise," "Blue Shade
  Blues") — it keeps readers engaged and discourages skipping whole chapters.
- **Eloise's plan is subtle** (author, 2026-08-10): she is NOT pursuing
  direct Gyges-disc weapons applications — **owning the means of
  communication across universes gives her ownership of the other side's
  technology**, weaponry included. When the sock message arrives — **in
  Estonian, needing translation (running gag)** — the room argues and
  **Eloise alone reads it correctly**: beings who wear socks and lose them
  are like us, and therefore relatively safe. Reworks accordingly: "Eloise
  Measures Risk," "Weaponizing the Unknown," "Sarah confronts Eloise"
  (their crude-weapons framing was old-generation). Recorded also in
  [[arc-tracker/eloises-empire]].
- **Part 6 opens with the inauguration** (author, 2026-08-08) — that is where
  the characters are in play for the Humpening to be decreed — **and with
  backstory distributed across early Part 6**, slotted between present-day
  chapters so the two never collide.
- **Part 5 closes on the war drum, not the heist.** The TMinus release
  (Operation Cleanse) stays in Part 5, short, cold, clinical. Part 6 opens into
  the aftermath: the Elvi decapitated and dying, Sublime Point at full strength.
- **The heist and the festival are independent** — author-confirmed; the concert
  is not cover for the heist and they never run simultaneously.
- **The heist block order** ([[arc-tracker/the-gyges-heist]]): Johnny enlists
  Chanta → the heist → Fridge Force learns of it → the Sarah/Chanta reunion.
  The five-disc pentagram firing is also Part 6. The tracker's "opens Part 6
  as a block" is superseded by the 2026-08-08 opening ruling above — the
  chain's internal order stands, but its beats braid like everything else.
- **The Humpening is Sarah's covert strategy** (revised 2026-08-07/08): she
  devises it, deflects credit to Chanta's apparent restorative powers, and her
  originating knowledge stays SEALED. The cure's discovery is **dramatised at
  the inauguration of the new High Elvis** ([[part6-new-high-elvis-chapter]]) —
  survivors as evidence, Chanta's admission as diagnosis, Kathy prescribing the
  method, Walter as the no-interiority witness of the old High Elvis's loss.
  **Refined staging (author, 2026-08-08)**: Sarah **observes** Chanta among
  Elvines who turn out to have survived Bent Street; his remark that they're
  looking lovelier every day, much like his Elvisettes, is what lets her make
  the connection; she then **primes Kathy** to suggest that something about
  Chanta counteracts the TMinus. The orgy starts at the end of the
  inauguration and only afterwards becomes known as the Humpening. Nothing of
  what Sarah knows — the TPlus, her mother, the transfer to Chanta **via the
  first Gyges Disc after the Vegas Show** — surfaces to anyone, **including
  the reader**, until as late as possible (sealed home: [[characters/sarah]]).
- **Melbourne Cup**: one remark only — the release "took about the same time as
  an average Melbourne Cup race, only a week late."
- **The reunion is held for after the heist**; the **confession** — Sarah
  telling Chanta what she is — is still ahead of it and held as long as
  possible. Sealed canon opens only there.
- **Canon fence**: Sarah is NOT a Hardy descendant. The lab, the mother, the
  test case are what there is to find; descent is misdirection.
- **Backstory handling**: distribution across early Part 6 is the author's
  ruling (2026-08-08, above). **"The medicine" and the confession are
  separate scenes in separate chapters, the medicine first (author,
  2026-08-10)** — superseding the earlier plays-inside-the-confession
  proposal: the reader learns Sarah's origin before Chanta gets his three
  flat sentences, both held as late as possible. "A Big Crush" runs
  as unframed chapters, early by design. "How Hardy killed the cure" was
  proposed **late, near the ending, away from Gerry's excavation** — whether
  it joins the early drops or stays the late exception is §6 Q1. Note the
  same-evening evolution: [[first-draft-mining]] §6.3's "narrator tells it in
  five sentences, no scene" has been superseded by the drafted Hardy-POV
  chapter — Hardy is no longer referenced-only. Neither backstory scene may

- **The detonator is on the page**: in "Wrestlemania — The Sages' Night Out"

## 5. Conflict resolutions — Part 5 rules over Part 6

**Principle (author, 2026-08-09): where Part 6 material conflicts with Part 5
or with decreed canon/plan, the Part 5 side rules — most Part 6 prose is
drawing-board material and conforms at redraft.** The resolutions below apply
that principle; they direct redrafting, and **no bundle prose has been changed
by this pass**. Only conflicts whose resolution would create new continuity
problems are promoted to the author — see the end of this section.

Generation map **[mine — assessment from internal evidence]**: **current** —
the four scenes drafted 2026-08-08 (three backstory + "Kathy Figures it Out")
and, mostly, "Wrestlemania," "Alexander's Return," "Hayley investigates Gyges
Disks"; **older** (predating the Part 5 close decisions) — the heist chain,
"The Viral Report," "The Gospel Truth," the Weaponising chain, and the Final
Countdown / confrontations material.

- **R1 — Rock Against Revival's position.** Canon rules: the festival is
  drafted into Part 5 (10 Nov). Every "upcoming concert" interior dies ("The
  Viral Report," the reunion's lend-us-The-Domain pitch). What survives
  unhurt — and strengthens — is the Viral Report's surveillance beat: Sarah,
  Kathy and Hayley entering Elvi Parliament twelve hours before the
  escalation **is** the inauguration visit under the new ordering; Gerry's
  movement timeline (Sublime Point → Parliament → Starborne One) stands.
- **R2 — Two war declarations → a two-step escalation.** The planned order
  ([[arc-tracker/dunhiltons-gambit]]: Viral Report → siege; the Pillow scene
  that used to open it is cut) rules.
  "The Viral Report" is the declaration, on the virus evidence; "Fridge Force
  learns of the heist" converts standing war into the mobilisation order —
  its "pest control, not war" line already reads as escalation, not a fresh
  declaration. "Surrounded by Thursday" stands as the deadline.
