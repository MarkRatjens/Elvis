# Pink Thai Jones (Agent Jones variant — pink-tie)

**Origin:** A [[elvi-and-grayling|Grayling]] clone of the same non-ELVIS line as [[agent-jones|Agent Jones]] — the "NOW WITH NEW PINK TIE!" discount variant, distinguished from the base unit by tie colour. One of a battalion of boxed, largely-unsold units in stasis on the **provisioning level** of [[chanta|Chanta]]'s [[../world-rules/geography|Starborne One]] estate bunker. [[chanta|Chanta]] discovers the boxed battalion while hunting a Harum Scarum unit and dubs the variant "Pink Thai Jones" (pink tie + his own Thai blood — claimed kinship). None are woken in that scene: his "Hey, Dude. You wanna come watch a movie?" is a deadpan joke to a sealed carton, not a revival. (scene: "Tony searches for his favorite clone series")

**Role (the planted asset):** A blank-slate counterpart to the antagonist [[agent-jones|Agent Jones]] — same square head, same caterpillar eyebrows, none of the history. The boxed line is a payoff-in-waiting: in the final countdown [[chanta|Chanta]] orders [[fisher|Fisher]] to revive the entire Pink-Thai-Jones battalion to fight at Sublime Point. Activated under Chanta's ownership, they answer to him rather than to PAPER. (scenes: "Tony searches for his favorite clone series"; final countdown / Sublime Point — to be drafted)

**Knowledge:** None yet — the battalion stays boxed and dormant until the Sublime Point revival. On activation, units wake with the Grayling line's built-in skills (per [[fisher|Fisher]] on blank-clone onboarding), oriented to Chanta as owner.

**Note — tie-colour variants (CANON):** Tie colour marks separate [[agent-jones|Agent Jones]] units (black, red, green, blue, pink named so far). **Pink is not unique to this unit:** the antagonist Jones wears pink during his gospel breakdown ("Ajent Jones sabotages Elvi jukeboxes"), and a pink unit is among the seven Tony activates in "ENCHANTED and the Ajent Jones Clones" ([[stajc]]). How the Pink-Thai-Jones line relates to the other pink appearances (the Ch65 gospel-breakdown Jones; the pink unit among the seven in the "ENCHANTED" note) is unresolved — a question for drafting, not to be pre-decided by notes. See [[agent-jones]] for the prose-grounded tie-colour disentanglement.
