# Chilo (Chilon Damagetus)

> Rebuilt from the **Manuscript draft only**. Part 6 "The Honeymoon is Over," Trash, and Project Notes are excluded per author direction (Jul 2026). Facts tied to the drafted scene that shows them; costume/incidental detail per-scene.

**Identity:** Business card "Chilon Damagetus, Prophecy Facilitator, Seven Sages Corp," tagline "Don't call us—heroes are called" (scene: "Chilo assists Johnny with the research"). "My father's name. It's Greek." Classical Chilon of Sparta — [[solo|Solo]]'s label "Chilo is… Spartan. Militaristic." is the joke made literal (scene: "Solo visits Tony"). (The "Chilo Windus" variant is Trash-only and disregarded.)

**Physical (build consistent, dress varies by scene):**
- Stick-insect / string-bean with a long white "Gandalf" beard, like the other two.
- Signature face: "long beard, crazed skin, deep eyes, a cat's grin that dripped of fang and mischief"; a waiter calls him "a deranged old man" (scene: "Chilo assists Johnny with the research").
- Dress per scene: "army ensemble" / "sweating through army surplus" (scenes: "Going to [[geography|Graceland]]," "Sarah rejects the Sages"); florid suit (scene: "Kathy fetches the Sages"); wizard hat, robe, board shorts, sandals (scene: "The Chuglinghams' barbecue"); trio wizard kit — scuffed medieval boots, robe of arcane symbols and washing instructions, wizard hat, ukulele (scene: "The Sages hijack the stage").

**Voice — two recurring registers:**
- *The blunt blurter* who says the apocalyptic quiet part aloud and gets reproved for it: "To the end of the universe!"; "So a new, perfect universe can bang into place"; "The prophecy demands a child be born"; "It is the end of days!"; "We'd like to name him Richard"; "The others are on hiatus… Pursuing personal projects" (scenes: "Sarah rejects the Sages," "The Sages call on the mansion").
- *The sly technical manipulator*: dumbs Gyges-disc physics down for [[johnny-stoat|Johnny]] — "something like a very very… very small black hole," "It would be more accurate to say it both sucks and blows," binary-system stabilisation; "Fear not, young Stoat. I've come to help you"; then plants a dangerous idea and disclaims it — "But I'm no physicist"; "Accelerating inevitability" (scenes: "Chilo assists Johnny with the research," "The Sage's Guidance").
In the barbecue scene (single scene, not established as a standing register), an anachronistic, bloodthirsty streak surfaces: "In my day you'd get nothing but scraps after the soldiers had eaten… after a long day of collecting abandoned weapons and slaying wounded survivors on the battlefield"; "It's kid, not lamb"; "I know exactly what century it is… The last one" (scene: "The Chuglinghams' barbecue").
Crowd-work drill-calls at Rock Against Revival, named on the page: "Louder!" — rasped; then hand to ear, a chided "I can't hear you!" when the back sings too softly; calls the back section while saluting past the engineering tower — the militaristic tic surfacing as stagecraft (the Spartan label made literal again) (scene: "The Sages hijack the stage").

**Manner — scheming behind a mild surface:** his modesty is a tell, not the truth: "sliding into the gap as though he'd been waiting for it to open. Which… he had"; "This was the sort of statement that sounded modest until you thought about it for more than two seconds" (scene: "The Sage's Guidance"). "circled the display with the practiced nonchalance of a man who'd spent several millennia pretending not to know things he absolutely did know"; "smiled with the sincerity of a used car salesman complimenting a mark's taste in transport"; "delighted at the direction the meeting was taking—checkmate in three moves, and Johnny couldn't see any of them"; his smile reads as "either delighted or… about to eat him raw" (scene: "Gyges disc pentagram configuration"). "Chilo merely smiled" recurs when he's challenged.

**Zeal & scheming:** a fervent prophecy-zealot who wants the child born "by Christmas" so "a new, perfect universe can bang into place," and blurts it against the group's caution (Kleo reproves him). He runs his own agenda outside the trio — "The others don't know I'm here"; gives Johnny his own second Gyges disc ("Here. Have mine."); openly defies Solo — "'This isn't what we discussed,' Solo hissed. Chilo merely smiled." He drives the disc-count escalation ("Or five"). (scenes: "Chilo assists Johnny with the research," "The Sage's Guidance," "Gyges disc pentagram configuration")

**Actions:** executes the drugging of Sarah and Chanta — "'Well done,' Chilo said, quietly dropping potions into their drinks" — and directs Solo to "distract the marks" (scene: "Sarah rejects the Sages"); terrorises Johnny with the disc revelation, then "rescues" him (scene: "Chilo assists Johnny with the research"). Active performer in the Sages' stage hijack at Rock Against Revival: named on the page; initiates the music — starts the chord pattern that opens the Praise the Dawkins number — and shares the section-calling as an active MC, not a backing figure; strums from his end of the stage during Kleo's solo wind-up (no left-of-Kleo positioning on the page; no grin noted) (scene: "The Sages hijack the stage").

**Knowledge:** deep Gyges-disc physics — Hawking radiation, event horizons, accretion, binary-disc stabilisation; possesses a second Gyges disc of his own (scene: "Chilo assists Johnny with the research").

**Dynamics:** subordinate to [[kleo|Kleo]] in the hierarchy (disciplined by him — kicks, "stomp," "see where you've led us?") but operates outside the trio's agreement; the impulsive, scheming one against Solo's caution.
