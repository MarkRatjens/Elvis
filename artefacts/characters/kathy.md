# Kathy (Dr Katherine Logan)

**Voice:** Warm, physically affectionate (kisses [[sarah|Sarah]]'s cheek, hugs, knee-touching). Endearments: "pretty woman," "precious thing," "sugar plum," "Boo Boo," "Holy Momma" (for Sarah — Sarah objects). Malapropisms: "blimp" for "blip." Counts in clinical units: "less than three thousand seconds." Politically sharp: "Some of your best friends are [[social-structure|Cloudborne]], is that it?" Direct moral challenge: "is beneath you." Self-deprecating humour: "Smarter than the average Boo Boo." Deadpan: "Do I look like someone who's ever nauseous?" (scenes: "Kathy visits Sarah," "Sarah and Kathy consider who is the father of Sarah's baby") With strangers: officious, guarded. Dismisses [[legal-framework|TRAMPS]] as "just a different kind of TRAMP." Blows raspberry at the notion [[chanta|Chanta]] is a terrorist: "He's a pussycat." Protective of patients: "I have a patient" as conversation-ender. Uses "under-clone" affectionately for the [[the-elvi|Elvi]]. (scene: "[[hayley|Hayley]] questions Kathy") "Hey, sport" — casual authority with Jones. "I did not know that" — weaponised gentleness with [[alexander|Alexander]]. "In another time, another place..." — regretful. (scene: "[[agent-jones|Agent Jones]] confronts Hayley") "She's not leaving" (matter-of-fact). "That's professional negligence" (scandalised at school report). "That's okay. We can be friends" (with giggle — warm, disarming). (scene: "[[eloise|Eloise]] proposes to Sarah")

**Physical:** Pretty, young, blonde, pigtails. Wears scrubs. Carries a medical bag with breakfast-tray-sized tablet screen. (scene: "Kathy visits Sarah") Siege appearance: tie-dyed lab coat, oversized crocheted tote, flower in her hair — hippy presentation. Hayley's assessment: "a cross between a hippy, a hair model, and a biology professor you'd try not to perv on in a staff meeting." (scene: "Hayley questions Kathy")

**Decisions:** Arrives at mansion on [[nancy|Nancy]]'s summons. Takes Sarah's samples (blood, DNA, urine) while she sleeps. (scenes: "Kathy visits Sarah," "Sarah and Kathy consider who is the father of Sarah's baby") Arrives at the siege by ground car; the Elvi guards step aside and let her through without challenge. (scene: "Hayley questions Kathy") Gatekeeper during Jones confrontation: blocks all parties, then negotiates conditional entry — Alexander and Hayley admitted, no camera crew, Jones excluded. Also blocks Hayley initially ("You either"). Goes upstairs to tend Sarah during the interviews. (scene: "Agent Jones confronts Hayley") Produces DNA report proving Clinton Dawkins is the baby's father (the report is real — the baby's genetic father IS Clinton Richard Dawkins via Sages' foetal implanting). Knows Eloise will soon discover Dawkins is long dead. Completes Eloise's sentence: "Cloudborne!" Fumbles for a patch (her recreational one) to settle Sarah's tears. Reveals baby is a boy and healthy. (scene: "Eloise proposes to Sarah")
