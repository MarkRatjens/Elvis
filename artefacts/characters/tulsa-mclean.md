# Tulsa McLean

**Voice:** Military formality combined with Elvi reverence. "We're here for your protection, ma'am." "Good morning, Mr Tony, sir. I'm Specialist Five Tulsa McLean. In the name of the Holy Momma, we've come to rescue you." Addresses Tony as "Mr Tony" (birth name — military formality, exception to Elvi "[[chanta|Chanta]]" convention). Lopsided grin. (scenes: "A battalion of Tulsas protect the mansion," "[[the-elvi|The Elvi]] break Tony out")

**Physical:** Elvis clone, military. Camouflage poncho with matching cape. (scene: "A battalion of Tulsas protect the mansion")

**Decisions:** Commands the Elvi battalion protecting [[sarah|Sarah]]'s mansion. Notices Sarah's gooseflesh — observant. Looks at her belly: "We're looking after you — and everyone you care about." Led the tank assault on Truth Central to rescue Tony. Emerges from the tank personally. (scenes: "A battalion of Tulsas protect the mansion," "The Elvi break Tony out")

**Relationships:** Protective, respectful toward Sarah. Reverential toward Tony. (scenes: "A battalion of Tulsas protect the mansion," "The Elvi break Tony out")

**Arc:** NEW character, first appearance "Battle hymn of the Elvi." Specialist Five. Introduces Elvi military capability — drill formations, tanks, judo-chop combat. Dual role: protects Sarah at the mansion, leads Tony's rescue from Truth Central. (scenes: "A battalion of Tulsas protect the mansion," "The Elvi break Tony out")
