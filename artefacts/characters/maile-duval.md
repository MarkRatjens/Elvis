# Maile Duval

**Identity:** An Elvine of the Maile Duval series, around forty — she got the burn at fifteen and has had it twenty-five years. One scene so far, on the mezzanine at the inauguration party for the second High Elvis, in a circle of Elvines catching up who have not seen each other in years. She knows [[kathy|Kathy]] on sight and by honorific — "Aunt Kathy! We know" — before Kathy can finish saying she's a doctor.

**The burn (scene: "The Inauguration of the new High Elvis"):** a burn from the wrist to the inside of the elbow and halfway to the armpit. Her boyfriend's car caught fire — a pink and white Cadillac, which she thinks he loved more than he loved her, and she says so without resentment. He had done a show out of town and was driving her home after. About halfway it started smelling hot and she said so; he said it was the road. It was the brakes. Smoke got into the car and she still had to beg him to stop. They stood in the grass and watched it go, and he sat down — no shouting, no running about, no trying to save anything, just gave up and sat with his hands hanging between his knees and tears down his face, because every single thing he owned was in it. She went in for the guitar. The vinyl on a guitar case goes up sudden and hot; she does not remember flames at all, only the heat, and then standing six paces back with her blouse smouldering.

*Where this comes from:* the real 5 June 1955 fire — Elvis leaving a show for Texarkana with a girl from that town, the pink-and-white Cadillac burning about halfway there at Fulton. Reassigned so the night belongs to the girl instead of to him.

**Voice:** She tells the story to a circle that mostly knows it. Bubbly, warm, unbothered — "Look! Almost vanished." She holds the arm out to be admired rather than pitied, and puts it into a doctor's hands "with the satisfaction of a woman who has finally found somebody qualified to agree with her." Goes back into the memory when she reaches the fire and slows down inside it. Entirely without self-pity about the boyfriend, the car or the arm.

**Maile's friend (unnamed):** the Elvine she has had since they were fourteen, and the one who squeals *"What happened to your scar?"* and starts the whole beat. Supplies the parts Maile skips — that it was the brakes, that she went in for the guitar — "as if the ordeal had happened to her," and offers "who'd have thought fabrics made from petrol wools burn" as though it were her own hard-won knowledge. Useful whenever a fact needs to reach the reader without the person it happened to having to claim it.

**What is happening to her (scene: "The Inauguration of the new High Elvis"):** the burn has all but gone. What [[kathy|Kathy]] finds under the flashlight is a little patchy redness like a skin irritation — redder at the wrist and the high end, paler in the middle, "like a word rubbed off a blackboard by somebody in a hurry" — and no ridges anywhere under her palm. Kathy doubts it was ever a serious thing, which is the wrong conclusion and the honest one. Maile cannot date the onset: "One morning I'm frying up breakfast and I think I'm imagining things. But it kept getting better." Nobody in the circle treats it as anything but good news. The mechanism is in [[../world-rules/biology]].

**Relationships:** known to Kathy by reputation and honorific rather than as a patient — the circle knows she is a doctor before she says it. Stands on the mezzanine with her friend since they were fourteen, and with Dianne Carter, who is watching the whole examination.
