# Solo (Solon Athena)

> Rebuilt from the **Manuscript draft only**. Part 6 "The Honeymoon is Over," Trash, and Project Notes are excluded per author direction (Jul 2026). Facts tied to the drafted scene that shows them; costume/incidental detail per-scene.

**Identity:** Business card "Solon Athena, Prophecy Facilitator, Seven Sages Corp," tagline "Don't call us—heroes are called" (scene: "Solo visits Tony"). Classical Solon of Athens — he ties himself to it: "I once scraped my knee during the capture of Salamis" (scene: "The Chuglinghams' barbecue"). Ranks below [[kleo|Kleo]] ("Principal Prophecy Facilitator").

**Physical (build consistent, dress varies by scene):**
- Stick-insect / string-bean with a long white "Gandalf" beard, like the other two.
- Dress per scene: onesie/pyjamas, then a leather jacket and pants (scene: "Going to [[geography|Graceland]]"); "strapped into leather from collar to ankle" (scene: "Sarah rejects the Sages"); "full leathers" (scene: "Solo visits Tony"); florid suit (scene: "Kathy fetches the Sages"); wizard hat, robe, board shorts, sandals (scene: "The Chuglinghams' barbecue"); trio wizard kit — scuffed medieval boots, robe of arcane symbols and washing instructions, wizard hat, ukulele (scene: "The Sages hijack the stage").

**Voice — two registers:**
- *Grand comic-relief declaratives / crowd-pleaser*: "A child will be born!"; "We are the three Sages of the Travelator" (repeated; "He appeared ready to sign autographs"); "We like eggnog" (narrator: "always the one to rescue tragic scenes"); "Indubitably"; "There's too much at stake," "Solo added, for gravitas" (scenes: "Kleo propositions [[sarah|Sarah]]," "Sarah rejects the Sages," "Solo visits Tony"); crowd-pleaser call named on the page at Rock Against Revival: "Let me hear just the front!" — sweeping a pointed finger over the part of the audience whose features he could make out (scene: "The Sages hijack the stage").
- *The anxious diplomat / conciliator*: "'It was a perfectly benign medical procedure,' Solo insisted, all anxious diplomacy" (of the foetal implanting); "Solo wavered"; "Solo looked at the floor"; "Gentlemen, there's wisdom in restraint" (scenes: "The Sages call on the mansion," "Solo visits Tony," "The Sage's Guidance").

**Manner:** in the barbecue scene he's gentle with the injured child — kneels, "pronouncing the boy's name perfectly," offers the Salamis knee anecdote and "the beauty of the birds and sky" (scene: "The Chuglinghams' barbecue"). His "best comforting smile" reads as "grotesque" to Sarah (scene: "Kleo propositions Sarah").

**Role — the operative and the (mild) restraint:**
- The prophecy-pitchman to [[chanta|Tony]]: "You are fundamental to the fruition of the prophecy. You will become a great influencer" (scene: "Solo visits Tony").
- An unwitting operative: distracts Sarah and Chanta so [[chilo|Chilo]] can drug their drinks, fetches the peanut cocktails, then gags on a pineapple chunk (scene: "Sarah rejects the Sages"). Touches Sarah's belly — "She's still with child" / "Solo sighed with relief" — and explains the conception as "a perfectly benign medical procedure… Foetal implanting" (scene: "The Sages call on the mansion").
- Voices caution against Chilo: "What about time? Gravity shifts. Surely time shifts too"; then, privately, "This isn't what we discussed," "Solo hissed" (scene: "The Sage's Guidance").
- Active performer in the Sages' stage hijack at Rock Against Revival: named on the page; punches in the riff eight bars after Chilo's chords and shares the section-calling; strums from his end of the stage during Kleo's solo wind-up (no right-of-Kleo positioning on the page; no autograph-readiness noted) (scene: "The Sages hijack the stage"). At the festival's closing address his presence is collective only — one of the Sages publicly thanked when [[the-high-elvis|The High Elvis]] recasts the hijack as a public service announcement; the ukulele solo teased there was Kleo's, not his (scene: "Religious Minds" [Rock Against Revival]).

**Dynamics:** ranks below Kleo and takes orders from both Kleo (waved to hush) and Chilo ("distract the marks") — often without grasping the plan. The comic front-man for morale and spectacle rather than strategy; the mild counterweight to Chilo's impulsiveness.

> Deliberately excluded (non-draft, per author direction): Solo's confession that he personally performed the implant ("I was the one who used the device upon you"), the "long, pale mask of regret," and his eulogy/grief for Kleo (Project Notes: "Blessed Mother meets Parliament"; Trash: "Kleo in Heaven"), plus the "Dissolution, yes; destruction, no" debate with Chilo (Part 6: "Wrestlemania").
