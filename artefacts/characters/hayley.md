# Hayley Lanning

**Titles (author-confirmed 2026-08-08) — canonical, do not drift.**
- **Police rank: Detective Superintendent.** This is the official title everywhere in the narrator's voice and in Hayley's own mouth. She never introduces herself by any other rank. *(One prose slip corrected 2026-08-08: she self-introduced as "Detective Inspector" in "Eloise proposes to Sarah", contradicting Eloise addressing her as Detective Superintendent later in the same scene.)*
- **Alexander is exempt.** [[alexander|Alexander]] may mangle the title however he likes, and does — "detective… inspector" (guessed wrong after visibly rewriting the script in his head), "Detective Inspector Hayley Lanyards", "Detective Inspector Executive Producer Holy Lanyards", and the bolt-on "Detective Inspector/Executive Producer Hayley Lanning". [[eloise|Eloise]] echoes his construction once, mockingly. **These are jokes, not errors — never correct them.**
- **Role under Eloise: Chief Security Officer — Research and Development.** May be shortened to **Chief Security Officer** or **CSO**. Established formally by Eloise in "Eloise and Sarah negotiate"; the short form is what Eloise uses publicly in Part 5 ("She's the Chief Security Officer here"). Not "Head of Security" — that title belongs to **Jessica Wade**, [[jamison-hardy|Hardy]]'s head of security in [[chanta|Chanta]]'s penthouse staff.
- **Later promotion: Detective Chief Superintendent**, claimed for killing Chanta. Currently recorded only in Project Notes, not yet on the page.

**Voice:** Flat, competent, minimal. Tactical silence as primary weapon. Post-trauma voice preserved in dissociation: "like some nocturnal marsupial." (scene: "Hayley visits [[halo|Halo]]'s apartment") Sarcastic but controlled. Uses "Shakti" for [[alexander|Alexander]] (his surname); emphasises "Lieutenant" with derision. (scene: "Alexander visits Hayley") Self-aware: "Even I'm stupider near him." "As if explaining arithmetic to a pelican with a head injury." (scene: "Hayley calls Alexander") Prosecutorial under pressure: rapid-fire evidence delivery, refuses to let Alexander redirect ("Tax the taxing story arcs, lieutenant, I just want the truth"). Sardonic wit: "At least I visit crime scenes." Socially awkward when flustered by Dr Logan — blathers, burns with embarrassment: "You're not [[the-elvi|Elvi]]" / "I'm — I'm Hayley." Earnest when pleading: "I want to stop these idiots out here from making a grave mistake." (scenes: "Hayley goes to confront Alexander," "Hayley questions [[kathy|Kathy]]") Accuses Jones of murder to his face while at gunpoint. Sardonic deflection of Alexander: "Why mess with your brand? It works." "Squawked" when blocked by Kathy — genuinely surprised. Shares amused glance with Kathy. Finds Kathy "both repelling and attractive" (drug-user assessment). Endures Alexander's name-mangles: Lanyards, Hayley-kins, Hayley-puss, Holly-girl, Helly-Whiskers, Holy Lanyards. Detects evasion during [[chanta|Chanta]] interview: "gritty mote of a lie." (scenes: "[[agent-jones|Agent Jones]] confronts Hayley," "Hayley fits most of the pieces together") At parlour: "I'm Detective Superintendent Hayley Lanning" (correct rank). "I'm not one of the lieutenant's sideshow underlings." "A warlord?" "Forgive me for muddling the difference." "And that's how a loving wife becomes an arms dealer. Happens every day." Blushing when Kathy hugs her — "Ah, I've just met them." (scene: "[[eloise|Eloise]] proposes to [[sarah|Sarah]]")

**Decisions:** Assigned to the [[scarlet-shower-surgeon|Scarlet Shower Surgeon]] serial killings investigation. Receives Alexander's visit at TRAMP Central; does not yield ground on the case despite his attempt to take over. (scene: "Alexander visits Hayley") Investigates over a "fevered weekend" since learning Shakti is taking over her case. Discovers new SSS murders occurred while Tony was incarcerated — proves Tony can't be the killer. Calls Alexander to confront him — gets nowhere. Ground her teeth. "I've consumed enough of your valuable time." (scene: "Hayley calls Alexander") Digs into ENCHANTED Cleaning accounts payable — discovers police crime-scene cleaning contracts. Accesses Shakti-led case files despite CASE CLOSED: SUICIDE warnings. Submits to seven-factor interrogation for access. Cross-references Sarah's hospital admission, employer, and Teh's death date. (scene: "Hayley investigates ENCHANTED Cleaning") Agrees to meet [[johnny-stoat|Johnny Stoat]] after tracing his call. Uses tactical silence and scepticism to draw out his fabricated account. Salvages gold leaf from overpriced cappuccino. (scene: "Johnny meets with Hayley re the Memphis Mafia Bar") Arrives at the mansion siege by ground cab. Overcomes visceral fear response to [[legal-framework|PAPER]] gunships overhead (triggered by her recent narrow escape at Halo's apartment). Confronts Alexander directly with accumulated evidence: fairy boxers found in both apartments, expensive [[social-structure|Airborne]] overnight bag with women's clothes at Chanta's, high-end bandages, witnesses placing Sarah and Chanta together twice the day of the Teh murder, PAPER agent at Teh crime scene. Chases Dr Logan through the gates, introduces herself, and attempts to extract information about Chanta and Sarah. Pleads her case to Dr Logan — appeals to shared interest in stopping Alexander's operation. (scenes: "Hayley goes to confront Alexander," "Hayley questions Kathy") Accuses Jones of PAPER murdering Teh while held at gunpoint. Interviews Chanta in the mansion parlour — first time inside the mansion; detects evasion ("gritty mote of a lie"). (scenes: "Agent Jones confronts Hayley," "Hayley fits most of the pieces together")

**Relationships:** Alexander (antagonistic — sees through his showrunner performance and physical tells). Remembers the [[commissioner|Commissioner]] from his press conference — Alexander was behind him, feeding lines. (scene: "Alexander visits Hayley") Johnny Stoat (new contact — reads him as self-satisfied Airborne bluffer; lets him ramble; suspects most of his heroic account is fabrication). (scene: "Johnny meets with Hayley re the Memphis Mafia Bar") Dr Logan/Kathy (new contact — immediate intrigue, flustered by her appearance, describes her as "a cross between a hippy, a hair model, and a biology professor you'd try not to perv on in a staff meeting"). Extracts key information through conversational gambles but is ultimately refused access to Sarah or Chanta. Shares amused glance with Kathy; finds her "both repelling and attractive" (drug-user assessment). (scenes: "Hayley questions Kathy," "Hayley fits most of the pieces together")

**Key traits:** "A good cop in a deformed universe of bad cops." Analytic trance (deep focus), weary intelligence, unimpressed by authority or performance. Investigative, persistent, frustrated. (scenes: "Alexander visits Hayley," "Hayley calls Alexander")

**Workplace:** TRAMP Central — investigators' pen, windowless, walls the colour of cinnamon latte or crushed cockroaches. (scene: "Alexander visits Hayley")

**Residence:** Apartment perched just above the Layer — an Airborne perk in name only, sunless, overshadowed by towers and hover-copt pads. Rail commuter (can't afford hover-copt on a detective's salary). (scene: "Alexander visits Hayley")

**Halo Assaults Sarah:** Spots Sarah missing from the reception. Picks up Sarah's shoe outside the service elevator. Presses the lift button — doors open to reveal Halo in waiter's uniform. Recognises him instantly as Haloren Smythe, suspected Scarlet Shower Surgeon. Shoves him firmly back into the elevator: "Going somewhere, sport?" This shove sends Halo backward into Sarah, who accidentally stabs him with the Point of Order. Barks "Someone get Kathy" and goes to Sarah's aid. Post-incident: "That was some fighting. Where'd you learn to move like that?" — detective's mind cataloguing the impossibilities. (scene: "Halo Assaults Sarah")

**Eloise takes Sarah to Sublime Point:** Insists on retrieving Chainsaw (devon rex cat) despite Eloise's impatience — 37 minutes of combat with carrier. "Are we free to leave if we wish?" — professional instinct in civilian setting. Immediately stakes out escape routes at [[geography|Sublime Point]]: "I'll work out how we leave. When you're ready." Inadvertently locked out of townhouse with Kathy. (scene: "Eloise takes Sarah to Sublime Point")

**Sarah and Eloise talk:** "There are seventeen settings. Seventeen. For coffee. This is obscene." "You've got to be taxing kidding me" (Chainsaw's defection). "Ah, police officer here!" (drug discussion). Brightens at chef offer — rare enthusiasm: "All I can cook is coffee." Requests computer gear; Eloise refuses retrieval, provides fresh hardware instead. Accepts Kathy's hand on her arm without resistance when restrained from following Sarah. Eloise identifies her as "smitten with Kathy." Cycles through "surprise, embarrassment, consideration, then something that might have been pleased" at Kathy's flirtation. (scene: "Sarah and Eloise talk")

**Pets:** Chainsaw — devon rex cat. "All ears and eyes and indignant superiority." Named for temperament, presumably. Non-negotiable companion. (scene: "Eloise takes Sarah to Sublime Point") Part 4 Ch3: Bypasses Hayley for Eloise after receiving sashimi-grade tuna from crystal dish. "Devotional intensity of a convert who'd found their calling." Presses against glass door with "wounded dignity" when excluded from patio. (scene: "Sarah and Eloise talk")

**Hayley Interviews Chip Again:** Second meeting with [[chip|Chip]] at the Gravity Well restaurant, acting under Eloise's authority. Presents Eloise's case to Chip with "accentuated pleasantry." Interrogates methodically: Chuglinghams → [[travis-teh|Travis Teh]] → Gyges Discs → stasis anomalies → Fridge Force's lack of judicial history → the Chuglinghams alive at the next table. Hubris noted: "tasting as good as fine Antarctic champagne." Produces Chuglinghams as leverage — "persuaded the waiting staff to bring out everything at once." Recruits Chip to Shakti Enterprises. No confidence Eloise's "peaceful applications" claim is true, "but hoped Sarah might make it true." Self-aware about the danger: "This was a dangerous game to play." (scene: "Hayley Interviews Chip Again")

**Alexander gets redirected:** Steers Alexander away from Sarah at the reception venue by feeding his conspiracy instincts. Deploys the fake "Lieutenant" title. Begins "Haloren Smythe is a deep, undercover—" (deliberately unfinished); Alexander fills in his own blanks. Says almost nothing — accomplishes the redirect through strategic silence and one incomplete sentence. Covers her smile when Alexander fails to notice his wife has left. (scene: "Alexander gets redirected")

**Sarah then Eloise arrives:** Openly a couple with Kathy at the festival — snuggling, public hand-holding (caught by young [[walter|Walter]]). Takes the chair nearest the far rail and scans the crowd for trouble even while snuggling — on duty inside her own night off. One flat line from the rail on why Chanta stays backstage: "Rider's better back there" — current on the festival's internal logistics (the backstage rider outclasses the platform's provisions). Gives Sarah a nod the narration rates as, for Hayley, a ticker-tape parade. When Eloise publicly names her "the Chief Security Officer here" — chief of security for the festival itself, given only through Eloise's declaration — leans forward and stares at Eloise: no verbal confirmation, no denial. Seated with the platform party as Aunt Hayley in the Elvi's familial honorific system. (scene: "Sarah then Eloise arrives")

**The Sages hijack the stage:** Tries to mimic Kathy's dancing and mostly manages toe-taps and hand-shakes — restrained physicality intact mid-festival. At the ditty, "Hayley actually laughed" — the narration's "actually" marking the laugh as exceptional for her. (scene: "The Sages hijack the stage")
