# Velvet Elvis

**Decisions:** Administers epipen to Blue Hawaii Elvis with textbook efficiency. Repeats the Elvi's received wisdom that [[elvi-and-grayling|Grayling]] "left all sorts of ticking clocks in the 'product'. Keeps things… interesting." (scene: "Vegas show")

**Note — he is not a source.** This is the *only* appearance of the Grayling-clock notion in the manuscript, and it is bar-talk: an Elvi repeating what Elvi repeat, with nothing behind it. Do not cite Velvet Elvis as establishing Grayling's design or motive. See [[../world-rules/biology]] → "The Grayling clock is apocrypha."
