# Halo

**Voice:** Psycho-killer monotone played for laughs, then not. Deadpan, predatory: "Can't talk. Hunting." "I know the shortcut to a woman's heart." Uses "Stacy bitch" as classification for women. Aesthetic disappointment when kills don't go to plan. (scene: "Tony meets his drinking buddies") Controlled fairy-tale menace: "All the better to see you with, my dear." (scene: "[[sarah|Sarah]] rescues Tony") Narcissistic internal monologue. Thinks in predatory terms — "search-and-destroy," "averages." (scene: "Halo meets [[avi|Avi]]") Clinical, connoisseur-level vocabulary for violence: "imparadising" — treats arterial spray as sacrament. Single-word outbursts when identity is threatened: "[[chanta|Chanta]]?" Deadpan understatement about escalation: "considered it a restorative holiday." Self-aware addiction framing: "The intervals between strikes grew ever shorter, the pleasure of each kill, duller." (scenes: "Halo discovers Tony has been framed," "Halo takes a vacation") At bar watching siege broadcast: "Taxing Shakti!" and "Stay out of it, you excuse of a marsupial!" — maintains "tax" as expletive, uses [[alexander|Alexander]]'s koala-resemblance as insult vector. (scene: "Alexander lays siege to the mansion")

**Decisions:** Pursues women as prey. Fixated on Sarah after seeing her at The Stickett Inn. (scene: "Tony meets his drinking buddies") Attempts empathy tech on Sarah; she breaks rapport through awareness. (scene: "Sarah rescues Tony") Picks up Avi, kills her during sex with his spike ("Point of Order") — his established ritual. When Avi reaches for a gun, he kills her defensively. Trashes her apartment for clues. (scene: "Halo meets Avi") Commits three additional serial kills after the framing broadcast, attempting to force reattribution. When this fails, pivots from serial killing to political assassination — kills the [[commissioner|Commissioner]]. Disposes of body via dog food factory with effigies burned on Prosperity Mountain Church lawns. (scene: "Halo takes a vacation") Planned to bust Chanta out of Truth Central himself — has or can acquire skeleton keys and security passes. Enraged when Elvi beat him to it ("supernova-grade fury"). (scene: "[[the-elvi|The Elvi]] break Tony out")

**Relationships:** Drinking buddy to Chanta, [[major|Major]], [[abe|Abe]] — posing as [[social-structure|Cloudborne]] among them; they are unaware of his true nature. Unnatural success with women. (scene: "Tony meets his drinking buddies") Views Sarah as special target — "she glowed with the kind of vitality Halo ached for." "Admired women who toyed with Cloudborne for sport" — confirms Airborne identity. (scenes: "Sarah rescues Tony," "Halo meets Avi") Chanta is "most valuable asset" — instrumental relationship predating Part 1 events by eight years of befriending and protecting him from "unexplained accidents, poisonings, and sporting pursuits." Needs Chanta free and operational to lead him to Sarah. Views [[dunhilton|Dunhilton]] with contempt ("flat, faintly alien salamander face"). Proprietary rage at the Commissioner for crediting Halo's kills to Chanta. (scenes: "Halo discovers Tony has been framed," "Halo takes a vacation")

**Halo Assaults Sarah:** Assaults Sarah in the amenities corridor using waiter disguise. Drags her to service elevator. Safe-house fantasy: altar with white sheet, candles, mirrors, restraints, cameras — "the perfect altar." Sarah stops fighting, then fights back with unexpected skill. Disarmed of the Point of Order. Takes escalating damage — arm wrenched, face gouged, knee snapped. Draws Point of Order as last resort; Sarah catches his wrist and disarms him. Stabbed accidentally when Hayley shoves him back into Sarah's hand holding the blade. "Perfect O of surprise." Dying, presses panic button and gives Chanta a thumb drive: "It's for you. It's time you knew." "I'm sorry. I can't protect you anymore." Falls limp. Full name: Haloren Smythe (identified by Hayley on sight). Body retrieved by PAPER team disguised as paramedics. (scene: "Halo Assaults Sarah")

**The Reception:** No dialogue — characterised through behaviour. Fondling Point of Order for "peace of mind." Eyes throbbing from empathy-tech withdrawal (switched off to avoid detection). Waiter disguise; waits for chaos. Believes only Alexander, Sarah, and Chanta can recognise him — does NOT account for Hayley (who has his photo from Abe's surveillance). Predatory patience: seizes bar-fight chaos to move on Sarah alone in the toilet. (scene: "The Reception")

**Arc:** Airborne [[legal-framework|PAPER]] agent posing as Cloudborne. Serial sexual killer. Ritual: "a baptism of blood" via carotid puncture at climax using self-made three-edged spike "Point of Order." Avi's death botched (cardiac puncture, no spray), leaving him unsatisfied. Redirects compulsion toward Sarah. Thwarted when Sarah recognises his empathy tech. (scenes: "Tony meets his drinking buddies," "Sarah rescues Tony," "Halo meets Avi") Escalation from ritual sexual killer to political assassin. Diminishing returns on serial killing established. Sarah remains "the magnetic ultimate." Pivots to strategic assassination when the Commissioner's framing of Chanta threatens his access to Sarah. The Commissioner's death creates a PAPER-loyalist vacancy in police leadership. (scenes: "Halo discovers Tony has been framed," "Halo takes a vacation") On "vacation." Deep knowledge of PAPER infrastructure. Pleased by Hardman's commissioning (PAPER plant). Temporary loss of misogyny and killing urge (terrifies others). Fury at Elvi for beating him to Tony's rescue. (scene: "The Elvi break Tony out") Watches Alexander's siege unfold on TV from a remote bar. Full name used: "Haloren Smythe." Identifies PAPER gunship sound (thwack-thwack-thwack) from broadcast audio. Agitated — his unstated "covert op" at the mansion is threatened by both Alexander's siege and PAPER. (scene: "Alexander lays siege to the mansion")
