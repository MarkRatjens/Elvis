# Orion Ortolan (Dr / "Uh-oh")

**Role:** Doctor of comparative mythical physics; chief/senior physics researcher on [[johnny-stoat|Johnny]]'s Gyges project at Shakti Research / Project Kebab, Resonance Cluster, [[geography|Sublime Point]]. Nicknamed "Uh-oh" by colleagues. (scenes: "Johnny tests the discs," "The Sage's Guidance")

**Voice:** Scientific precision overridden by institutional deference. Panicked under pressure: "Wait a minute, sir!" / "It's taxing stuck!" / "We can't have someone inside the bunker tossing the disc while we hit it with lethal radiation." Frowns deepen "past confusion into the territory of a man performing rapid calculations he didn't like the results of." Cautious hedges: "It's an interesting thought." / "With appropriate safety measures." (scenes: "Johnny tests the discs," "The Sage's Guidance")

**Decisions:** Attempts to halt the radiation test when the exit keypad jams; voices the practical objection against tossing the disc during radiation tests. Later defers to Johnny despite disagreement — "because Johnny signed the purchase orders." Looks at [[chip|Chip]]; Chip looks back; neither proceeds; then turns to his workstation — institutional hierarchy beats scientific caution. (scenes: "Johnny tests the discs," "The Sage's Guidance")

**Relationships:** Subordinate to Johnny — defers with "sir" but pushes back on safety. Johnny presents Ortolan's work as his own; Ortolan opens his mouth to clarify "what he had and hadn't confirmed" but is steamrolled — a man "whose presentation had just been hijacked by someone who couldn't spell 'resonance.'" (scenes: "Johnny tests the discs," "The Sage's Guidance")

**First appearance:** Part 3 "Johnny tests the discs" (as chief researcher). Named: Ch29 "The Sage's Guidance."
