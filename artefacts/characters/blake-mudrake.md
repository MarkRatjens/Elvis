# Blake Mudrake

**Voice:** Breathless sycophancy: "Oh, how exciting! A special episode! You heard it here first, folks — a Channel Forty-Eight exclusive." Repeats "Channel Forty-Eight exclusive" as verbal tic. Slightly sharper than he appears — asks the logically devastating question about how [[chanta|Chanta]] ended up on C.Y.I. rather than [[legal-framework|Candid Criminal]]. (scene: "[[alexander|Alexander]] lays siege to the mansion")

**Relationships:** Channel Forty-Eight Sty News journalist. Sycophantic toward Alexander but capable of incisive questioning. Alexander gets his name wrong ("Drake"). (scene: "Alexander lays siege to the mansion")

**Arc (The Monday conversation):** Major progression — from sycophantic establishment journalist to convert. Gets a Sty News camera crew onto the Bent Street occupation; the footage is destroyed ("consigned to a bonfire of wage agreement contracts") because nearly all of it shows [[social-structure|Airborne]] kids having an exceptionally fun time. Later footage catches Mudrake himself "out of his TV-personality mind, playing air guitar, karate kicking and singing, 'Praise the Dawkins, he's the one.'" He quits his job and becomes "a non-for-profit terrorist" — full defection to the occupation; his lawyers' prepared involuntary-drugging defence is abandoned unheard because he no longer wants his old life back. (Channel name appears here as "Channel Sty News"; introduced as "Channel 48" in the siege chapter.) (scene: "The Monday conversation")

**First appearance:** "Alexander lays siege to the mansion"
