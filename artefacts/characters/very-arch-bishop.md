# Very Arch Bishop d'Molesto

**Voice:** Ecclesiastical register with performative gravity: "Dearly invested." Ceremonial shorthand: "I do. I do. Done." Sycophantic toward power: trips over himself explaining the "Holy Joint Venture" terminology to Dunhilton. (scene: "Holy Joint Venture")

**Arc:** Officiates Sarah and Alexander's wedding at St Hedges Cathedral. Invites the Elvi into the ceremony — an act Dunhilton views as sanctifying a rival religion. Vacates his throne when the High Elvis enters, then loses it permanently (High Elvis pinches the seat). Confronted by Dunhilton after the ceremony over the warranty implications. (scene: "Holy Joint Venture")

**Decisions:** Allowed Elvi parachute insertion into the cathedral. Declared the ceremony a "Holy Joint Venture" rather than a standard wedding. Did not resist when the High Elvis took his seat. (scene: "Holy Joint Venture")
