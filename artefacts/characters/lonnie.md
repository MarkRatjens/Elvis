# Lonnie

**Role note:** Lonnie is a *waitress*, not the bar's manager. In "[[the-gospel-contagion|Lonnie discovers the jukebox sabotage]]" she steps up to take command during the gospel-contagion emergency — a waitress rising to a crisis, not someone exercising established authority. Do not recast her as running the Memphis Mafia Bar.

**Voice:** Warm, knowing, lightly mocking. Voice "inked in pathos." [[the-elvi|Elvi]]-brand lopsided smile. Blunt: "Sugar, he's... not much, is he" (about [[chanta|Chanta]]). "You don't have to impress us here, sugar." Apologised for non-Elvi impersonator on stage: "Don't they know it's cultural appropriation?" Uses "sweetie" and "sugar." (scenes: "[[sarah|Sarah]] and Tony take refuge in the [[geography|Memphis Mafia Bar]]," "[[agent-jones|Ajent]] raids the Memphis Mafia Bar")

**Decisions:** Greets Chanta with hug, kiss, grope. Overrides Sarah's cocktail order. (scene: "Sarah and Tony take refuge in the Memphis Mafia Bar") Extracts Sarah during [[legal-framework|PAPER]] raid; punches [[johnny-stoat|Johnny]] unconscious; arranges waiter to fetch Chanta. (scene: "Ajent raids the Memphis Mafia Bar") Pats Sarah's elbow at Graceland; explains Elvi culture. (scene: "Vegas show") Secures lodging by getting innkeeper to renege on a [[social-structure|Cloudborne]] reservation. (scene: "The [[gyges-disc|Gyges Disc]] attacks Sarah")

**Relationships:** Waitress at Memphis Mafia Bar. Fond of Chanta (grope, twinkling eyes). Protective of Sarah — Elvi escort. Dismissive of Chanta's value as Sarah's partner. Hostile to Johnny. (scenes: "Sarah and Tony take refuge in the Memphis Mafia Bar," "Ajent raids the Memphis Mafia Bar")

**Arc:** Sarah's Elvi protector/escort throughout the Ghetto sequence. Growing warmth with Sarah across scenes 18-25. **[PLANNED — close of Part 5]** Her workplace, the [[geography|Memphis Mafia Bar]], is converted into Rex Humble's "New Graceland" — the cost of the gospel arc. She remains a waitress there; the conversion happens to her, she does not own or run the Bar. (decisions-log §G, §J)
