# Agent Jones (Ajent / "Ah-zhant")

**Voice:** Terse, menacing, present-tense declaratives with implied threat: "I am to sign some documents." "I will see the files now." Chews slowly on thought. (scene: "[[sarah|Sarah]] meets Agent Jones") Internal monologue is raw, violent, misogynistic — refers to Sarah as "Miss Cyber-Security Beauty Queen Bitch"; self-justifying violence as "public service." (scene: "I am not human") Escalates to violence without hesitation: "Cut off the pinkies." "Administer Burning Cock." (scene: "Ajent questions [[dick|Dick]] and [[rick|Rick]]") Commands with military authority during raids: "EVERYONE WILL FREEZE!" Becomes schoolboy-like under [[kleo|Kleo]]'s verbal influence. (scene: "Ajent raids the [[geography|Memphis Mafia Bar]]") Part 3: Snaps pencil "like a neck." At mansion: "You will come with me." Calls tanks "little beetles." Extended at gunpoint confrontation: "Little beetles. With tiny parasites crawling inside"; "I have the tactical advantage." Declarative imperatives: "You will come with me." Snaps fingers to summon gunship squadron. Smile described as "flat and joyless"; grinned "like a gremlin" when offered violence. (scene: "Agent Jones confronts [[hayley|Hayley]]")

**Physical:** Square-headed, caterpillar eyebrows. Tie colour (per drafted prose — marks separate units; see Variants): **red** — "Sarah meets Agent Jones" (shimmering, "at odds with his menace"), "Ajent questions Dick and Rick," "Tony & Sarah flee Ajent," "Ajent raids the Memphis Mafia Bar," "Agent Jones confronts Hayley" (siege); **green** — "The Elvi break Tony out" (black suit, glistening green tie); **black** — "Agent Jones sets himself against Gerry," "Agent Jones hones in on Gerry," "Gerry becomes Commissioner of Police"; **pink** — "Ajent Jones sabotages Elvi jukeboxes" (Ch65). No tie colour stated on-page in "I am not human," "Emil in the morgue," or "Halo Assaults Sarah" (masked). (Corrects an earlier note that mis-logged Memphis as black and read the colours as one aesthetic "pattern.") Grinding teeth as stress response. Wrist device used for communicating with gunship squadron. (scene: "Agent Jones confronts Hayley")

**Origin (Part 5 reveal):** Agent Jones is not human — a [[elvi-and-grayling|Grayling]] clone from a non-ELVIS product line, mass-produced and largely unsold. Boxed stock sits in stasis on the **provisioning level** of [[chanta|Chanta]]'s [[../world-rules/geography|Starborne One]] estate bunker, racked in apartment-block-sized stasis lockers among the series stock; every fourth or fifth crate wears a "NOW WITH NEW PINK TIE!" discount starburst — Jones's shifting tie colours reframed as product variants and a failed refresh. Chanta finds the battalion while hunting a Harum Scarum unit and under-reacts entirely — his women-by-vocation taxonomy has no drawer for a man except the one he keeps shut for police, so he files Jones there ("the correct drawer for a narc"). Retroactively literalises the Part 1 interior monologue titled "I am not human": a line that read as metaphor for his cruelty becomes a product spec. Chanta dubs the pink-tie variant "Pink Thai Jones" (pink tie + his own Thai blood — claimed kinship) and lobs it a deadpan "Hey, Dude. You wanna come watch a movie?" — a joke at a still-boxed carton, not a waking; nothing is revived in this scene. The boxed Pink-Thai-Jones battalion is the planted asset, paid off in the final countdown when Chanta orders [[fisher|Fisher]] to revive the whole line to fight at Sublime Point. (scene: "Tony searches for his favorite clone series")
