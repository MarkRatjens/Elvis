# Avi (Grubler)

**Decisions:** Approached by [[halo|Halo]] at the bar (or sent to target him). Led him to her apartment. Reached for a particle pistol hidden under the pillow — intended to kill or capture Halo. (scene: "Halo meets Avi")

**Arc:** Dead. Killed by Halo's spike through the left ventricle. Carried agency-issue weaponry — a professional/operative. (scene: "Halo meets Avi") Full name: Avi Grubler. [[legal-framework|PAPER]] agent with "a taste for detonations." Halo's flashback: "I come so hard when I blow things up." Implied connection to the Stickett Inn bombing. Halo speculates she may have been targeting [[chanta|Chanta]] rather than him. (scene: "Halo takes a vacation")
