# Gerry Hardman (G. Hardman)

## Core

The one operator in the cast who is **not** a grotesque — no vanity, delusion, appetite, or mythology distorting him. Where everyone around him is desperate to be seen ([[dunhilton|Dunhilton]] needs the show, [[alexander|Alexander]] needs the credit, the [[the-elvi|Elvi]] are performers), Gerry's edge is the inverse: he engineers outcomes and accrues power **without appearing to act**. Thesis line — his ascent to control of PAPER, "the most powerful security appointment in Ulyssian history received with the ceremony of a coffee order." His controlled, economical affect is voice-by-design, not absence of voice; reading it as "flat" measures him against the loud cast, which is the wrong yardstick.

**Temperature:** not a serene mastermind above the mess, but not cowed either — the relationship plays as comedy, not thriller. He *tolerates* [[dunhilton|Dunhilton]]: a blustering CEO whose menace is grotesque and played for laughs, humoured by the smarter man who fully expects to outlast him. Wry, patient, faintly mutinous; takes quiet pleasure in working a rival; mouths "Yes, CEO" to a fool while privately running rings around him. A sly operator playing for time, not a leashed functionary.

## Voice — three registers by rank

The register shifts with who outranks whom in the room, and the shift IS the characterisation.

- **Downward, against a rival** ("[[dunhilton|Dunhilton]] & Hardman review the SSS situation"): sly, controlled, quietly sadistic. Dismantles a man while appearing merely helpful — prompts him into self-incrimination ("Why don't you tell the CEO about the Scarlet Shower Surgeon's day job"), answers for him to expose him, drops the quiet knife ("A shame about the previous director, hugging a pillow like he did"), and enjoys it ("allowed the corner of his mouth to curl with a mischievous smile … watched Heston wilt"). Coffee-slurp as punctuation and dominance.
- **Lateral, in cabinet** ("Dunhilton reconvenes the war cabinet"): near-silent. A diplomatic cough to protect the virus secret, one muttered "Psychological warfare," and he walks out running PAPER. The silence is the move.
- **Upward, alone with the CEO** ("Gerry announces he has recovered the virus"): most voiced, most human. Obsequious surface ("Yes, CEO" as performed deference, not felt) over a clocking, mutinous interior ("Ah, there it is, Gerry thought"). Dry needling he can't suppress (the Parramatta runner), and a private literate joke his boss can't read ("And their hair was perfect" — Zevon bait).

## Spine — tolerance and the long game

The unattributability holds **downward and laterally** (Heston never sees the blade; the cabinet sees a coffee-order promotion). **Upward** he performs deference rather than feels it. The dynamic with [[dunhilton|Dunhilton]] is comic power-theatre, not a thriller: Dunhilton holds the title and the bluster — genocide-as-economic-housekeeping, "hugging a pillow," salamander menace — but the bluster is the joke, and the joke is that the man being blustered at is the smarter one, unbothered, already certain he'll be standing when Dunhilton isn't. There is no leash. Gerry humours the performance because outlasting Dunhilton is the plan and the plan is going fine. The discomfort is comedy — biting his tongue at an idiot — not fear; the tells of his real stance are the dry private contempt: the Parramatta needling, the Zevon bait the boss can't catch, the milky coffee in defiance of a man who takes his black.

## Threads & tells

- **Milky coffee** — quiet defiance of Dunhilton, who "never trusted anyone who didn't drink coffee as black as their heart."
- **"Very Hard Man"** — his tablet autocompletes the caller as "Very Hard Man," the same syllabic gag as [[agent-jones|Jones]]'s "G hard man → Jihad Man" doodle. Two PAPER agents who can't see each other, joined by a pun. See [[gerry-vs-jones]].
- **Family in Parramatta** — his one streak of personal principle, and the only thing that cracks the controlled surface.
- **Performed incompetence** — throws the axe with "deliberate incompetence," performs ignorance of the Great Teacher: the grey-man cover of a deep PAPER plant.
