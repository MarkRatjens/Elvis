# Chip Stockwell

**Voice:** Terse, procedural, officiously formal in address ("ma'am," "sir") with sudden blunt menace. Uses world-specific expletive "taxing" / "gravity tax" under stress. Delivers threats flatly without inflation: "You don't want me to glue you to the wall, ma'am." Never explains himself unless forced to by procedure. (scene: "Chip investigates a stasis anomaly")

**Decisions:** Immediately escalates to weapon when a resident hesitates. Collects children as collateral without ethical friction. Prioritises the missing disc over the welfare of everyone in the scene. Charges using the Food Preservation Secrets Act to justify action. (scene: "Chip investigates a stasis anomaly")

**Relationships:** Power dynamic is everything. The Chuglinghams are a problem-set, not people. No warmth, no negotiation, no false courtesy beyond bureaucratic forms of address. Passes Unit 15 and registers the grief inside as ambient noise. (scene: "Chip investigates a stasis anomaly")

**Arc:** Introduction. Baseline: total institutional comfort with casual cruelty. No arc movement yet. The [[travis-teh|Travis Teh]] connection (Unit 15 on the way to Unit 17) positions Chip adjacent to Part 1's central murder without his knowledge. (scene: "Chip investigates a stasis anomaly") Second encounter with [[hayley|Hayley]] — now under duress (explosive patch, Gravity Well restaurant). Forced into admissions about Gyges Discs, stasis anomalies. Visibly terrified when Gyges Discs mentioned. Collapses under Hayley's pressure — agrees to cooperate, recruited to Shakti Enterprises. From bully to bullied. (scene: "Hayley Interviews Chip Again")

**Chip's Integration into Project Kebab:** Operates in covert cataloguing mode throughout — notes exit routes, security checkpoints, CCTV cameras, fire suppression systems. Files [[johnny-stoat|Johnny]] under "manageable." NOT openly aggressive when not in power; operates by subterfuge and institutional authority. Fifteen DECLINED promotions — perfected the art of being underestimated. Arc trajectory: subterfuge methods will eventually fail him; he'll act directly with Project Kebab, but it will be too late. (scenes: "Chip's Integration into Project Kebab," "The Sage's Guidance")

**Voice (Chip's Integration into Project Kebab):** Grumbles — "he did sound something like a bear." Terse procedural observation: "Two exits: the elevator behind him and the lab doors ahead. No stairs. No fire escape visible." Internal monologue catalogues everything: vintage of water cooler water, chair sturdiness, who's looking up and who isn't. Diplomatic resistance: "The Food Preservation Secrets Act prohibits me from discussing specific technical details, but—." Whispers because "whispering felt less treasonous than talking." (scenes: "Chip's Integration into Project Kebab," "The Sage's Guidance")

**Decisions (Chip's Integration into Project Kebab):** Accepts immaculate townhouse but installs independent security system (not connected to [[geography|Sublime Point]]'s network). Splits signing bonus across anonymous accounts for quick escape. Resists [[sarah|Sarah]]'s initial explanation of stasis tech (doesn't need lecturing on his own expertise). Asks Sarah directly: "Are you building a weapon here?" Stays because the data is wrong in a way that confirms his long-held suspicions about [[legal-framework|Fridge Force]] incompleteness. Advises against temporal calibration in the Resonance Cluster — ignored by Johnny. Plans to compose urgent message to Sarah about the danger. Opens his own stasis unit's maintenance panel for the first time. (scenes: "Chip's Integration into Project Kebab," "The Sage's Guidance")

**Relationships (Chip's Integration into Project Kebab):** Views [[eloise|Eloise]] as "just another employer" — confident he can achieve equilibrium covertly. Files Johnny as "manageable" — recognises a superior so busy broadcasting competence he never checks others' frequencies. Respects Sarah's directness — she uses silence as a weapon and reads his file to pressure him; he recognises she's not lying, which is "unusual in an employer and therefore suspicious." Wary of the Sages: "I don't care what Goldilocks and the three fossils think." Frustrated by Chuglingham family's happiness next door. (scenes: "Chip's Integration into Project Kebab," "The Sage's Guidance")

**Knowledge (Chip's Integration into Project Kebab):** Knows stasis works by time, not temperature. Knows stasis fields must never be allowed to resonate with one another. Has read classified Fridge Force reports on temporal resonance events — not supposed to have access to them. Telling anyone would "solve one problem and create seven others, all of which involved prison." Knows Fridge Force has listed him as a "person of interest" in a technological espionage investigation. Now investigating his own stasis unit from the inside. (scenes: "Chip's Integration into Project Kebab," "The Sage's Guidance")

**Voice (Hayley Interviews Chip Again):** Defensive bluster collapsing into fear. "You are the one who tampered with the stasis unit at Shakti's house." Defiant flashes: "It will be a pleasure." Snorts, snarls, scoffs. Miserable surrender: "Go ahead. Just detonate my heart right now." Feet-as-torment running gag: brogues squeezing blood, burning calves, feet stop hurting when terrified. (scene: "Hayley Interviews Chip Again")

**Decisions (Hayley Interviews Chip Again):** Attended the Gravity Well meeting in a bespoke suit against his instincts. Failed to check his surroundings. Blurted confirmations Hayley exploited. Cited Defence, PAPER, and Department of Aged Care as sectors that must never access Gyges Discs. Surrendered: "Go ahead. Just detonate my heart right now." Reluctantly accepts Shakti employment. (scene: "Hayley Interviews Chip Again")

**Part 5 — "The Chuglinghams' barbecue":** Dragged next door by [[sarah|Sarah]]. Padmala needles him about Fridge Force callouts with hostess-perfect timing until Bala warns her off. At the back fence he reaches for absolution — "Look at where you are. Mountains. Real sky. I saved you." — and Bala refuses it: "Not by intent." His excuses, rationalisations and guilt are declined ("What would I do with it? Use it to pay for my children's education?"); all Bala asks is that they be good neighbours. No forgiveness, no punishment — which is worse. (scene: "The Chuglinghams' barbecue")

**Part 5 — "Gyges disc pentagram configuration":** Names the Rotherhithe Stasis Collapse in the room and is dismissed as the professionally paranoid. Resolves not to play constant doom-sayer but to "time his run" and wait for the evidence to mount — then breaks his own strategy within the scene as [[johnny-stoat|Johnny]] bids the configuration up from three discs to five. Exits on "I need to talk with Ms Jacobson"; Johnny's parting shot ("Sarah and I have been the best of intimates for years!") lands in the room unanswered. Not seen again in the drafted Part 5 — the warning is undelivered. (scene: "Gyges disc pentagram configuration")

**Part 5 — "Chip escalates issues with Sarah & Hayley":** Brings the five-disc warning to [[sarah|Sarah]] and [[hayley|Hayley]] in her office, and does it as an accusation rather than an engineer's brief. Contempt for the Sages worked through formal address — names [[chilo|Chilo]] as "Mr Damagetus", asks whether they are a witch's coven. Refuses Sarah's framing of the research outright: "You see patterns. I see weapons development." Names her employer to her face ("Says the employee of an arms dealer") and takes Hayley's silent warning about the room seriously enough to spend the rest of the meeting hunting for the device. Offers no safer configuration and concedes nothing; the slower-speeds idea is Sarah's, not his. Ends by revising his estimate of her upward — "he shouldn't underestimate Sarah". Still to establish in one or two sentences only: he cannot leave the Sublime Point compound, which [[eloise|Eloise]] frames as being for his own good, covering his absence from the rest of Part 5. Full staging in [[../arc-tracker/chip-stockwell]].

**Knowledge:** Recognises the [[gyges-disc|Gyges Disc]] by name on sight from an empty housing inside a stasis unit. Reacts with classified-level alarm. Chokes back the name before blurting it — knows it should not be named in front of civilians. GSTA membership grants access to secrets beyond routine field work. (scene: "Chip investigates a stasis anomaly") Knows stasis anomalies include: memory loss, time travel claims, ground car accidents, earthquakes. Knows the dangers of Gyges Discs falling into Defence, PAPER, or Aged Care hands. Now knows the Chuglinghams are alive and in Eloise's custody. (scene: "Hayley Interviews Chip Again")
