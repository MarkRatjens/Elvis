# Little Clinton

[[sarah|Sarah]]'s unborn son — the household name for the baby during her confinement (scene: "Sarah has nightmares"). A boy, healthy; the DNA report names Clinton Richard Dawkins his genetic father, via the Sages' foetal implanting (scene: "[[eloise|Eloise]] proposes to Sarah"). [[chanta|Chanta]] first glimpses him as an orange glow over Sarah's belly and privately credits him as his creative wellspring (scene: "Let Your Momma Go").

**Relationships:** Worshipped by the Evolution [[the-elvi|Elvi]] — in Chanta's judgement, in ways no kid his age, or even older, should be worshipped; Chanta privately credits him as the wellspring who gave him all his songs, yet the new song arrives without sight of him. (scene: "Chanta's tower")

**[PLANNED — end of book, author 2026-07-30, NOT drafted]:** he fulfils the Sages' prophecy and ascends as The One After the One at the moment Sarah's waters break — the novel's last line — with the consequences carried in an extended epilogue. Rulings and open questions in [[../review/ending-decisions-log]]; not restated here. (Note for later passes: the ascendant is referred to there as "the Dawkins" — that is Little Clinton, not the original Richard Dawkins.)

**Arc:** Seven months along; present at Rock Against Revival on the platform adjoining the sound tower, one floor down, his golden orange aura not visible to Chanta — obscured, Chanta guesses, by the back of Sarah's throne. (scene: "Chanta's tower") During the opening number of the hijacked headline set Sarah feels somehow aided by an energy flowing from Little Clinton through her limbs and out to the crowd — recorded as Sarah's felt sense, not narratorial fact; the mass singalong's expiring refrain is the couplet naming the Holy Momma and her unborn Son, sung by the whole Domain and finally by the Elvi alone. (scene: "The Sages hijack the stage") Publicly framed by [[the-high-elvis|The High Elvis]] as the coming One After the One, arrival set as not tonight — he states he asked, and the Holy Momma confirmed delivery "when the night was right for it and not one hour before." Thirty-five weeks at the premiere: "thirty-five weeks of the One After the One rode out in front of her in oceanic velvet." First on-page foetal movement: turns inside Sarah during the song's accusation — "a slow oceanic roll timed to nothing" — prompting her involuntary hand. (scene: "Religious Minds" [Rock Against Revival])
