# Heinrich

**Voice:** Swiss-German code-switching (*Guten Tag*,
*Heute*, *Mein Gott*, *völlig vollgestopft*,
*Schwarzwälder Schinken*). Panics at logistics, not
at authority. Competent underneath the alarm. Dry
practicality: "The market rate for absurdist corporate
espionage has always been difficult to price."
Brother-in-law joke as closer: "He's been explaining
things he doesn't understand at family dinners for
twenty years." (scene: "The VorpalTech Contingency")

**Decisions:** Accepts VorpalTech contingency activation
despite impossible 24-hour timeline. Improvises
operational cover from available assets: ski chalet
(wrapped as slasher movie set), condemned restaurants,
private bank, cheese factory. Proposes cheese-as-
biological-medium cover story. Suggests malfunctioning
VR headsets from Zurich startup as quantum lens
stand-ins. (scene: "The VorpalTech Contingency")

**Relationships:** [[eloise|Eloise]]'s Swiss-German
contractor for covert operations. Deferential but
pushes back on impossible timelines. Long-standing
retainer relationship — has pre-planned contingencies
on file (VorpalTech job title, business cards).
(scene: "The VorpalTech Contingency")

**Knowledge:** Knows VorpalTech contingency by name
and job title. Knows [[alexander|Alexander]] by
reputation ("somewhat self-absorbed"). Has access to
Alexander's flight information. Knows a VR startup in
Zurich whose malfunctioning headsets can be repurposed.
Has brother-in-law who can explain quantum physics
"convincingly while knowing absolutely nothing about
it." (scene: "The VorpalTech Contingency")

**Arc:** First appearance Ch59. Activated to manage
Alexander's exile in Liechtenstein. Operational
competence under comedy duress.

**Location:** Liechtenstein (Vaduz / Balzers region).
