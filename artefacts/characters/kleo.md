# Kleo (Kleoboulos Windus)

> Rebuilt from the **Manuscript draft only**. Part 6 "The Honeymoon is Over," Trash, and Project Notes are excluded per author direction (Jul 2026). Every fact is tied to the drafted scene that shows it; costume/incidental detail is per-scene, not a fixed trait.

**Identity:** Kleoboulos Windus; business card "Principal Prophecy Facilitator, Seven Sages Corp," tagline "Don't call us—heroes are called" (scene: "Kleo propositions [[sarah|Sarah]]"). The classical Seven Sages of Greece, now a small prophecy-facilitation firm — only three of the seven still active ("on hiatus" / "artistic differences"). Its standing on the page: "It's been a quiet few decades—generations, to be truthful" (scene: "Kleo propositions Sarah"); "The glory days of the Seven Sages were apparently over" (scene: "The Sages call on the mansion").

**Physical (build consistent, dress varies by scene):**
- Stick-insect / string-bean, long white "Gandalf" beard, "light as wicker"; "bearded Jack-O-Lantern" (scenes: "Kleo propositions Sarah," "Sarah rejects the Sages").
- Ancient / apparition-like: "grandad flesh and ancient poltergeist"; he explains it himself — "What you sense as a quality of otherworldliness is a product of my weariness" (scene: "Kleo visits Sarah"). Deep-time marker: "practicing beneficent smiles… since [[jamison-hardy|Jamison Hardy]] was a boy" (scene: "Ajent raids the [[geography|Memphis Mafia Bar]]"). Has tinnitus; the others debate whether his beard looks "distinguished or simply homeless" (scene: "Gyges disc pentagram configuration"). Trio staging at Rock Against Revival: revealed already flanking [[the-high-elvis|The High Elvis]] when centre-front stage relights after the opening number — scuffed medieval boots, robes depicting arcane symbols and washing instructions, tapered beards older than anyone in the audience, wizard hats (one with a pompom — not assigned to an individual), three ukuleles; three expressions — patience, compassion, amusement — likewise unassigned. Foreknowledge is the entrance's uncanny signature: the trio arrives already wired for sound — three wireless mics and three wireless transmitters plugged into the ukes, patched in hours beforehand, and the bewildered stage manager cannot say when or by whom. Footwear note: the entrance gives all three medieval boots, yet Kleo later rocks back on his sandals — the current text carries both, recorded as unsettled (scene: "The Sages hijack the stage").
- Dress per scene: deep-purple child's pyjama onesie with stars, planets and rainbows (scene: "Kleo propositions Sarah"); jumpsuit of many colours + cape (scenes: "Sarah rejects the Sages," "Going to [[geography|Graceland]]"); hooded monk's robe (scene: "Kleo visits Sarah"); pyjamas — "the string-bean garden gnome" to Jones (scene: "Ajent raids the Memphis Mafia Bar"); florid suit + wheeled suitcase (scene: "Kathy fetches the Sages"); wizard hat, robe, board shorts, sandals, esky (scene: "The Chuglinghams' barbecue"); trio wizard kit — medieval boots, robe of arcane symbols and washing instructions, wizard hat, ukulele; footwear unsettled on the current page (boots at the entrance, "his sandals" at the solo) (scene: "The Sages hijack the stage").

**Voice:**
- Whisper / raspy register (scene: "Kleo visits Sarah"). Habitual address: "young Sarah," "young Ajent," "young Anthony," "young Bala," "young Mr Stockwell," "young [[chip|Chip]]." Doubled affirmation: "Yes! Yes! You follow!"; "Yes, yes… Enough. I've had my fill of boyish outbursts tonight."
- Elaborate bureaucratic-mystical courtesy: "Excuse me, dear lady"; "May I present my card?"; "this is facilitative oversight. It's our job"; "completely systemic and minimal interventions." Once mangles "fulmination" for fulfilment (scene: "Kleo propositions Sarah").
- Aphoristic riddles: "Feeling invincible is not the same as being invincible"; "There are wheels within wheels, and he will cycle back around many times yet" (scene: "Kleo visits Sarah"). Dry wordplay: "Probably more of a monocycle"; "More extra-virgin, I suppose."
- Pronounces "Agent/Ajent" as "Ah-zhant," which unsettles Jones (scene: "Ajent raids the Memphis Mafia Bar").
- The proposition, verbatim: "Sarah, could we get you pregnant?" (scene: "Kleo propositions Sarah").
- Festival register is called, not whispered — greets half a million with "Good century, everyone!" (the deep-time joke as salutation; the crowd cheers it). The public-service-announcement line — "Just a little public service announcement" — survives on the page but is attributed only to "the middle one," not to Kleo by name; the Sages sing through their own pre-patched wireless mics — there is no hijacked headline microphone (scene: "The Sages hijack the stage").

**Manner — practised, not sincere, benevolence:** his beneficence is shown as a rehearsed act — "practicing beneficent smiles—chortles, guffaws, stares, and even snarls too—since Jamison Hardy was a boy"; "never quite mastered beneficent gloating"; "the product of much-practiced beneficence" (scene: "Ajent raids the Memphis Mafia Bar"), and "his beneficent tone masking deeper calculations" (scene: "Gyges disc pentagram configuration"). In the raid the act works on Agent Jones — "You don't want to kill her. You want to please her. You want to worship her," and "Jones sagged" (scene: "Ajent raids the Memphis Mafia Bar").

**Role:**
- Leader/spokesman; mediator; disciplines [[chilo|Chilo]] ("Chilo, see where you've led us? I need a drink"; "Keep your head down, Chilo. Sometimes I wonder if you forget what century we live in"; a shin-kick and a "stomp") and waves [[solo|Solo]] to hush.
- Sanctions the drugging of Sarah and Chanta with weary reluctance: "Kleo emitted a sigh of last-ditch disappointment. 'So it's come to this, then.'" (scene: "Sarah rejects the Sages"). Later apologises — "I am truly sorry for the way you became pregnant… I wanted, needed your consent. Others thought your enrolment surplus to requirements" — distancing himself from "Others" who overrode him (scene: "Kleo visits Sarah").
- Proposes the name "Clinton" to defuse Elvi tension (scene: "Sarah rejects the Sages").
- Musical lead of the trio at Rock Against Revival, the stage labour now distributed: leads the musical architecture — embroiders arpeggios over Chilo's chord pattern and Solo's riff eight bars after each — and leaps about the stage with the other two like Rumpelstiltskins in red shoes; Chilo and Solo run the crowd-section calls in their own voices while Kleo keeps the teaching, the Elvi rounds and the solo — no longer a front-man with silent backing. After the ditty's third singing, lifts his picking hand the way a conductor asks a room to breathe with him and teaches half a million strangers "a song they already half-knew," soon having them sing on their own. Calls the Elvi-only rounds while the Sages stop playing — the move that turns ditty to hymn. Then takes the ukulele solo: rocks back on his sandals as though a wind had come up off the lawn, lays a flat hand to his beard while the other two strum from their ends of the stage, and plays on — technically extraordinary (hammers and pulls to make a Los Angeles Guitar Academy graduate proud), the audience amused for 32 bars then standing like a congregation asked to admire their vicar's caravan, even the fig trees wanting to be elsewhere — into "the great baffled quiet," "immaculate and unloved," until The High Elvis pulls his plug mid-solo and the band relaunches over him: the first time on the page a Sage's performance is ended by someone else's authority (scene: "The Sages hijack the stage"). The solo's afterlife: it is the opening condition of the festival's closing scene — the emptied quiet it abandoned the crowd in is what The High Elvis builds his silence out of — and in the closing address he publicly thanks and teases it, promising it will live forever in defiance of everything the Dawkins taught about survival of the fittest, recasting the Sages' hijack as a public service announcement (scene: "Religious Minds" [Rock Against Revival]).

**Knowledge (draft):** Claims to know how Sarah became "special" (invincible) and defers revealing it; states her marriage to [[alexander|Alexander]] will be "a public shell only," and that [[chanta|Tony]] is "blameless… innocent as a newborn lamb" of the pregnancy (scene: "Kleo visits Sarah"). See [[sarah]] SEALED CANON.

**Dynamics:** Senior to Solo and Chilo. Mediates between them; the moderating/managing presence.
