# Dunhilton (Piotr)

## Core

A grotesque played for laughs, not a credible thriller villain. His menace — genocide as economic housekeeping, "hugging a pillow" for out-of-favour lackeys, the salamander face, the crocodile and Gaboon-viper smiles — is real cruelty rendered as deadpan comedy; the bluster is the joke. And the joke is often on him. He holds [[gerry|Gerry]] in open contempt (files him under "Very Hard Man" in his tablet's contempt hierarchy) and keeps handing him power — acting Director of Freedom, Director of Elvi Rights, PAPER oversight — blind to the fact that the man he bullies is the smarter one, humouring him and fully expecting to outlast him. Dunhilton is the louder; he is not the one to watch. His power is positional and real (he holds the title, gives the orders, the genocide proceeds) but it is not a hold over Gerry, who tolerates the performance because outlasting it is the plan. Where his dominance genuinely lands is downward — disposable underlings like Heston — and over the machinery of the state, never over the operator quietly running rings around him.

**Model:** Lampoon of Peter Dutton — the humourless, charisma-free Australian hardliner (former police officer; Home Affairs / border / Defence portfolios; led his party to a landslide 2025 election defeat and lost his own seat of Dickson, the first federal Opposition Leader voted out at an election). The satirical method is to take a grimly cruel, utterly mirthless figure and render him as grotesque comedy; the salamander / reptilian face caricatures the "potato"/reptilian mockery Dutton attracted ("Minister of Defence, Border Walls, and Trade" maps the portfolios; "foreign policy disasters left Ulyssia with cement soap" the gaffe-prone record). His lack of humour is the thing being lampooned — which is exactly why the register around him must stay comic and never harden into a credible thriller menace.

**Voice:** Political-address register: "My fellow [[social-structure|Airborne]] Ulyssians." Unctuous, exclusionary — addresses only the Airborne. Says the quiet part loud without irony: "I promised decent people everywhere I would scourge all lazy, undeserving minority groups." "Those who have virtuously inherited, cleverly grifted, or have been righteously awarded their wealth." (scene: "[[halo|Halo]] discovers [[chanta|Tony]] has been framed")

Deflection style: timelines are "commercially confidential"; "you do not negotiate with a picnic"; the genuinely dangerous question gets "a long, amphibious moment" and the end of the briefing. (scene: "The Monday conversation")
