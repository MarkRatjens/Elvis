# Johnny Stoat

**Naming convention:** In his own POV narration and to everyone else: "Johnny." The Sages call him "Young Mr Stoat." (author-confirmed)

**Voice:** Anxious, fastidious, health-conscious. Calculates cost of skin peels. "An ELVIS bar? You're joking. Tell me you're joking." Oath register cracks under pressure: "Taxing whore claws!" / "Oh, tax my gambling." Pompous, self-regarding — dialogue longer and more self-referential than other characters. (scenes: "Sarah celebrates her promotion," "Sarah shows Johnny the disc") Snobbish, squeamish about [[social-structure|Cloudborne]]: "Did you get a look at the heads out there? It's like a leper's grog den." Self-aware in flashes: "I've handled worse. Actually, that's not true." (scene: "Sarah shows Johnny the disc") In his own POV: narcissistic mirror-talk, baby-talk with his dog contrasting with sadistic dismissiveness toward staff. Shifts between DJ-smooth command voice ("Initiate... thirty revs per minute") and pseudo-technical bluster ("It's just hitting destabilising resonance"). Interior thought addresses his own brain as "magic neocortex." (scenes: "Johnny tests the discs," "Getting kinetic")

**Physical:** Bald, ruddy-skinned, soft-bodied, water-eyed, prodigious butt, pear-shaped. Wears suits; throws away clothes after single wearing. (scenes: "Sarah shows Johnny the disc," "Getting kinetic")

**Decisions:** Refuses to sit on dirty stool; dabs with napkins. Capitulates to Sarah's lead. (scene: "Sarah celebrates her promotion") Examines disc but finds only an ancient drawing. Threatens to toss it. Attempts to pocket it. Places disc where Sarah must reach across him. Pre-drinks expensive Argentinian brandy before coming. (scene: "Sarah shows Johnny the disc") Hides under table during [[legal-framework|PAPER]] raid; grabs [[lonnie|Lonnie]]'s lapels trying to join escape. (scene: "[[agent-jones|Ajent]] raids the [[geography|Memphis Mafia Bar]]") Buys apartment on impulse without due diligence, imagining it will impress Sarah. Pranks techs with fake radiation countdown — one (Saunders) has a heart attack; shows no remorse. Pushes disc test to redline against staff protests, driven by hero complex and car-engine analogy. Gets techs vaporised. Finds satisfaction in the result regardless of casualties. (scenes: "Johnny tests the discs," "Getting kinetic") Revisits Blue Shade bar alone; sits on the stool he previously feared; orders Sarah's drink (Death in a Bathroom). No reflection on dead techs — sociopathic absence. (scene: "[[eloise|Eloise]] fires Johnny")

**Relationships:** Sarah's "awkward acquaintance." He "adored her." Subordinate in their dynamic. (scene: "Sarah celebrates her promotion") Romantically interested — sits too close, attempts hand-holding, tries to grope. Explicitly rejected: "If I'm fucking someone, I promise you'll be the third to know." Attempts to position himself as protector: "I can't protect you if you don't tell me what's going on." Wealthy — funded by an "endowment." (scene: "Sarah shows Johnny the disc") Shakti engineers went over Johnny's head after seeing his homemade apparatus; approval came down to build the proper design while Johnny's contraption was compacted and incinerated. (scene: "Getting kinetic") Despises Voltworthy (neighbour above). Treats all technical staff as "plebeians" and "sycophants." Has a cavadoodle named Levi (Leviathan); previous dog Gigi (Gigantor) killed by falling elephant gantry. (scene: "Johnny tests the discs")

**Arc:** Reluctant companion dragged into the Pale by Sarah. Technical resource but ineffective with the disc. Emotional arc in Ch 10: preening → intellectual excitement → hurt pride → desperate protectiveness. Cowardly under fire (hides during raid). Knocked unconscious by Lonnie. Left behind at the Memphis Mafia Bar. [[kleo|Kleo]] implies he may be "called to conduct great experiments." (scenes: "Sarah celebrates her promotion," "Sarah shows Johnny the disc," "Kleo propositions Sarah," "Ajent raids the Memphis Mafia Bar") First POV scenes in Part 3: progression from prank-cruelty to lethal-cruelty — narcissism + authority = body count. Discovers the disc responds to kinetic force (spinning/throwing) after exhausting all radiation/chemical/magnetic tests. Witnesses disc eruption — fractal pattern etched on observation window. (scenes: "Johnny tests the discs," "Getting kinetic") Fired by Phyllis Gatekeeper via "Contract.fu" — Eloise summons then cancels as misdirection; the real axe comes from HR. Revisits Blue Shade bar seeking Sarah; orders her drink, sits where she sat. "The Blunder of You" plays as ironic counterpoint. (scene: "Eloise fires Johnny") Contacts [[hayley|Hayley Lanning]], offering to suppress her data piracy — claiming power he no longer has (typical Johnny). Arranges meeting at Layer Cake cafe. Fabricates heroic account: claims he confronted the PAPER agent (actually Kleo's words), claims Chantara kidnapped Sarah, positions himself as the brave Airborne man. Truthful elements: Sages' pregnancy approach, [[the-elvi|Elvi]] fighting capability. Chokes when asked about Elvi tanks' source. Inadvertently reveals he visited Sarah ~5 weeks after the bar incident. (scenes: "Johnny contacts Hayley," "Johnny meets with Hayley re the Memphis Mafia Bar")

**The Sage's Guidance:** Credit-stealing in the Resonance Cluster — presents Dr Ortolan's findings as his own; relays Ortolan's notes to technicians "as though he'd composed them himself." "Precisely what I was about to suggest." Walks three paces ahead of everyone as a "leadership technique." Dismisses [[chip|Chip]]: "your expertise in fresh food is noted, but this is cutting-edge physics." Corrects a 2,600-year-old man "with the satisfaction of someone who'd been waiting all morning to correct somebody." Chip could feel the colour leaving his face — Johnny overrides safety objections because he signs the purchase orders. (scene: "The Sage's Guidance")

**Sarah receives Johnny:** Arrives at Shakti Tower expecting to negotiate with Eloise from strength; finds Sarah instead. Smugness cascade: admires shoes, manicured fingernails, contemplates making Eloise beg → all collapses when Sarah is behind the desk. Mental abacus loses rods and sprays beads. "Well above your pay grade" — reverts to entitled dismissal. Pivots to leverage play: reveals second disc and "activate in pairs" theory. Claims calculations are his own (actually [[chilo|Chilo]]'s — "outright lied"). Concedes to Sarah's terms when threatened with permanent silence. Condescends about Sarah's dreams ("Oh, Sarah") then is forced to believe her. Gulps: "I believe you." Accepts role under Project Kebab at salary above Sarah's (currently zero). Tells Sarah the disc's source under pressure — off-page reveal triggers Sarah's fury. (scene: "Sarah receives Johnny")

**Knowledge:** Knows Sarah is being pursued by PAPER. Knows disc shimmers; tablet search found only ancient drawing (possibly Atlantis/Lemuria/government health logo). Does not know about Chanta specifically — saw "an angry-looking guy with a burned face" but didn't connect him to Sarah. (scene: "Sarah shows Johnny the disc") Has the disc — received when Eloise visited the mansion and demanded it; Johnny was present (author-confirmed). Recalls conversation with Sarah at the Memphis Mafia Bar the night PAPER raided. Has learned the disc responds to kinetic energy but is immune to radiation, chemicals, magnetism, and all other static tests. Has witnessed the disc's destructive eruption and the fractal pattern etched on the observation window. Does not understand what the disc is or what the pattern means. (scenes: "Johnny tests the discs," "Getting kinetic") Knows Hayley Lanning is investigating. Knows she traced his call. Has revealed (truthfully) the Sages' pregnancy approach and Elvi fighting capability. Has fabricated his own heroism (using Kleo's words as his own). (scene: "Johnny meets with Hayley re the Memphis Mafia Bar") Depression following firing by Eloise. Sits catatonic over untouched gold-leaf cappuccino at Airborne cafe above the Layer. Still calls Sarah "my girlfriend." Dismisses deaths at Shakti facility: "No one important." Receives second Gyges disc from Chilo plus the binary-system physics (two discs in infinity-pattern create stable energy loop). Now possesses a second disc. (scene: "Chilo assists Johnny with the research")
