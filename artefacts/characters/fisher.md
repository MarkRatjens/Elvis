# Fisher (King Creole Series ELVIS)

**Voice:** Formal, deferential, precisely measured.
Impeccable livery (velvet coat, gold brooch, purple
vest, cravat, white gloves, breeches, buckled shoes).
"You are the one and lawful heir to Jamison Hardy."
"A simple matter. It was only a cheap whisky, sir."
Professional detachment extending to human death.
Occasional lopsided ELVIS smile beneath the formality.
"Not that we've had a corporate execution for some
time." Uses "we" for the estate's operations. "Would
you prefer I performed my duties naked, sir?"
(scenes: "Tony meets Fisher," "Tony meets the
penthouse staff")

**Decisions:** Presents the inheritance as accomplished
fact. Dismisses the old man's death as a cleaning
matter. Accepts Chanta's correction on Elvi
emancipation and revises his mental model in real
time. Immediately acts on Chanta's order to free the
basement staff. (scenes: "Tony meets Fisher," "Tony
meets the penthouse staff")

**Relationships:** Subordinate to Chanta by design —
the Rose Door designates the heir, and Fisher serves.
Pre-emancipation worldview: views Elvi and Cloudborne
as property ("mere chattels"). Shifts when corrected
— "You are indeed the Great Teacher." Manages other
staff with quiet authority (polite cough to redirect
Greg's flirtation). (scenes: "Tony meets Fisher,"
"Tony meets the penthouse staff")

**Arc:** In stasis since Jamison Hardy's death
(centuries). Awakened by Chanta's arrival as heir.
Pre-emancipation conditioning: did not know the Elvi
were freed. Rapid adapter — revises worldview within
the scene. (scenes: "Tony meets Fisher," "Tony meets
the penthouse staff")

**Knowledge:** Knows the Rose Door mechanism (DNA-
activated). Knows the estate's financial position
(a fortune so vast it had become unspendable, sealed since Hardy's death). Knows the staff are in
stasis in the basement. Does NOT know about Elvi
emancipation, modern Cloudborne status, or anything
post-Hardy. Now knows (from Chanta) that the Elvi
are free citizens. (scenes: "Tony meets Fisher,"
"Tony meets the penthouse staff")

**Location:** Starborne One penthouse.
