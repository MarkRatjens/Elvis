# Nancy

**Role:** Housekeeper at the mansion ([[the-elvi|Elvi]] property, [[geography|Graceland]]-modelled).

**Voice:** Deadpan domestic pragmatism. "Oh, it's so good to hear life in the house again." "You know it's called half-and-half because it's the other half of the coffee." "Chewed her disapproval." (scene: "[[sarah|Sarah]] sees a doctor")

**Decisions:** Overrides Sarah's refusal of medical help after four days. Manages household around Sarah's situation. (scene: "Sarah sees a doctor")

**Relationships:** Housekeeper to Sarah. Protective, slightly overbearing. Acts against Sarah's wishes for her own good. (scene: "Sarah sees a doctor") Present in "A battalion of Tulsas protect the mansion" but silent — Sarah "strong-armed Nancy out of her way" rushing downstairs. (scene: "A battalion of Tulsas protect the mansion")
