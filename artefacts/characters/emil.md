# Emil (Kalheri)

**Voice:** Self-deprecating, crude, oddly tender. Marvels at the moon ("it seemed unfairly famous"). "You idiot, Emil. That's the moon." Defiant: "Tax you! You won't get the chance to kill me!" Dark humour: "That, or the tequila's stronger than your insurance." Refuses to wash hands. (scene: "Emil walks into a bar")

**Decisions:** Chose the pub after committing murder rather than delivering the disc. Drinks his account dry. Tosses disc casually, causing bar tremor. Dies before delivery. (scene: "Emil walks into a bar")

**Relationships:** Working for [[agent-jones|Agent Jones]] under a Blank Contract — promised a quick death after delivery. Family: wife Peggy and children, whom he believes will be "so much better off without him." (scene: "Emil walks into a bar")

**Arc:** Complete in Part 1. Dying man (testicular cancer) who signed a Blank Contract. Murdered [[travis-teh|Travis Teh]], retrieved the disc, died of heart attack in the street before completing delivery. (scene: "Emil walks into a bar")
