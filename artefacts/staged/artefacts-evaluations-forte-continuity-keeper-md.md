## Finding 1 — CRITICAL: The TMinus deployment timing contradicts the planned sequence

**What the ledger decrees:**
The PLANNED section of the timeline states:

> **Get (Occupy) Bent Street / TMinusVirus release** — Gerry releases the TMinusVirus (the long-planned MAIN weapon, at last deploying); Elvi start falling seriously ill within hours (serious illness within hours, deaths follow). **Time: after the concert.**

The ledger's drafting-updates note (added 2026-07-23, the Get Bent Street entry) states:
> The cross-class vigil above the Layer that the Rock Against Revival sparks. […] **Pays (planted):** the owed **Humble split-screen** (protest ‖ revival — now on the page). **Advances S203** ("Religious Minds" loose in the world / straight-to-#1 / Dunhilton's-dentist button still owed). **New plants:** (a) the **shareholders'-children shield** — the Airborne young in the crowd are why the authorities dare not clear the plaza, which later pushes Dunhilton toward a *deniable* weapon (→ Operation Cleanse).

**What Operation Cleanse says:**
The scene opens:

> *"The protest above had entered its third day, growing larger with each passing hour."*

If the concert was Saturday night (10 November) and the occupation began that night/Sunday morning, the **third day** is **Monday or Tuesday 12–13 November**. Yet Operation Cleanse deploys the TMinus weapon against the occupation with no mention of:
- "Religious Minds" being number one
- The Monday conversation (the blank scene immediately preceding)
- Any elapsed story events between the concert and this action

This is not merely a pacing issue — it is a **sequence contradiction**. The PLANNED notes and the ledger both indicate that the Dunhilton-toward-deniable-weapon logic is mediated by the shareholders'-children shield established in *Occupy Bent Street*. The ledger specifically flags this as a *plant* that pushes Dunhilton toward Operation Cleanse — meaning *Occupy Bent Street* must be read, and the Monday conversation must resolve, before the deployment.

**The third-day marker also sets a hard problem:** The TMinus virus is the MAIN weapon described in S155 and the PLANNED section as having *already missed its Melbourne Cup Day deadline*. The timeline records:

> **Gerry announces he has recovered the virus** — Gerry reports TMinusVirus recovery to Dunhilton. Melbourne Cup Day deadline set.
> **Gerry devises an alternative to the TMinusVirus** — With the Melbourne Cup Day TMinusVirus deadline missed, Gerry proposes gospel music as an INTERIM divide-and-conquer weapon to weaken the Elvi *while the TMinusVirus (the main weapon) is finished* — not a replacement.

Melbourne Cup Day (the first Tuesday in November in Australia) falls on approximately **5–6 November** in the story's Aeon calendar — just days before the 10 November festival. Operation Cleanse must therefore occur **after** the missed Melbourne Cup deadline, with the virus now recovered, and after the concert reveals the scale of the Elvi threat. The "third day" timing is consistent with that arithmetic **only if** The Monday Conversation scene (currently blank) provides the bridge — Gerry receives the report, Dunhilton reacts to the occupation's growth, and the deployment is ordered. That scene is **missing from the chapter as supplied**.

**Action required:** The continuity is not broken by the third-day marker *per se*, but the blank Monday conversation scene is load-bearing. Without it, Operation Cleanse has no on-page authorisation chain from the concert to the deployment, and the third-day reference has no anchor. This scene must be drafted before Operation Cleanse is revised.

## Finding 2 — CRITICAL: The Operation Cleanse scene contradicts the ledger's planned sequence for the TMinus deployment

**What the ledger (PLANNED) says will happen:**
> **Get (Occupy) Bent Street / TMinusVirus release** — Elvi start falling seriously ill **within hours**, deaths follow. Time: after the concert.
> **Kathy diagnoses + Sarah's hints** — Kathy isolates the virus as the reason the Elvi are sickening and confirms Chanta is the source via blood test; she extrapolates from Sarah's hints.
> **The Humpening** — begins as the Clambake in Parliament House, spreads city-wide; spreads TPlus body-to-body and saves the "Evolution Elvi."
> **The Gospel Truth (Humble's death)** — the steadfast Holy Rollers who refuse the Humpening get no TPlus immunity and die of the TMinusVirus, ending the schism.

**What Operation Cleanse actually stages:**
The scene depicts the virus deployed, Elvi *falling ill and being taken away on stretchers*, Health Department vehicles cordoning off the area, and:

> *"all protesters into segregated screening areas—Elvi showing symptoms in one section, everyone else in another."*

> *"stretchers began removing unconscious Elvi from the scene."*

> *"the last Evolution Elvi performers were being loaded into quarantine transport vehicles."*

This is the **complete** dramatic event — onset, collapse, removal, the occupation cleared — staged within the Operation Cleanse scene itself. There is no survivors-to-treat scenario, no Kathy-diagnoses beat, no Humpening hook, and no Chanta-as-counter-agent beat. The scene resolves the virus deployment as a concluded catastrophe in one self-contained action.

This directly contradicts the planned structure. The PLANNED sequence uses the TMinus deployment as the *trigger* for a multi-scene response arc (Kathy's diagnosis → The Humpening → Gospel Truth). If Operation Cleanse has already shown the Elvi removed and the plaza cleared, there is no remaining story space for:
- Kathy to diagnose the virus and extrapolate the counter-agent
- The Humpening to spread the TPlusVirus body-to-body and save the Elvi
- The Elvi to survive the event at all

**This is the chapter's most serious continuity error.** Operation Cleanse as drafted narratively forecloses the planned closing arc of Part 5. It cannot stand as written if the author intends to proceed to the Humpening.

**Two possible resolutions exist:**
1. **Reframe Operation Cleanse as the deployment scene only** — cut to black after the mist is released, leave the effect to subsequent scenes (this preserves the Kathy/Humpening arc).
2. **Author decision needed: has the Part 5 closing plan changed?** If Operation Cleanse has been consciously rewritten as a compressed resolution (collapsing the Humpening arc), the timeline's PLANNED section and the ledger's S155 and downstream setups (S170, S184+) all require revision. This is not a continuity keeper decision — it requires an explicit author ruling.

## Finding 3 — MODERATE: The TMinusVirus targets "Elvi genetic markers" — this contradicts the virus's earlier characterisation

**What the timeline records:**
S155 (ledger): *"Dunhilton's TMinusVirus (recovered, Elvi-targeted) + a covert anti-'Holy Momma' virus project — the genocide clock; Operation Cleanse is the long-planned main deployment."*

The "Gerry proposes a solution" scene in the timeline establishes: *"Gerry reveals HIBReLabs, TMinusVirus, TPlusVirus; Dunhilton grants total resources."*

The "Gerry announces he has recovered the virus" scene: *"Gerry reports TMinusVirus recovery to Dunhilton."*

**What Operation Cleanse says:**
> *"The virus will only activate in subjects with Elvi genetic markers."*
> *"Unlike traditional pathogens, this engineered virus operated with precision, activating only when it encountered specific genetic sequences."*

The genetic-marker targeting is internally consistent with the Elvi being genetic descendants of Grayling's engineered lines. The ledger's S99/S101 entries confirm: *"Grayling engineered the Elvi / left 'ticking clocks'"* and *"Grayling can encode skills into Elvi DNA (skydiving, acting)."* This is consistent.

> **Superseded quotation (added 2026-08-15).** The S99/S101 wording quoted above is the *old* ledger text and has since been corrected. "Left 'ticking clocks'" is **Elvi apocrypha, not canon** — Grayling's established engineering is the series allergies, and no design, term or motive is established. Do not lift the quoted phrasing from this evaluation. The finding above still stands: it only needs the Elvi to be a distinct genetic line, which they are. See [[../world-rules/biology]] → "The Grayling clock is apocrypha."

However, the scene introduces the designation **"Evolution Elvi"** (*"locations of Evolution Elvi within the crowd"*; *"the last Evolution Elvi performers were being loaded into quarantine transport vehicles"*) as the targeted sub-population. The timeline and ledger use **"Elvi"** or **"Evolution Elvi"** inconsistently throughout — in some places they are the same population, in others they are distinguished (the Holy Rollers are Elvi who converted to gospel and presumably away from Darwinist Elvi identity).

**The specific question:** Does the TMinus virus target *all* Elvi (all who carry Grayling's genetic markers) or only *Evolution Elvi* (those who have maintained Darwinist faith and not converted)? The scene's internal logic suggests it targets the genetic marker regardless of religious affiliation, but the terminology "Evolution Elvi" in the targeting description implies a distinction. This matters downstream because:
- If it targets all Grayling-lineage Elvi, the Holy Rollers are also at risk — the Gospel Truth scene (Humble's congregation dies of TMinus) would be consistent.
- If it targets only "Evolution Elvi," the Holy Rollers are protected, which contradicts the planned Gospel Truth scene.

**The ledger's PLANNED Gospel Truth entry:** *"the steadfast Holy Rollers who refuse the Humpening get no TPlus immunity and die of the TMinusVirus."* This confirms the TMinus targets all Grayling-lineage Elvi, Holy Rollers included. The Operation Cleanse scene's use of "Evolution Elvi" as the target label is therefore **inaccurate against the planned canon** and should be revised to confirm the genetic target is all Elvi, not a sub-group.

## Finding 4 — MODERATE: Gerry Hardman's characterisation in Operation Cleanse conflicts with the established arc

**What the timeline records:**
The ledger entry for "The Elvi break Tony out" establishes: *"Gerry Hardman sworn in as Commissioner of TRAMPS (broadcast event); Halo watches from a bar, identifies Hardman as PAPER plant."*

The ledger entry for "Government emergency meeting" establishes: *"Dunhilton convenes emergency cabinet after Elvi tank assault; Gerry presents, Jones lurks."*

The ledger entry for "Gerry proposes a solution": *"Gerry reveals HIBReLabs, TMinusVirus, TPlusVirus; Dunhilton grants total resources."*

Crucially, the ledger's "Gerry devises an alternative to the TMinusVirus" entry establishes that Gerry *proposed the gospel music gambit as an INTERIM measure* while the TMinus was being finished. This is characterised as a strategic proposal, not passive compliance. Gerry has agency in prior scenes.

**What Operation Cleanse presents:**
> *"He'd spent the previous night reviewing and revising his recommendation to deploy. The original plan had targeted isolated Elvi communities, not a mixed crowd of Airborne, Cloudborne, and Evolution Elvi protesters."*

This internal note — that Gerry had *reservations about the mixed-crowd context* — is the only interiority Operation Cleanse affords him. His subsequent actions show no further hesitation: he confirms deployment, dismisses the accelerated timeline without resistance, and monitors the effect with *"clinical detachment."* The word "Proceed" ends the scene.

The Dialogue Tuner has assessed the voice problem. The Continuity Keeper flags a distinct but related issue: the ledger has planted **S155** as the Operation Cleanse arc and nowhere establishes Gerry as a reluctant participant. The ledger's characterisation of Gerry across multiple scenes is of a *strategically capable PAPER operative* — a man who proposes solutions, reads political situations, and manages upward. The Operation Cleanse scene's attempt to give him a single night of *revising his recommendation* as moral texture, then immediately having him override it without consequence, creates an inconsistency in the arc: it suggests depth that is not paid off and does not match the confident operator the timeline has built.

This is not a continuity error in the technical sense — it is an arc consistency issue. The Continuity Keeper flags it because it will require resolution in the ledger if Gerry's characterisation is to remain coherent.

## Finding 5 — MINOR: "Third day" of the occupation — arithmetic check

*Operation Cleanse* states the protest has *entered its third day*. Checking against the timeline:

- **Night of 10 November (Saturday):** Rock Against Revival ends just before midnight; Airborne ferry crowd to Bent Street Plaza.
- **Sunday 11 November (Day 1):** *Occupy Bent Street* — the entire scene covers Sunday through Sunday evening; ends with the second night beginning ("The second night came on warmer than the first").
- **Monday 12 November (Day 2):** The blank *Monday Conversation* scene.
- **Tuesday 13 November (Day 3):** Operation Cleanse, by the "third day" marker.

This arithmetic is internally consistent. The occupation begins after midnight Saturday/Sunday and Operation Cleanse deploys on its third day (Tuesday morning). No error here, but the blank Monday scene is confirmed as covering Day 2, and its absence leaves a gap the Continuity Keeper has already flagged (Finding 1).

## Finding 6 — MINOR: Dunhilton's Sunday address in *Occupy Bent Street* vs. Operation Cleanse

In *Occupy Bent Street*, Dunhilton's televised address occurs *"a little after five"* on Sunday, and ends with:

> *"Bent Street will be returned to a clean and profitable condition."*
> *"TRAMPS riot squads into the precinct this evening, to maintain and enforce the current perimeter. Not one more layabout will be admitted."*

This is explicitly a **containment** order, not a clearance order. The on-plaza reading confirms this: *"nobody would be cleared and no one else let in — the occupation now had a door policy."*

In *Operation Cleanse*, Dunhilton's phone call on the "third day" opens with:

> *"I'm looking at real-time satellite footage. They're setting up portable shower facilities now. Shower facilities! They're planning to stay indefinitely. I want this handled today."*

This is consistent: the containment failed to deter the occupation, it has grown for two more days, and Dunhilton has now escalated. No contradiction. The two scenes are correctly sequenced in their authority chain.

**However:** In *Occupy Bent Street*, UBC's count of protesters is "as many as ten thousand," Channel Slime News finds "almost thirty thousand," and Sty News reports "in excess of fifty thousand." The Operation Cleanse opening says the occupation "now threatened to exceed thirty thousand." These numbers are from different sources (media counts vary wildly in *Occupy Bent Street*, which is the point) but Operation Cleanse's thirty thousand figure sits at the low end of the inflated Sunday evening estimates. By the third day the growth is plausible, but the thirty thousand figure is slightly deflated relative to the Sunday media landscape. Not an error, but worth noting for consistency of scale.

## Finding 7 — MINOR: The Elvi's First Little Law and Operation Cleanse

*Occupy Bent Street* carefully establishes the Elvi's observance of the Great Teacher's First Little Law and the subsequent scenes build a culture of lyric-only speech (the Elvi answer the press in song; the Elvine at the grill answers in song-titles). *Operation Cleanse* depicts Elvi "leading a science lesson on a small stage, demonstrating principles of natural selection" and "an Elvine performer led the crowd in yet another rendition of their protest anthem." Neither of these is inconsistent with the established Elvi culture, but the science lesson (spoken instruction, presumably) would technically require the Elvi to speak in the Great Teacher's words. This is a minor detail that could be resolved in revision by making the lesson sung or by specifying it is conducted by Cloudborne rather than Elvi. No immediate action required at First Draft.

## Hard craft question

*Occupy Bent Street* ends with the occupation established as a self-sustaining culture — the tarp now reads *"less like a slogan than a forwarding address"*, the rubbish is stacked against an unnamed flood, and the night guard's wave is met with the warmest sound the precinct has ever had aimed at it. *Operation Cleanse* then destroys that community, and its final image is *"their instruments abandoned on the makeshift stage."*

**The question is not whether the destruction is earned — it is whether the story knows who it belongs to.** Operation Cleanse is narrated entirely from Gerry's point of view: clinical, inside-the-operation, ending on his log notation. The community being destroyed — the one the chapter has spent five thousand words building — is visible only from above, through a window, as a surveillance target.

**Is that the right perspective for this scene? Who does the reader need to be inside when the virus lands?**

If the answer is Gerry, the scene needs to earn that choice by making Gerry's interiority load-bearing — his detachment, his calculation, his suppression of whatever he felt the night before must be the dramatic event, and the Elvi's collapse is the cost of that choice. If the answer is *not* Gerry — if this scene is meant to be the chapter's moral catastrophe rather than its strategic procedure — then the perspective is working against the scene, and the revision question is whether the climax should be inside the crowd when the mist arrives, not inside the tower window when it leaves.
