### Vertical stratification & transit (canon consolidation, 2026-07-06)

- **The world is vertical, not horizontal.** [[social-structure|Airborne]] live *above* the Layer (crystalline air, visible stars); **Cloudborne** live *below* it at street level; the **Pale/Ghetto** are the ruined deep-below. There are **no clean streets that "become" the Airborne** — below the Layer it is uniformly toxic and awful; it does not get nicer as you drive toward wealth. Wealth is *up*, not *over*.
- **Towers pierce the Layer.** A tower's base sits at Cloudborne street level; its upper floors rise above the Layer into Airborne air (cf. the Airborne office tower: "216+ storeys above the Layer… lower ground exit to Cloudborne street"). You ascend *within* a tower by **lift / turbo-lift**, or hop tower-to-tower by **hover-copt** (pads sprout from tower flanks; "only a quick hover-copt jump" Airborne↔Cloudborne; collisions plentiful).
- **Ground vehicles stay below the Layer.** A limo/Cadillac cannot drive "up to" an Airborne residence; it delivers to the tower base at street level, and the passenger ascends by lift or hover-copt.
- **The CBD is doubled at the Layer (author ruling 2026-07-24).** Where the towers pierce the Layer, the downtown exists at two levels: the Cloudborne ground-level street plan *and*, "like most of the CBD," an above-the-Layer version of it — the connections among the buildings **part-duplicated, part-redesigned above the Layer**. So a named ground address (e.g. **Bent Street**, below) also has an **above-the-Layer plaza/frontage** of the same buildings. This is the general case; the Domain (ground-level bubble) is the stated exception, where Airborne turf is kept *down* instead.
- **Crossing the Layer is transgressive.** Airborne never willingly descend below it (no Airborne "with sense or self-preservation" enters the Pale); Elvi/Cloudborne rarely ascend, and doing so is dangerous. ([[sarah|Sarah]] is the exception — Airborne who slums the Pale/Ghetto in disguise.)
- **Airborne green space is up top** — elevated platform-lawns and gardens above the Layer, a flaunt of wasted sunlight (St Hedges' lawns; Gravity Well). **Exception: the Domain** — Airborne parkland kept liveable at **ground level** by an anti-climate bubble, NOT by altitude (see below). Airborne green space is usually up; the Domain is the ground-level bubbled one.
- **Domain ↔ Ghetto separation confirmed (new descriptor):** from the ground-level Domain the Ghetto is "a whole city and a Layer and a great deal of **engineered weather** away, down where the towers kept the sun out on principle" — reaffirms the vertical Airborne/Domain-over-Ghetto split; the Ghetto receives Airborne media "second-hand and from above." ("engineered weather" = new descriptor for the between-space climate.) (scene: "Religious Minds" [Rock Against Revival])
