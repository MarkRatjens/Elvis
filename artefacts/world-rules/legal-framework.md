- **The Three Laws (the Ghetto — central establishing event):** at the Rock Against Revival festival the [[the-high-elvis|High Elvis]], quelling the running lyrics-riot, decrees three new laws for the Elvi "to abide by" and for "friends and fans to observe as a mark of solidarity and respect" — framed as "a little more legislation… lay a little law down":
  1. Sing the words of The Great Teacher.
  2. Do not punch up on anyone who does not sing the words of the Great Teacher.
  3. Sing the words of The Great Teacher.
  Extends the recorded Elvi decree power (the High Elvis / first Elvi Parliament of the Ghetto issues decrees — see [[social-structure]]). Closed with his sign-off "thank you very much." (scene: "The three laws") **Re-invoked at the finale (Second Law enforcement gloss):** in his pre-finale recap the High Elvis names the no-punching law "the Second Law" — it "is the hard one; that is why it travels with an escort" (security-enforced); it "applies in the queue," and "especially to anybody singing the wrong words, who is to be pitied, corrected in writing, and left standing" — the prescribed non-violent response to lyric-transgressors (written correction, not karate). (scene: "Religious Minds" [Rock Against Revival])
