## Close of Part 5 — planned virus mechanics (PLAN, not yet drafted)

*Author-set rules governing the unwritten close, propagated from [[../review/part5-close-decisions-log]] (§A–F). Held here, tagged PLAN, so they are not mistaken for on-page canon until the scenes are drafted. This section is the single home for these mechanics; other files reference it rather than restating it.*

- **Speed.** The viral platform acts fast: serious illness within **hours** of exposure, not days (deaths follow). Chanta's overnight TPlus transformation (above) is the established in-world evidence. When [[../characters/gerry|Gerry]] releases TMinus at Get Bent Street, Elvi fall seriously ill within hours; deaths there are blunted because many of the sick go home, but real. The speed is load-bearing — it is why body-to-body spread beats a lab (below).
- **Transmission & head start.** TPlus passes person-to-person by sex/fluid contact. [[../characters/chanta|Chanta]] and the Elvisettes have been passing it for weeks, so a core of Elvi are **already immune** when TMinus lands — most likely the [[../characters/the-high-elvis|High Elvis]], the musicians, and the studio recording crew. That seed is what the Humpening grows from.
- **The panacea guard-rail.** A body-to-body/sexual TPlus dose is **weaker** than the pristine intravenous dose. When a passed-on TPlus meets a TMinus already resident in a body, the two (same platform) "duke it out," reach equilibrium, and eventually **both burn out** — survival, not invincibility. This is why the Humpening carries people through the worst of the attack without becoming a cure-all that renders everyone permanently unkillable.
- **Sarah's fade (Part-6 seed).** If TMinus can wrestle TPlus to a draw, [[../characters/sarah|Sarah]]'s own lifelong invincibility may not be secure. As the birth nears she may weaken — tire, bleed, feel it — and go through the birth more like an ordinary woman. Born from these mechanics; pays in Part 6. **On-page status (Part-5-close festival, "Religious Minds" [Rock Against Revival]):** the fade has **not** begun — at this point Sarah is "still the Holy Momma, still invincible… perfectly all right"; the Evolutionary song lands as an emotional crack only (her hand goes to her belly "before she had been consulted about it"), no physical weakening yet.
- **The knowledge chain.** [[../characters/kathy|Kathy]] is the medical mind: she isolates the virus, blood-tests Chanta ("more alcohol and burger cells than blood, but she isolates the virus"), notes he has never looked healthier, and extrapolates the inoculation from Sarah's guarded hints. She can lab-produce TPlus but far too slowly to outrun a virus that kills in hours — so it must move body to body. Sarah never over-explains; her own TPlus nature stays sealed.
- **Two weapons.** TMinus / Operation Cleanse is the government's long-planned **main** weapon (in train since Part 2), finally deploying; Gerry's gospel-music gambit was the concurrent **interim** softening-up. Neither is a fallback for the other.
