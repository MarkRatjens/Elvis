### [[gyges-disc|Gyges Disc]]

- Roughly twice the width of a thumb; not metal, not leather; stiffly flexible, springs back into shape; "the colour of last year's promises." (scene: "[[emil|Emil]] walks into a bar")
- When tossed in the air, causes violent environmental tremors: bar shook, tequila splashed, lights swung, bottles fell. Closing hand around it quiets it. (scene: "Emil walks into a bar")
- Shimmers with rainbow iridescence when held or touched. (scene: "[[chanta|Tony]] & [[sarah|Sarah]] flee [[agent-jones|Ajent]]")
- Tossing it caused (or coincided with) van flipping with no visible external cause — "as if some angry sky-god had upended the van by hand." (scene: "Tony & Sarah flee Ajent")
- Glows hot when Sarah touches/protects it, as if "roused to life." Caresses her contours against skin. (scene: "Ajent raids the [[geography|Memphis Mafia Bar]]")
- Animate/alive: can melt, fuse to flesh, latch onto skin, solidify. Temperature states: hot when active, cools and solidifies. (scene: "The Gyges Disc attacks Sarah")
- Activated after [[chilo|Chilo]]'s potion took effect. Latched onto Sarah's nipple, attempted fusion — "a carnivorous slug intent on turning her tit into breakfast." Then latched onto Chanta's hand. (scene: "The Gyges Disc attacks Sarah")
- On wall impact, caused structural vibration — wall shook, door rattled, floor wobbled, windows buzzed, dust cascade. (scene: "The Gyges Disc attacks Sarah")
- The Sages recognise it on sight with awe. [[kleo|Kleo]]: "too dangerous in the hands of your masters." (scenes: "Kleo propositions Sarah," "Ajent raids the Memphis Mafia Bar")
- Immune to gamma radiation, chemicals, and magnetism — all standard tests produce no effect. (scene: "[[johnny-stoat|Johnny]] tests the discs")
- Responds to kinetic force — discovered accidentally when a frisbee struck it. (scene: "Johnny tests the discs")
- At low RPM spinning (~30), induces trance in observers — syncopated swaying, involuntary ritual gestures. (scene: "Getting kinetic")
- At high RPM, erupts with furious blue lightning and lethal radiation — vaporises nearby personnel through metal-polycarbonate observation glass. Fractal/recursive pattern etched on observation window: concentric spirals, branching filaments, hints of extra dimensions. (scene: "Getting kinetic")

### [[biology|TMinusVirus]]

- Re-sequenced pararetrovirus with variable specificity, from HIBReLabs. Can target individual DNA or entire phenotype. Distributed by air, water, food. Clones especially vulnerable (shared genotype).
- "Parramatta Virus" is [[dunhilton|Dunhilton]]'s wrong name for TMinusVirus.
- The government's long-planned **main** weapon (Operation Cleanse), in train since Part 2; [[gerry|Gerry]]'s gospel gambit was the concurrent **interim** tactic, not a fallback for it. Deployment, speed of onset, and the TPlus equilibrium limit are PLAN for the close — single home in [[biology]] → "Close of Part 5 — planned virus mechanics."

### TPlusVirus

- Same viral platform as TMinusVirus, loaded with tardigrade DNA, volcanic vent fungi, immortal jellyfish, anaerobic bacteria. Optimises every cell: perfect memory, sight, healing, immunity, denser bones, sharper nerves, extended lifespan. "Functionally invincible."
- Spreads via sex/fluid contact only.
- Tony confirmed infected.
- The "functionally invincible" outcome is the **pristine/intravenous** dose. A body-to-body dose (the sex/fluid route above) is weaker and does not confer invincibility; where it meets a resident TMinus the two reach equilibrium and both burn out. Full mechanics and the close's use of this (the Humpening) live in [[biology]] → "Close of Part 5 — planned virus mechanics."

### Rock Against Revival festival

- **Rock Against Revival Air Guitar:** a festival-branded soft case sold as a collectible "instrument." Contains only stickers, a bag of plectrums, and pumped-in air inflating the case to guitar proportions. An `UNLOCK` button releases the air through **six pipes** (a deluxe edition has **twelve**), sounding either a low, flatulent drop-D power chord or a B♭ minor major 13th "spy chord" for jazz enthusiasts. Comes with a strap; carries a tag reading "thank you very much," allegedly in the hand of the [[the-high-elvis|High Elvis]]. (Its price / derivatives-market treatment is in [[social-structure]].) Invoked later as a crowd appreciation device that can "discharge" — established only negatively ("Not one Air Guitar case discharged" during an unloved solo), mechanism left implicit there. (scenes: "The three laws," "The Sages hijack the stage")
- **Festival amplification tower (half-million-watt PA, centrally patched):** a monolithic sound tower amplifies performers across the entire [[geography|Domain]] lawn — "the half-million-watt sound system aimed at them," whose opening blast turns the crowd into "excited bacteria in a bloodstream within three beats" (the wattage parallels the half-million crowd); an unloved ukulele solo reaches "even the fig trees," who "wanted to be anywhere else." Sound is patched centrally from the engineering tower: the tower engineer calls down to the stage manager to check performers are "wired for sound" and takes "the patch numbers." (scene: "The Sages hijack the stage")
- **Instrument wireless rigs (Sages pre-wired covertly):** the three Sages' ukuleles carry "three wireless mics and three wireless transmitters" patched into the PA in advance — the stage manager cannot say by whom or when ("dunno when, hours ago I'd guess"). Wireless mic-plus-transmitter instrument rigs are standard festival tech; the un-logged install is a Seven Sages minimal-intervention beat (see [[social-structure]]). (scene: "The Sages hijack the stage")
- **Stage-lighting reveals:** "four tight spots flashed to full and produced The High Elvis on centre stage as if they'd teleported him there" — festival lighting used for instantaneous performer reveals (lights-up = performer simply present; no teleport tech). The same device covers the Sages' arrival: lights down between songs, then "the stage lit back up, and everyone saw The High Elvis had unexpected company." (scene: "The Sages hijack the stage")
- **Festival screens (live caption + broadcast):** large screens flank the stage and jumbotrons ring the lawn ("every screen around the lawn" — scene: "The Sages hijack the stage"); the ringed jumbotrons also cycle display slides (used to show the Three Laws — scene: "The three laws"). They "captioned every act all day" (live captioning treated as standard), going "back to black" when there is "no face left worth throwing thirty feet high" (scenes: "The three laws," "Religious Minds" [Rock Against Revival]). They cut to live transmission: at the hijack every screen flashes to live video of Sarah on the VIP platform — "ten metres of her own face" over the lawn, "the Holy Momma delivered at last to every part of the crowd." (scene: "The Sages hijack the stage")
- **Festival live stream (reaches the Ghetto):** the festival is broadcast/streamed live to every Elvi in The Pale and across the wider Ulyssian Meritocracy, plus "a great many of the uncloned." It reaches the [[geography|Ghetto]] only **second-hand and from above** — "bleeding down out of the Domain's stream through somebody's phone" — a top-down Airborne-event-to-below-Layer media flow. (scenes: "The three laws," "Religious Minds" [Rock Against Revival])
