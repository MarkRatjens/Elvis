# Part 6 — New chapter: the inauguration of the new High Elvis

*Author's plan, given 2026-08-08, when deciding where the death of the old High
Elvis gets witnessed. Everything in the body is the author's unless marked
**[mine]**. Provenance rules: [[working-principles]] §12.*

## Why it exists

The Part 5 review asked for "a single Elvi noticing" the High Elvis's death — the
death arrives inside Gerry's POV, where Gerry does not know what the High Elvis
was, so the coldness lands into nothing. Part 5 has no room left: the massacre
scene closes the Part and must stay short and cold. **Decision (author,
2026-08-08): reserve the witnessing for a new chapter in Part 6.**

## What the chapter does

- **The inauguration of the new High Elvis**, riffing on the first inauguration.
- **Walter can be the one who witnesses** the loss — he survives the release (a
  Cloudborne fan, not a genotype target; see [[characters/walter]]).
  **Handling (author, 2026-08-08): the reader sees it over Walter's shoulder —
  no interiority for Walter.** He is a vantage point, not a viewpoint
- **Hayley and Kathy attend.**
- **Enough Elvisettes present, and casual comments from Chanta, to realise there
  is something about their health** — the reader assembles it before it is said.
- **Enough surviving Elvines from the viral cleanse for Chanta to admit he has
  slept with all of them.** **Handling (author, 2026-08-08): Chanta is the last
  to work out what his own admission means** — even Walter is already smirking.
  Consistent with the standing rule that he does not know he is a carrier.
- **Sarah suggests a cure might be propagated.**
- **Kathy recommends the sexual solution.** **Handling (author, 2026-08-08):
  starts as a joke and takes some persuading, but the pushing is not all hers —
  there are others in the room more likely to argue for it, even though Kathy
  enjoys a good dose of promiscuity as much as anyone.** She applies another
  patch; Hayley snuggles in and gently peels it off again.
- **The new High Elvis goes forth to "hump."**

## What it connects to

This dramatises the TPlusVirus spread already logged in [[arc-tracker/tminus-virus]]
— "everyone Tony sleeps with; body-to-body TPlus spread; the panacea guard-rail
holds." The chapter is where that mechanism stops being a mechanism and becomes a
scene: the survivors are the evidence, and Chanta's admission is the diagnosis.

Also pays the Part 6 note already held: a new High Elvis elected and decreed to be
of the same series, character identical — the joke being that it *should* be
possible to replace him, and quietly appalling that it is.
