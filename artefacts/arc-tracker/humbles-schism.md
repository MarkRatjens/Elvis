# Humble's Schism

The personal arc of [[../characters/humble|Pastor Rex Humble]] — the human (well, cloned) face of the gospel plague. The thematic weapon-and-spread arc lives in [[the-gospel-contagion|The Gospel Contagion]]; **this** thread tracks the man the contagion promoted: nobody → prophet → martyr-by-natural-selection. Keep the two in sync; do not duplicate the mechanism beats here.

- **Status:** Provisional — ONE beat kept (his introduction in "The High Elvis kidnaps Tony"); rise, peak and death exist only in junk sketches. Arc **shape** is authorial intent; the prose is unwritten.
- **Tension:** Rise-and-fall — a nobody inflated to a demagogue by a virus, then deflated by the same evolution he preached against. The comic engine is dramatic irony the character never shares; the menace is real while it lasts.
- **Who he is (recast 2026-07-05):** **not an Elvis / not a clone** — a human fan who dressed and styled himself in the King's direction and sang him badly at open-mic nights (past only; **he does not sing now**, least of all Chanta's songs). No real resemblance to Elvis; the effect lands televangelist.
- **Model — all referents OFF PAGE:** the satire targets the TV-evangelist apparatus/mentality, never a named person. Two off-page seeds fuse into the name: **Rex Humbard** (the TV-megachurch template and Humble's *look*) + **Rex Dyson** (the Apostolic preacher who, in fan-lore, baptised the original Elvis). **Rex Humble = Rex (Dyson) + Humb(ard)** — neither named on the page. Full note in the [[../characters/humble|character doc]]. Structural irony to protect: Humble "converting" the Elvi is the clone being reclaimed by the King's own baptism — the contagion is inheritance, not corruption.
