# STAJC — Chanta and the Jones Clones

**STAJC = "Supa Tones and the Jones Clones."** The
ST is Supa Tones Cleaning Services — the original
name of what became ENCHANTED Cleaning Services.
Both company names pun on their proprietor (Supa
Tones → Tony; Enchanted → Chanta), so the modern
rendering of the entity is **"Chanta and the Jones
Clones."**

- **Status:** Identity settled; scene undrafted
  (synopsis fragment only, no prose).
- **Tension:** One of several simultaneous arrivals
  at Sublime Point.
- **Key beats:**
  The synopsis fragment: Chanta (Tony) rides the
  Cadillac through the government checkpoint with
  the Ajent Jones clones. Their Grayling security
  clearances, contractor status and authorisation

  the officer clears them to staging area Alpha.
  Blue Tie's reflection in the rearview shows
  "something that might be anticipation — or

  perimeter, carrying Tony toward whatever reunion
