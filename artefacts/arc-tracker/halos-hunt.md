## Why he is not dead — the on-page evidence

The Part 5 draft plants the survival three times and
never states it:

1. **The body vanishes into PAPER's hands.** A "paramedic"
   team collects him; Chanta's gut (and Hayley's) reads
   the masked one as [[agent-jones|Jones]].
2. **Ch61, "Dunhilton & Hardman review the Scarlet Shower
   Surgeon situation":** Gerry tells the CEO plainly —
   *"Neither I nor, dare I say, Heston can say for certain
   that Smythe is dead. His body is missing."* No delivery
   to any hospital or morgue; the ambulance off-grid for
   hours; the drivers ("Dick van Dyke," "Rick von Reich")
   are known part-time PAPER grunts who have since
   disappeared. The state itself cannot close the file.
3. **Ch62, "Tony watches Halo's vid" — the tell.** Halo's
   recorded sign-off: *"I'll see you when you get there.
   Now that time has stopped for me, I will always be your
   friend."* Read straight, this is sentimental
   ghost-talk. Read against this book's own physics it is
   a **literal statement of stasis** — the technology on
   which the entire novel turns is time stopped in a
   micro-pocket. "I'll see you when you get there"
   ("there" = the address on the card, STARBORNE ONE) is
   an appointment, not a farewell.

Halo directed Chanta to Starborne One, where Hardy's
bunker holds apartment-block-sized stasis lockers on the
provisioning level. Chanta has walked those aisles and
opened perhaps five lockers out of thousands.
