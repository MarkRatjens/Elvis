# Gerry vs Jones

- **Status:** Simmering — Jones working under
  Gerry's orders while convinced he's a traitor
- **Tension:** Contained but pressurised —
  mutual suspicion, no confrontation yet
- **Key beats so far:**
  **Part 2 "Agent Jones sets himself against
  Gerry":** Jones briefs the new Commissioner.
  Gerry asks sharp questions about Chantara's
  charges — Jones stonewalls with "need to know."
  Gerry doesn't push back, which Jones reads as
  suspicious rather than tactical. After the
  meeting, doodling at his desk, Jones surfaces
  from a meditative state to find he has written
  "Gerry Hardman is a spy." Institutional
  stupidity: Jones doesn't know Gerry is a fellow
  PAPER agent. Neither does Gerry know Jones
  knows. "I am not human. I am the biological
  manifestation of the Company's will."
  **Part 4 "Agent Jones hones in on Gerry":**
  At a bar, tonic water, no ice ("ice is an
  adulterous garnish"), burning through napkins.
  Jones maps connections: Gyges Disc → Eloise
  Shakti → the Elvi's music threat → "Jihad Man."
  Writes "G. Hardman / G hard man / Jihad Man"
  in escalating pencil pressure. Concludes Gerry
  is the Elvi's inside man — "Elvian Jihad Man."
  Suspicious of Gerry's rapid rise, the previous
  Commissioner's disappearance, and PAPER's
  effectively unanchored chain of command.
  "Gerry Hardman had all the scent of a double
  agent. Agent Jones could smell double agents,
  even under the Layer."
  **Part 5 "Ajent Jones sabotages Elvi
  jukeboxes":** Jones executes the gospel
  sabotage mission at Gerry's instruction.
  Still convinced Gerry is a traitor — but
  follows orders because the mission aligns
  with his own objectives. Working for the
  enemy while believing him to be the enemy.
