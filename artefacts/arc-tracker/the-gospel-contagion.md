  "Praise the Dawkins" breaks the gospel spell.
  Dr Carpenter grounded, anchored. Kathy's horror:
  if you can swap gospel for Dawkins, you can swap
  Dawkins for anything. High Elvis approves
  deploying it.

  where Humble's second row silently mouths the
  refrain during his own benediction — "the floor
  of his church had begun, very softly, to learn
  the words" (S162 complication; S203). Eloise
  prices the song at number one, every market, by
  Tuesday. The gospel's defeat is now in motion by
  the *same mechanism* that spread it — catchiness,
  belief optional.
