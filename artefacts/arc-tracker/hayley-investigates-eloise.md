## Why this thread matters structurally

This is the decision that fixes Part 5's standing
problem — Sarah reacting rather than deciding.
Crucially it is *delegated* action, which is the
honest version for a woman in a difficult pregnancy
running a project she can no longer personally
supervise: she cannot go and do it, so she commissions
it, and admitting why is the cost she pays. (Her
stage of pregnancy depends on placement: thirty-five
weeks is the festival figure; at any pre-festival
slot she is earlier than that. What she is actually
experiencing at the escalation scene is undecided —
to be established in drafting, not inherited from
the KB; the timeline's symptom claims for the
medical are flagged as unsupported by the prose.)

It also converts [[kathy|Kathy]]'s existing private
decision into a pattern rather than an oddity. Kathy
is already lying to Eloise about the SARA readouts
("I can lie to the best of them"). Now Sarah and
Hayley are running an investigation into her. Three
of the four women Eloise gathered at Sublime Point
are independently withholding from her, none of them
knowing the others are. That is a live house, and it
costs one scene.

## What Hayley has to work with

- Eloise rigged the Random Allocation to marry Sarah
  to Alexander in record time ([[eloises-empire]]).
- Eloise had already jailbroken Chip's "encrypted"
  tablet, neutralised his private security, and
  traced his split signing bonus before hiring him.
- Eloise assembled the principals — physician,
  security, engineer, heir-bearer — into one compound.
- Eloise relocated the [[sages-prophecy|Sages]] into
  that compound with their suitcases.
- Eloise refers to Sarah's baby "like she was speaking
  about a transaction" (festival platform, noted by
  the whole platform "perhaps even Walter").
- Alexander was removed to Liechtenstein the moment
  the marriage was executed and the pregnancy became
  "aesthetically challenging" — Hayley has no reason
  yet to read that as anything but contempt.
