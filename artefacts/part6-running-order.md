# Part 6 — proposed running order

*Proposal, revised 2026-08-10 (v6); [[part6-master-plan]] is reconciled to
this version. Twenty-two chapters. Chapters hold two or
three pieces from **different** arcs; the three backstory documents are
**decomposed into fragments and distributed** in binder order — Hardy, then
The medicine, then A Big Crush. Chapter titles are descriptive placeholders.
Status tags: **[drafted]** · **[rework]** · **[synopsis]** · **[to write]** ·
**[fragment]** = a slice of an existing drafted document.*

**⏱ THE PARALLEL SET.** These five scenes **all end on the sentence "And
that's when Sarah's waters broke," and they are therefore all happening at the
same instant** — one moment, five rooms. Chapter 22's second scene is the
sixth and last occurrence, and it is the reveal that locates the moment for
the reader. Marked **⏱ ENDS ON THE LINE** in place below, and nowhere else:

1. *Elvi vs Government forces* (ch 19)
2. *Halo vs Hayley & Kathy* (ch 19)
3. *Ajent vs Gerry* (ch 20)
4. *Tony vs Alexander* (ch 21)
5. *Sarah confronts Eloise* (ch 22) — the last, and the reveal

*Each scene may finish its business in full and then take the line as a coda —
Halo dies, and that's when Sarah's waters broke. Where a scene runs past the
instant, the elastic form is available: **"And n minutes later, Sarah's waters
broke."** No drafted ending needs truncating for this.*

*What is excluded is anything that must happen at a different time: the
arrivals and the siege build-up (days and hours earlier), and — see the master
plan §8 — **"Chilo makes his move,"** whose ending chains directly into
chapter 22's first scene.*

*v6 changes: **M1's swimming-carnival content reinstated** — the author
confirms it as canon and explains what it is doing (a child who should be
losing the ability to walk is fit enough to compete, while the signature her
mother cannot give is the doctor's; **Sarah must never donate blood or permit
a blood test**). LGMD reference file recorded. **Chapter 22 rebuilt as two
scenes** per the author; the drafted terrace scene moves back to **chapter
13**, where its clock still works. The author's scene annotations are carried
verbatim as **[AUTHOR]** notes.*

**1. In the Brothel**

  (the former Clambake), **before** the ceremony, Sarah — watching Chanta's
  suspiciously radiant Elvines — primes Kathy to name him the cure; the new

- The new High Elvis delivers his inauguration speech echoing the first, and
  incorporates his response to the Get Bent protest. He explains Kathy's
  theory, and has a new proposal.
- Chanta climbs into the spa bath and tells the audience "Go forth and hump."
  For the first time in living memory, the old spa is filled with water.

**2. The Humpening**

  be copied.
- *The Humpening* [to write; empty stub exists] — The orgy decreed at the
  inauguration becomes a city-wide institution that never stops, sick Elvi

**3. The Viral Report**

- *Johnny enlists Chanta* [rework] — Johnny rings Chanta with the Fridge

- *The Viral Report* [rework] — Gerry guesses at the collapsing death rates

**4. Seven Hours**

**7. First Contact**

**8. The Gospel Truth**

- *What Hayley Found* [to write] — **[AUTHOR: what she found is essentially

**9. Warnings**
- *Late Night Experiments* [drafted] — **[AUTHOR: mostly bullshit.]** What
  survives: an unauthorised night run, reality cracked open for minutes.
  Sarah has already recognised the patterns by this stage, so the scene
  carries no discovery for her.
- *Medicine M2* [fragment] — The injection ritual unchanged since she was
  seven — the tin opened away from her, the pencil mark, the two vials of
  blood, the rules: tell no one, never let anybody take your blood.

**10. Risk Assessment**
- *Eloise Measures Risk* [rework] — **[AUTHOR: mostly bullshit. Eloise would
  definitely NOT be talking about who controls the channel. She would keep
  this to herself. A reveal for it must be well placed and not
  over-egged.]** So: her real aim stays entirely hidden here and surfaces
  once, at chapter 14. What the scene does instead is measure risk and
  authorise — nothing declared.
- *Medicine M3* [fragment] — On the last night everything is in the wrong
  order: five ampoules instead of one, no measurements, a bruise, "I'll be
  back for your birthday," and the door. **The reveal lands here: young Sarah
  had Limb-Girdle Muscular Dystrophy, severe early-onset (LGMD R1 or R9)** —
  autosomal recessive, so it takes girls as readily as boys, and neither
  parent knew they carried it. Against its timetable she should have lost
  the ability to walk at twelve and died in her late teens. All clinical
  detail from [[research/Limb-Girdle Muscular Dystrophy]].
- *The Bunker Ultimatum* [drafted] — **[AUTHOR: mostly bullshit. Not clear
  about the technical feasibility of machinery that could take variable
  numbers of discs. Maybe a circular track on which any number of harnesses
  can be mounted (each able to spin its own disc) and calibrated to focus
  the energy waves emanating from the discs.]** The track answers the
  1→3→5-disc progression across the part; see the master plan.

**21. More Nemeses**
- *Tony vs Alexander* **⏱ ENDS ON THE LINE** [rework] — Chanta and Alexander have the least

**22. The Last Chapter** *(two scenes; last in story time, and the reveal
that locates every earlier "And that's when Sarah's waters broke")*
- *The Doors Burst Open* [to write] — The doors burst open on Chilo's hand
  at the controls; every strand arrives in the chamber at once; and Sarah
  **loses control of the control room** — the array climbing toward maximum
  resonance with nobody she can overrule standing at it.
- *Sarah confronts Eloise* **⏱ ENDS ON THE LINE — the last occurrence, and the novel's final sentence** [to write; new scene, the title carried over] —
  Having lost the room, Sarah goes to the one person who owns the building
  and can still stop it. **Eloise appears to break Sarah** — the last and
  worst blow in the book, from the woman who has spent the part being right
  — and on that, at last, the novel's final sentence: *And that's when
  Sarah's waters broke.*
