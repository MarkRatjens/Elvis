## Function (one line)
Chanta, dumped into the Elvi's festival machine as its reluctant Great Teacher, hits the wall — he cannot write the anthem they need, and he knows exactly why: Sarah. End before the phone rings.

## POV / naming
Chanta's POV; Elvi milieu → **"Chanta."** Voice: deadpan, self-deprecating, urchin-cunning, "tax" as expletive.

## Beats
1. **Arrival at the festival apparatus.** Straight off the kidnapping — Chanta installed somewhere that shows the scale and absurdity of what he's been conscripted into (the half-built Domain stage, a "writing room," an Elvi entourage treating him as holy). The machine assumes he'll deliver; he's a prisoner with a pen.
2. **The ask made concrete.** The High Elvis (or a delegated Elvi) spells out what "Great Teacher" now means: one anthem that can out-sing the gospel — a counter-contagion. Reaffirm the stakes from the kidnap scene (lose this and the Elvi don't recover) without re-explaining the schism.
3. **The block, dramatised.** Chanta tries. Nothing — or worse, doggerel (canon: he's been rhyming "selection" with "erection/affection" and giving up). The comedy of a man ordered to manufacture inspiration on deadline. The Elvi read the silence as holy contemplation, which makes it worse.
4. **The diagnosis — it's Sarah.** Chanta admits (to himself, or to one Elvi he half-trusts) that the well went dry when Sarah left; the words used to come *through* her / the baby. The pivot the whole slot exists for. Dry, not maudlin — an involuntary crack, mastered.
5. **The unreachable source.** Establish cleanly that Sarah is at Sublime Point and he's cut off from her — learned *here*, from the Elvi (who revere and guard the Holy Momma), so his knowing it in the next scene is earned, not bled in. Ache + powerlessness = the hook.
6. **Button, handing off.** Land on Chanta stuck and aching — the exact state Johnny's "access to Sarah" call will exploit. Do **not** stage the call; let "Johnny enlists Chanta" open on it.

## Character development
- **Chanta:** dissipated-and-blocked (penthouse) → coerced participant → a man who now *wants* the thing he can't have (Sarah), for a reason both creative and emotional. The scene converts his passive block into an active need. Seed his ambivalence about the Dawkinsian crusade he accidentally founded — the reluctant prophet of a religion he finds ridiculous.
- **The High Elvis:** counselling register, not messianic bombast (the cape-unfurling cartoon is what we're leaving behind). Exhausted, patient, quietly ruthless — the man who said "or do we need another pool cue."
- **Optional third presence:** rather than repeat the Chanta/High-Elvis two-hander from the kidnap scene, consider one distinct Elvi (an Elvisette, a road-crew Elvis, or a canonical cabinet figure — **Vince Everett / a Minister from "The High Elvis calls a meeting,"** never "Kalamazoo/Memphis") to vary the texture and give Chanta someone to be dry at.

## Threads planted / advanced
- **Muse → reunion:** Chanta's need for Sarah, made acute → the engine that pulls his plot back to hers.
- **Disc-heist setup:** his isolation from Sarah is the vulnerability Johnny converts next scene (Sarah-access ⇄ the Elvi as thieves). Don't foreshadow Johnny; just leave the wound open.
- **Festival stakes / Humble:** keep Rex Humble and the gospel threat present as pressure, not re-exposition.

- Australian spelling; "tax" profanity intact; avoid em-dashes unless the rhythm wants them.
- **Sealed-canon fence:** Chanta's health / his effect on others is not his to know or explain here (it surfaces in "Sarah's Viral Contingency" as an *observed* mystery). Don't touch it.
- No manufactured hooks — the natural button is Chanta stuck and reaching for a Sarah he can't get to.
