### Rock Against Revival (the festival) — how it happens

- **The mechanism: money, permit, tickets, stream.** The [[the-high-elvis|High Elvis]]'s answer to the gospel contagion ("a war of memes… fight music with music") is a festival on **The Domain** — the ~30–40ha ground-level lawn at the western end of the Eastern Suburbs anti-climate bubble, where the Airborne hold galas and executions (often both on the one event card, especially at Christmas) — chosen so "no one can look away," neither Holy Rollers nor Airborne. **Money:** the High Elvis asks [[chanta|Chanta]] not for money (Chanta's songs already made the Elvi rich) but for **influence and paperwork** — Chanta's Hardy-estate lawyers, accountants, and "that terrifying woman who runs your diary." **Permit cover:** a **shelf company** lodges it as a **classical concert** of unpopular film-score composers; the Domain Trust waves it through for the booking fee and a tax write-off, expecting near-nobody to show, and no official ever inspects the build (subwoofers and rigging declared as "timpani, choral risers, incidental audio fidelity projectors" — "the permit said classical; what the city got was an uprising"; the paperwork declares the genre "classical polka thrash metal" — "classical did not make the authorities nervous" — and its one health question, whether any guests suffer "altitude deficiency sickness," is the easiest NO box the High Elvis ever ticked; no permit in the Domain's heavily audited history has ever asked whether the audience might be the wrong people — the Elvi, or the Cloudborne without a WORK HERE card). **Tickets:** a **free ticket slipped inside every vinyl dust jacket** the Elvi sell nationwide, with a web address to buy more — viral-but-quiet, so the authorities don't notice; this is how it reaches **Airborne youth** (Elvi vinyl plays on **Triple Pay**, the Airborne paywalled mutation of Triple J — the "The High Elvis kidnaps Tony" draft still says "college radio," a line to update). **Reach:** ~half a million on the lawn plus a **live stream** to "every last Elvi, and the uncloned along with them," aiming to reprogram the Holy Rollers and "shove a drumstick up those Airborne noses." (scenes: "The High Elvis kidnaps Tony," "The Domain")

### Rock Against Revival — the festival day

- **Old money, height, and the ground.** Anyone can buy height ("there was always another taller tower on the drawing board") — the sky is the one thing the meritocracy can always make more of, so it counts for less every year; harbour and beach frontage cannot be made, and was bought, married into, and litigated/killed over before the first Airborne ate breakfast at altitude. New money went up; old money kept the ground as well — "anyone who counted could tell the two apart at a glance." Real views from real places are quietly bid for at prestigious art auction houses — provided there is not a Banksy tag in sight. Before The Layer became reality, old money funded both Layer denialism and a campaign for government protection of "culturally significant landmarks" — their Eastern Suburbs mansions, apartments and bachelor pads, plus all beaches south to Coogee (the campaign predates [[jamison-hardy|Jamison Hardy]] joining the Mergers and Acquisitions Youth). Bondi Beach came to be protected; Bondi Junction was "acid-rained back to the 19th century." (scene: "The Domain")
- **The Eastern Suburbs Bubbles.** A taxpayer-funded public infrastructure project dwarfing Snowy Hydro-Nuclear 6: a series of connected semi-permeable electrostatic bubbles protecting old-money assets from the metropolitan climate old money itself precipitated (old money definitely is not the taxpayer). Entry is privately policed by the Bubble Police — efficient and "commercially influenceable"; admission requires the right genetic markers, credit rating, couture, six generations of paperwork, or a valid I WORK HERE card on your tablet ("It was not a place you got into by accident"). The Botanic Gardens and the Domain sit enclosed under the Bubbles' western end and are no longer freely accessible to the general public. The Ulyssian Greens Party was banned by law as a hate group.
