**Locations**: The Chuglinghams' backyard; patio and fire pit.

**Tone and Voice Sentence**: "Punctuality is a construct as mutable as temporal variance, which, I should add, is entirely subjective unless you’ve done the maths."

**Synopsis**:
Chilo arrives late to the barbecue, accompanied unexpectedly by the flamboyant and irrepressible Johnny. The Sages and Sarah immediately question the pair's tardiness, prompting Chilo to excuse their delay with characteristically grandiose musings on the Travelator's failings and temporal relativity. Johnny deflects questions about his presence with over-the-top compliments and theatrical charisma, swiftly capturing the ire and reluctant amusement of the gathered guests.

While Chilo diffuses attention through obtuse behavior, Johnny leans into his charm offensive, presenting Padmala with a bottle of ornate wine and engaging in affected banter about universal constants and barbecue flames. His blend of insincerity and sheer magnetism transforms initial annoyance into grudging tolerance, even eliciting hesitant laughter from Bala. As the evening progresses, the group navigates their mixed feelings about Johnny, acknowledging his disruption while allowing his personality to play out against a backdrop of firelight and smoldering coals.
