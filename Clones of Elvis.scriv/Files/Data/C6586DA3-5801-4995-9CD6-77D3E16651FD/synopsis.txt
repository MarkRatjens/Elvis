**Locations**: Train to the Zetland hover-pad; cab to the American Sound Studios; the Ghetto; Tony's apartment within the studio.

**Tone and Voice Sentence**: "You come around here in your designer clothes—which I’m pretty sure you’ve vomited on—and try to claim you wrote this Elvi song? That’s cultural appropriation, sport."

**Synopsis**:
Tony makes his way back to the American Sound Studios in the Ghetto, navigating a challenging return journey made worse by a quarrel with a self-righteous cab driver and a second, decrepit cab's jarring ride. Back at the studio, Tony's clarity begins to sharpen as he recalls recent events and discovers the forgotten thumb drive left by Haloren Smythe ("Halo"). 

Tony views the encrypted video recorded by Halo, who delivers ominous revelations about his secret role as a deep-cover PAPER agent assigned to protect Tony. Halo details multiple assassination attempts on Tony, the Stickett Inn bombing, and mysterious threats linked to Sarah Jacobson. Despite Halo’s fragmentary warnings and unfiltered misogynistic rambling, he instructs Tony to visit an address and follow recorded instructions for critical next steps. The video ends cryptically, leaving Tony with a chilling sense of Halo's role in the escalating intrigue and his place within a broader, high-stakes conspiracy.
