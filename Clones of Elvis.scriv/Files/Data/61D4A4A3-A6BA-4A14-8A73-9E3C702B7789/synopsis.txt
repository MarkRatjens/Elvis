**Locations**: Outside the mansion in the Ghetto; Alexander's police cordon.

**Synopsis**:
Hayley arrives at the police cordon surrounding the mansion, where Alexander Shakti directs a contentious operation. Confronting Alexander, Hayley challenges his narrative that Anthony Chantara is a terrorist and kidnapper, presenting evidence of Chantara’s personal relationship with Sarah Jacobson. She reveals connections between Chantara, Jacobson, and key events at the Stickett Inn and Travis Teh crime scene, pointing to inconsistencies in Alexander’s investigation and the potential involvement of PAPER agents pursuing undisclosed documents. Alexander, increasingly defensive and incredulous about the revelations, becomes visibly flustered. As Hayley pushes further, a new arrival at the gates catches their attention, while Alexander scrambles for information, amplifying the tension of the unfolding confrontation.
