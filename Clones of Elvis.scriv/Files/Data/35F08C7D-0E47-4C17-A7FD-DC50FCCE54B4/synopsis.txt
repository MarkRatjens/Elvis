**Locations**: Eloise Shakti's Sublime Point compound; Sarah's bedroom.

**Tone and Voice Sentence**: "Oh, my god! They’re impersonating me!"

**Synopsis**:
The day passes in reflective solitude as Sarah, Hayley, and Kathy find separate spaces in their luxurious "prison," avoiding each other as they process their circumstances. Deliveries of personal belongings offer a small reprieve, with Kathy taking comfort in her resupplied patches and an expensive bottle of red wine.

As night falls, Sarah and Kathy share a bed, leading to playful banter. Sarah hints at Hayley’s romantic interest in Kathy, but Kathy brushes it off with her usual irreverence. To distance herself, Sarah tunes into a music streaming app on her tablet, only to discover a new Elvi song, "Are you evolving tonight?" featuring a band parodying her image. The band's stage setup includes a giant figure clad in pink fairy-motif boxer shorts—a clear homage to Tony. Struck by the imagery, Sarah experiences an emotional moment that evolves into resolve, waking the next morning determined to regain control over her life and circumstances.
