**Locations**: Mansion parlour.

**Tone and Voice Sentence**: "If your grandmother was a snake that could smile, she’d smile exactly the way Eloise smiled."

**Plot Summary**:
- Eloise Shakti, a powerful and calculating figure cloaked in grandmotherly charm and menace, visits Sarah in the Elvi-controlled mansion. Flanked by an ominous entourage, she demands the Gyges Disc, demonstrating both keen knowledge of Sarah’s life and an unnerving willingness to commit violence.
- Eloise’s lethal authority is underscored when she nonchalantly sacrifices a member of her team to illustrate the consequences of Sarah’s resistance. Cornered and emotionally unraveling, Sarah reluctantly retrieves the disc, presenting it to Eloise in a handkerchief.
- Doubts about alliances deepen when Johnny, a "friend" from Sarah’s past, reveals his longstanding allegiance to one of Eloise’s subsidiaries. He confirms the authenticity of the disc and secures it for Eloise.
- Eloise departs, leaving everyone shaken, but not before orchestrating one final dramatic turn—introducing Tony, re-entering the narrative under unclear motives, adding to looming tensions between Sarah and her unresolved sense of betrayal.
