**Locations**: High-security boardroom after cabinet dismissal.

**Tone and Voice Sentence**: "If there’s one thing history has taught us, it’s that the next best thing after having no religion is having at least two religions."

**Synopsis**:
Following the cabinet meeting, Piotr Dunhilton consults privately with Gerry Hardman to fast-track "Operation Parramatta," no longer willing to wait until November to address the growing Elvi threat. Searching for actionable solutions, Gerry examines the Grayling Corporation warranty terms for exploitable weaknesses in the Elvi clones, humorously entertaining ideas like dumping bacon and coffee over the Ghetto, to no avail. The conversation evolves into identifying religion as the most effective tool for destabilization, arguing that introducing a competing belief system could exploit the Elvi’s penchant for internal conflict and fracture their burgeoning unity.

Although Gerry acknowledges risks, he advocates for the profitability of a prolonged religious divide, convincing Dunhilton of its viability. The decision is sealed in theatrical fashion with a coin toss, landing on "Profits." Gerry proposes spreading gospel music among the Elvi as the catalyst for this new religious schism, envisioning a financially beneficial scheme that could simultaneously neutralize their cohesion while presenting a façade of permissibility for the Airborne leadership. Dunhilton, emboldened by the plan, leaves the room with unwarranted confidence in the coin’s verdict as Gerry contemplates the chaos he is about to unleash.
