Hayley, having failed to uncover any covert Gyges programme inside Shakti, changes direction after Chip's warnings and investigates Eloise's previous encounters with Fridge Force. She discovers that Fridge Force once closed a Shakti stasis project after objecting to experiments involving interacting fields. The project's official purpose was innocuous enough, but the agency's technical assessment identified work that went beyond that purpose and had plausible military applications. Eloise fought the shutdown bitterly and regarded Fridge Force's restrictions as obstructionist. Hayley can find no evidence that a weapon was actually being developed.
