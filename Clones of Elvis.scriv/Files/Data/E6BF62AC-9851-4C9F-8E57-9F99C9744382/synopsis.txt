**Development kit: Chanta’s landing → Sarah calls and reassures him**

The uploaded timeline places Chanta’s landing at **20:17**, the Elvi’s protective response from **20:17–20:31**, and Sarah’s call at **20:20–20:23**. The call therefore happens while the Elvi are still responding to his arrival.

**What survives from the Scrivener material**

Very little of the arrival mechanics. The Cadillac, government checkpoint, Grayling authorisation codes, Gerry permitting a demonstration, stage outside the gates and separate infiltration of Elvi fighters belong to superseded arrangements. The concert and concealed discs have already entered the compound.

The useful underlying material is:

- Chanta arrives with Jones clones whose appearance carries institutional menace.
- The Elvi have reasons of their own to protect him.
- Chanta wants Sarah; the people accompanying and receiving him interpret his arrival through their own concerns.
- The clones’ independent conspiratorial thinking can complicate an otherwise straightforward intention.

The current clone-development prose matters more than the old arrival synopsis. These are the Joneses who investigated breakfast, discovered surveillance devices and built a conspiracy operation in a sauna. They are capable, socially peculiar and increasingly convinced that apparently unrelated things belong to one explanation. They are not simply interchangeable bodyguards awaiting Chanta’s orders.

**Engine**

Chanta has come to reach Sarah, but arriving publicly with a corporate hover-copt and several Ajent Joneses makes his arrival look like something larger. The Elvi move to protect their Great Teacher; security has to decide what his entourage represents; the Joneses find abundant confirmation that something is going on.

Sarah’s call gives Chanta the information he actually needs. It can settle his immediate fear without settling everyone else’s interpretation of the situation. The useful turn is from **trying to reach her immediately** to **agreeing to remain nearby and wait**.

**Causal spine — proposed**

1. **The Hardy Enterprises hover-copt lands inside the compound.** Its corporate identity gives the arrival a public face before Chanta appears. Landing permission needs a brief credible basis, but does not require an aerial confrontation or an elaborate clearance sequence.

2. **Chanta emerges with the Joneses.** Recognition produces different reactions: the Elvi recognise their Great Teacher; security sees the accompanying men. Chanta may initially assume both reactions mean people will help him find Sarah.

3. **The High Elvis and nearby Elvi move towards him.** Their presence makes it harder to isolate Chanta, move him somewhere against his wishes or quietly turn him away. Protection can emerge through gathering, greeting and accompanying him before anyone issues a threat.

4. **The Joneses interpret the reception.** Security interest, the closed compound, the interrupted broadcast and the gathering Elvi can all enter their existing theory. Chanta is trying to ask a simple question while they are establishing what sort of conspiracy has answered it.

5. **News of Chanta’s arrival reaches the bunker.** The command post is now available; a report or camera view is sufficient. Sarah calls him. There is no need for Chanta to acquire knowledge of the underground layout first.

6. **Sarah supplies a recognisable, personal account of her condition.** She is alive, with Kathy and Hayley, and somewhere protected. What matters is that Chanta hears her speaking for herself rather than receives another assurance from Shakti personnel.

7. **Chanta accepts a reason to wait.** A strong possibility is that Sarah wants rest and asks him to stay with the High Elvis until they can arrange to meet. She can want him nearby without wanting another journey, argument or reunion immediately.

8. **The High Elvis helps make that agreement practical.** He is explicitly included in the timeline’s call. He can undertake to keep Chanta with them and receive further word. The gathering continues after the call, but its immediate purpose has changed: Chanta is staying, rather than trying to force his way towards Sarah.

**Concrete handles**

- **Hardy Enterprises markings on the hover-copt.** Chanta’s access to enormous resources remains disproportionate to the personal errand he is using them for.
- **The first familiar face stepping out, followed by repeated unfamiliar-to-most faces.** Recognition of Chanta can become unease about his company.
- **Different ties on otherwise identical Joneses.** Useful for distinguishing actions without repeatedly explaining who is who.
- **Concert sound carrying to the helipad.** The compound has not become an empty battlefield. People are still performing, listening and moving between activities.
- **Elvi arriving with what they were already holding:** instruments, drinks, food, concert equipment. Their protective response interrupts an actual evening.
- **A security employee trying to address one Jones while another answers.** Institutional confidence becomes awkward without making the employee incompetent.
- **Chanta’s device passing to the High Elvis.** A simple way to give the High Elvis an actual part in the reassurance and agreement.
- **Sarah’s voice competing with music and several men offering advice.** Chanta may have to move a few steps or tell everyone to stop helping.
- **The Joneses watching different things during the call.** One watches security; another the crowd; another listens to the conversation. Similar appearance need not mean identical attention.
- **The contrast between arrival and purpose.** Corporate aircraft, formidable escorts, a gathering crowd—and Chanta wants to know whether Sarah is all right.

**Character collisions**

**Chanta and the Joneses: concern versus explanation.**  
Chanta wants news of Sarah. The Joneses want to understand the forces acting upon the situation. Their explanations can make him more anxious while being almost entirely unhelpful to his immediate question.

Keep their paranoia attached to specific observations. A guard receiving an instruction, two cameras turning, or a repeated phrase is better material than a generic speech about the Deep State. They can be right that power is being contested and wrong about the connections.

**The High Elvis and Shakti security: hospitality with consequences.**  
The High Elvis can greet Chanta as someone who plainly belongs among them. Every additional Elvis makes that welcome harder to interrupt. Security does not have to retreat or surrender for the protection to work; its available choices simply become more public and more troublesome.

**Sarah and Chanta: reassurance without dismissal.**  
The call should give him something more than “I’m fine; stay there.” His concern deserves an answer. Sarah can acknowledge that she wants to see him, tell him what she needs now, and give him a place in what happens next.

That also distinguishes her use of reassurance here from pacifying Alexander during the escape. With Chanta, she can be direct. His willingness to listen can carry the emotional difference.

**Modular possibilities**

- **An Elvis initially mistakes the Joneses for captors.** Chanta has to explain that they came with him. His assurance may be less reassuring than he expects.
- **A Jones treats the Elvi’s spontaneous welcome as a coordinated operation.** He asks who organised it. Several Elvi offer incompatible answers, each sincerely.
- **Chanta tries to introduce his companions.** He discovers he has not developed an adequate convention for introducing several men with the same name.
- **The High Elvis makes protection look like hospitality.** Someone finds Chanta a chair or drink while others remain close. Nobody needs to announce a defensive perimeter.
- **A Jones objects to the phone conversation’s security.** Chanta is sufficiently relieved to hear Sarah that he ignores an objection he would ordinarily entertain.
- **Sarah hears the crowd and asks what he has brought with him.** Chanta’s answer can reveal his limited grasp of how his arrival looks.
- **The clones interpret Sarah’s reassurance differently.** Chanta hears that she is safe; a Jones notices the exact words she did not use. This need not undo the agreement to wait.
- **The concert continues around the supposed emergency.** A musician may need an Elvis back onstage while that Elvis considers himself occupied protecting the Great Teacher.

**Boundaries and later seeds**

- Chanta lands **inside the compound**. No return to the obsolete northern-checkpoint arrival.
- The Elvi are already inside and have an operating stage. Their response does not require another admission or equipment movement.
- The new timeline establishes the landing but not its authorising party. Keep that a small practical choice rather than importing the old Grayling credentials.
- All of Chanta’s Jones clones revived population accompanies Chanta.
- Sarah’s reassurance should account for Chanta accepting the overnight pause. It need not resolve the security split or grant him immediate underground access.
- The call can establish an expectation of further contact. The later movement around the Traverse then has something specific to disturb.
- Keep the final Sarah–Chanta encounter available for its later emotional work. Here, hearing her voice and knowing she wants him nearby can be enough.
