Locations: Parliament House (the old Clambake brothel), the Ghetto — the same room as the first inauguration.

Register: Deadpan, external. The reader sees the scene over Walter's shoulder and is never taken inside his head.

Synopsis:
Eight days after Operation Cleanse, the Elvi inaugurate a new High Elvis in a room that is visibly short of Elvi. Parliament decrees the office continuous and every future High Elvis to be of the same series as the first; the man who walks in has the same face, the same walk and the same "thank you very much," and the room takes that as a comfort. Walter, brought along on the Holy Momma's promise, does not chant.

Kathy has isolated the virus killing the Elvi and can offer nothing that outruns it. Hayley, counting, finds nineteen of the forty-one Elvi in the room were on the plaza and eleven of those are Elvines in improbably good health. Chanta, boasting about the clean living of his Elvisettes, admits he has slept with all the surviving Elvines — and is the last person in the room to work out what he has just said. Sarah, without explaining how she knows, observes that a thing which has moved once can be moved again. Kathy delivers the prescription as a joke and then has to stand behind it; Dianne Carter, healthiest woman at four funerals, argues it harder than she does.

The High Elvis decrees the Humpening from the spa bath and the Ghetto empties into the streets to comply. On his way out he signs Walter's festival poster. It is the same signature.
