**Register**: Comic-grotesque throughout — NOT a sincere protest drama. Dramatised on the plaza (show, don’t report — the old draft’s failure was reporting the vigil from behind Gerry’s window). Tone anchor: “Linguistic abominations! In my day, protesters at least had the decency to use proper grammar when undermining authority.” (Dunhilton’s line lands next scene, but it is the register to write toward.)

**Function**: Bridges the concert to Operation Cleanse. The cross-class crowd the Domain united does not go home — it takes the protest UP above The layer and camps on their doorstep. A trespass, not a supplication. This is the crowd the virus will later target. Ends with the vigil settled in for its second night; the Gerry/Dunhilton reckoning is the next scene (“The Monday conversation”).

**Outline** (Saturday midnight → Sunday night):
1. Dispersal from the Domain at midnight; trains and buses stopping. A strand of the concert crowd rides UP with the Airborne kids into the Bent Street plaza rather than heading home — the trespass is an inside job, the Airborne young smuggling the ground into the sky. [Plaza access = new canon; see Open.]
2. The vigil forms outside Tramp Tower. First shout up the tower glass; a night guard forgets his training and waves; the crowd cheers him. Nobody leaves.
3. “Supplies” arrive through the small hours (audible quotation marks) — eskies, then bedding. Festival merch turns out to have been camping gear all along (a cape is a blanket; an Air Guitar case, with a bicycle pump, can be inflated to a serviceable pillow - beware the strap does not choke you). A barbecue trailer; snags up the plaza like a second anthem.
4. The OCCUPY →  bedsheet correction christens the movement: the name is an instruction.
5. Sunday: the vigil organises itself the way soup organises on a low heat — eskies federate into kitchens. The High Elvis’s “take your rubbish home” covenant leaves neat rubbish bags stacked along the kerb like sandbags — plant the irony (the government’s answer will be a literal cleanse). Above-the-Layer inversion comedy available: Cloudborne and Elvi kids standing in raw, unrationed sunlight (sunburn) — the “flaunt of wasted sunlight” now flaunted at the wrong people. “GET BENT” T-shirts are quickly printed and circulated for free
6. “Religious Minds” mutates into “Piglicious Minds,” sung AT the buildings (different from singing near one). Plant the song’s spread — its climb to No. 1 pays off in the Monday scene.

News crews arrive; evening bulletins lead with it; split-screen against Pastor Humble’s revival (pays the owed Humble split-screen — faith and revolt, the two contagions side by side); Channel Forty-Eight Sty News caption comedy (“AGITATORS SEIZE GOVERNMENT PRECINCT” over vision of a man turning sausages). Crowd size never pinned — sources lowball/inflate per vested interest; one absurd exact figure for comedy. Close on the second night, warmer than the first, the vigil dug in.
