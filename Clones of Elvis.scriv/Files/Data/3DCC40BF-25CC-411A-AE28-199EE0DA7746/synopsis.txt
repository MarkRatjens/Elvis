**Locations**: Mansion parlour; mansion grounds; Eloise Shakti's limousine.

**Tone and Voice Sentence**: "Now, if you’re wondering how your gunships were disabled, I took such action in accordance with the Warranties and Service, and the Proprietary Rights provisions of our contract."

**Synopsis**:
Tony witnesses the initial chaos as the mansion collapses, only to find the parlour miraculously intact. Inside, Eloise Shakti showcases unparalleled control, disabling both PAPER’s and the Elvi's weaponry through her backdoor access to all systems. She neutralizes Agent Jones’s tactical operation with a calculated threat of destruction, forcing his reluctant compliance. As Sarah, Kathy, Alexander, and Hayley escape through a window into the backyard, Tony watches helplessly, yearning to assist Sarah but dwarfed by her immediate protectors.

Outside, Eloise confronts the Elvi guards, destroying their guitar and reducing their defiance with a sharp rebuke. She gathers her party into her limousine, bypassing immobilised opposition forces with regal composure. Tony, left behind, feels the stark distance growing between him and Sarah. In the limousine, Eloise contemplates the formidable potential of the women accompanying her, subtly positioning them within her larger, ambitious schemes.
