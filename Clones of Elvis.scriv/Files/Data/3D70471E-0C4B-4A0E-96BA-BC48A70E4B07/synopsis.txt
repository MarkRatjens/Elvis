Key elements in this chapter:
 
1. The arrival of Elvi doctors (Dr John and Dr Nick) to treat Sarah's symptoms.
2. The revelation of Sarah's pregnancy to the Elvi and their reaction to it.
3. Sarah's consideration of abortion and the Elvi's strong opposition to it.
4. The performance of a threatening song by the Elvi to prevent Sarah from considering abortion.
5. Sarah's official confinement by order of the "Supreme Kingness" until the birth of the baby.
