**Night Infiltration**

In his mobile command unit, Gerry studies the tactical displays showing Alexander's media circus and Tony's Ajent clone incursion converging on Sublime Point's main compound. The quantum suppression fields are holding steady, but the multiple variables are creating opportunities for chaos—exactly the kind of chaos that could serve his deeper agenda.

"Dunhilton," he says into his encrypted phone, "I'm requesting permission for a covert advance team. Small unit, night insertion, observation only."

"Explain."

"We have Alexander's crew broadcasting live, four rogue Ajent clones with unknown programming, and Eloise's people trapped inside. That's a powder keg waiting to explode." Gerry's voice takes on the tone of reasonable concern. "If we can monitor the situation from inside, maybe we can ensure the explosion happens in our favor."

From his harbor office, Dunhilton considers this. The public operation provides perfect cover for private objectives, and Gerry's value has always been his ability to work in shadows while others operate in spotlights.

"Approved. Keep it quiet, keep it small. I want deniability if things go sideways."

"Understood. We'll go in dark—no radio contact until extraction."

**The Team**

Gerry assembles his squad in the pre-dawn darkness: five of his most trusted operatives, all with extensive black ops experience. They move with practiced silence, checking gear and reviewing infiltration routes on tactical tablets. Night vision, suppressed weapons, monitoring equipment—everything needed for covert observation.

What Gerry doesn't notice is the slight difference in one operative's gait, the way Haloren Smythe's supposedly scarred throat shows no damage in the infrared imaging, the predatory satisfaction that flickers across his features when he thinks no one is watching. The man who was supposedly killed at the Gravity Well restaurant has made his way back into Gerry's inner circle through forged credentials and careful timing.

"Primary objective is intelligence gathering," Gerry briefs the team. "Secondary objective is identifying opportunities for advantageous chaos. Alexander's media operation gives us cover, but I want eyes on those Ajent clones specifically."

Halo nods along with the others, but his mind is elsewhere. This isn't about government objectives or tactical advantages. This is about getting close to Sarah again, finishing what he started. The chaos Gerry wants to foment will provide perfect cover for personal business.

**The Shadow**

What neither Gerry nor his team realizes is that they're being followed. Special Agent Ajent Jones moves through the darkness fifty meters behind them, his paranoia and gospel-music-addled mind finally crystallizing around a single, burning certainty: Gerry Hardman is "Jihad Man," the master manipulator behind every conspiracy that has plagued his investigations.

Jones has spent weeks analyzing Gerry's rise through the ranks, his convenient positioning during every major crisis, his uncanny ability to be both solution and problem. The pieces fit too perfectly to be coincidence. Tonight, in the confusion of multiple operations, Jones plans to settle accounts with the man who has made his professional life a maze of contradictions.

As Gerry's team slips through the outer perimeter, Jones follows like a vengeful shadow, his service weapon ready and his mind rehearsing the confrontation he's planned. The gospel music that broke his psyche has been replaced by something more dangerous—absolute certainty in his righteousness.

**Convergence in Darkness**

The three forces move through Sublime Point's darkened grounds like pieces on an invisible chessboard. Gerry's team advances toward the main compound, seeking vantage points to monitor Alexander's broadcast and the Ajent clones' infiltration. Halo moves with them, his true agenda hidden behind professional competence. And Jones stalks them all, his fractured mind focused on finally exposing the conspiracy he believes Gerry represents.

None of them realize that their convergence is being watched from Sublime Point's security center, where Eloise and Hayley monitor multiple feeds with growing concern. The siege has become a multi-layered nightmare of competing agendas, and the night is about to explode into chaos that none of the participants can fully control or predict.

In the darkness outside Sublime Point, personal vendettas and professional objectives blur together, setting the stage for a confrontation that will determine not just the fate of the facility, but the survival of everyone trapped within its walls.
