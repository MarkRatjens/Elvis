The massive stage spanned clear across the enormous square hole that once was the Ulyssian Trade Tower, a historic and ugly stone relic, neglected for decades before its final demise. Elderly lime cement, exhausted by a century long vigil, had turned to grit and poured out of joints like hourglass sand, gradually bleeding away structural integrity. The small, but powerful terrorist bomb had found a perfect mark, cracking the structure to its foundations and rendering it nothing but smoldering rubble. 
Above the stage a huge banner read “Elvis Arts and Entertainment Benefit concert - Bring back the Stick it Inn” (Description of stage equipment) A benefit concert was an event that no-one in the PTD had ever attended. The concept of earning money and then giving it away was so novel, that a massive crowd of Dawks and bemused citizens arrived on the street many hours before the music began. They began chanting, “Dawks, Dawks, Dawks!” and singing their favourite Elvis tunes loudly and horribly. A few roadie-Elvises quickly tired of their songs being destroyed in such a manner, broke out their guitars and waded into the crowd. The crowd cheered loudly and parted to allow entry to the minstrels as they serenaded the throng, killing off the wretched singing that had offended their ears so.
