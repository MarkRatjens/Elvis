**Locations**: Sarah's office, Sublime Point.

**Tone and Voice Sentence**: "You see patterns. I see weapons development."

**Synopsis**:
Chip brings his objection to the five-disc pentagram to Sarah, who calls Hayley in and then waits him out in silence. He arrives contemptuous — naming Chilo formally as Mr Damagetus, asking whether they are a witch's coven — and argues from what one disc does when it spins too fast.

Sarah answers with the project's own position rather than reassurance: two discs proved more stable than one, they are testing whether slower speeds get the same result, and the work is cleaning up signals already being received. She does not say aloud what the patterns are, or that they have grown more vivid as Little Clinton has grown. Chip refuses the frame — she sees patterns, he sees weapons development.

Hayley signals, without a word, that the office is not to be treated as private, and Chip spends the rest of the meeting locating the corners a device would sit in. The exchange continues on two levels after that: Sarah undertakes that the discs will not be developed to blow shit up, Chip observes that she works for an arms dealer, and Hayley notes that all three of them do.

Chip delivers that Johnny wants five discs. Sarah answers that the Sages report to her, that Johnny's reports come to her, and that none of them have asked for three more. Chip names Rotherhithe and is told she has already studied it. He leaves having decided not to underestimate her.
