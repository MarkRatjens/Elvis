**Locations**: Restaurant high in the Airborne city, Gravity Well.

**Tone and Voice Sentence**: "What’s a Gyges Disc?"

**Synopsis**:
In a tense sequel to their prior encounter, Hayley corners Stasis Anomalies Investigator Chip Stockwell at a public restaurant arranged for negotiations under Eloise Shakti's authority. Eloise sets the tone with dominance, introducing Hayley to Chip as her representative before leaving, but not before warning Chip about an explosive device covertly attached to him.

Hayley interrogates Chip about the Chuglinghams family disappearance, their tampered stasis unit, and its potential link to Travis Teh’s death, forcing him into admissions with her incisive questioning. When Hayley brings up the Gyges Discs, Chip’s demeanor shifts to genuine terror, confirming the connection between these devices and stasis technology. Hayley exploits Chip’s inability to counter her claims, escalating to the point of extracting veiled agreements to Eloise’s terms. By the end, Chip, visibly shaken, reluctantly agrees to cooperate, marking a decisive victory for Eloise's ambitions and Hayley’s resourceful pressures.
