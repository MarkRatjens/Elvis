Johnny smuggles Tony and three of the discs into Sublime Point. Being terrified of so many discs together, he tells the Elvi, “I don’t want them, you take them!” Then recovers to instruct, “I ah may need them later. Please store them in a cool dry place till then.” Bewildered, the Elvi shrug, hand over three of the discs and take the rest in guitar cases back to the Ghetto.
