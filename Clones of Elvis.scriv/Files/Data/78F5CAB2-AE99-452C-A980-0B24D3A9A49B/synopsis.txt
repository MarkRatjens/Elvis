\- The confrontation: Joneses appear with pistols trained on guards who have a modest numerical advantage and automatic weapons. They demand Sarah; the guards deny knowing her.

\- Chanta's diplomacy: He intervenes, promises harmlessness and offers to let the guards play cards until he returns. A guard aims at Chanta.

\- The cigarette shot: A Jones fires without injuring the guard, specifies the exact two cigarettes penetrated, and proves it when the guard checks his thigh pocket. He orders the guard to release Sarah and stop smoking. The primary-school-teacher tobacco cartel belongs to this satire.

\- The great reversal: Elvi enter singing “Glory, Glory Holy Momma.” The Joneses dissolve into tears. Guards spontaneously console them. The singing stops; the Joneses recover and overpower their comforters.

\- Victory: Elvi use discs against the remaining guards. Joneses bind both their own captives and the concussed guards. Chanta remains concerned about casualties.

\- The sabotage: Elvi deliberately wreck the empty maglev cars and guideway. Chanta objects; they insist it was planned. His understanding of the plan needn't match theirs.

\- Unequal knowledge: Chanta has experienced discs overturning vehicles. The Elvi may have learned considerably more through Johnny's desperate need for three. Neither their knowledge nor Chanta's should be artificially fixed.

\- The departure: The entire contingent takes service trucks along the parallel road. The damaged passenger system doesn't prevent their journey.

\- The seismic consequence: The bunker seismic egress releases. Its relationship to the disc activity and its consequences at Ruined Castle belong in this development span.

\- The larger arc: Chanta is moving towards a confrontation with Alexander, whose established friendship with him makes the conflict personally consequential.

### Three genuine openings for invention

1. What actually happens during the night that makes Chanta's vigil progressively funnier and his eventual risk decision earned?
2. What do the Elvi think their sabotage achieves, and how has Chanta misunderstood their intentions?
3. What does the seismic egress release change for the people already at Ruined Castle and for Chanta's approaching party?

Timing follows the action. There is no predetermined 04:30 assault, no required accidental sleep, and no requirement to preserve earlier clock entries at the expense of the scene.

This is the material to carry into your drafting, without treating the failed draft as canon.
