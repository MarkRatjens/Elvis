### Eloise argues for another test — development kit

**Present:** Eloise, Johnny, Dr Quark, Chip.

**Scene job:** clear the way for another Kebab test by removing Chip as the project’s remaining effective brake.

**Starting balance:** Sarah is gone on “stress leave”. Hayley is absent. Chip’s strongest allies are spent. Johnny senses that the room finally favours him.

**Pressure progression:**

1. **Another test is proposed.**
   - Missing Socks was an extraordinary result.
   - Gibson remains correlation, not causation.
   - Quark sees repeatability as the proper scientific way forward.

2. **Chip objects.**
   - He knows the danger is real.
   - He still cannot produce evidence anyone else can examine.

3. **Quark discounts him.**
   - Not theatrically.
   - Unsupported warnings do not carry scientific weight.
   - There is no need for Quark to call him a charlatan; his behaviour makes the judgement clear enough.

4. **Johnny gets bolder because Chip is isolated.**
   - He pecks at the obvious weakness: no evidence.
   - He is smug, petulant and opportunistic, not formidable.
   - He leans on Quark’s scepticism and Eloise’s impatience rather than making a strong argument of his own.

5. **Chip keeps getting in the way.**
   - He refuses to endorse the test.
   - Possibly refuses to cooperate with preparations.
   - His warnings now look, to the others, like obstruction rather than expertise.

6. **Eloise decides he is no longer useful.**
   - She is not trying to extract his secrets.
   - She has simply concluded that he is preventing the project from proceeding.

7. **Chip resists removal.**
   - This should force Eloise to escalate from administrative authority to physical coercion.

8. **End: the brig.**
   - Eloise has him confined in the same brig where she kept the Three Sisters.
   - Chip exits the project not because anyone proved him wrong, but because nobody who matters still accepts his warnings.

**End state:**
- another test can proceed;
- Quark remains scientifically cautious but not aligned with Chip;
- Johnny feels vindicated;
- Eloise has removed the obstacle;
- Chip, who knows the danger is real, is locked away.

**Do not spend here:**
- Chip revealing controllable remote quakes;
- Eloise interrogating him for Fridge Force secrets;
- Sarah re-entering the argument;
- Johnny suddenly becoming clever or brave;

**Chip is right → cannot prove it → loses credibility → becomes obstruction → gets removed.**
