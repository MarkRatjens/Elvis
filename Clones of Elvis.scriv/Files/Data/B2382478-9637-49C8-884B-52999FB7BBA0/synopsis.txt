**Locations**: Sarah’s room, mansion under Elvi custody.

**Tone and Voice Sentence**: "I’ve got about forty minutes of lucidity.’”

**Plot Summary**:
- Sarah awakens in her mansion room to find herself attended by a strange, over-friendly doctor, Kathy Logan. Kathy administers a patch to alleviate Sarah’s nausea while revealing she has already conducted medical tests on an unconscious Sarah, collecting samples without her consent.
- As Kathy reviews the test results, her demeanour shifts from playful to professional, revealing extraordinary findings about Sarah’s physiology that baffle medical explanation. Sarah remains defensive and anxious, suspecting Kathy knows more than she lets on.
- Tension heightens when Kathy discovers Sarah’s pregnancy, assuming Sarah was already aware due to the Elvi’s reverence for her as "Holy Momma." However, Sarah is shocked and disoriented by the revelation, asserting that she believed pregnancy to be impossible for her.
- Wracked with confusion, Sarah recalls the night of the Vegas Show and her time afterward with Tony. Her memory spirals into accusatory anger, unleashing a furious and devastating conclusion as she falsely labels the pregnancy the result of assault by Tony.
