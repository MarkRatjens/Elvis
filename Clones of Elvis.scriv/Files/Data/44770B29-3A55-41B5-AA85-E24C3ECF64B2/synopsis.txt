Christmas — Sarah confronts Eloise
Function: personal rupture; technological disagreement becomes betrayal.
Sarah enters the confrontation already knowing:
	•	interacting Gyges fields carry Rotherhithe-type danger;
	•	Eloise has previously overseen related research;
	•	Fridge Force identified military applications and shut that work down;
	•	Kebab has now produced a remote physical effect;
	•	the remote effect is related to the same coherence which produces intelligible communication;
	•	Sarah’s dream-patterns may be involved in organising that coherence.
The immediate trigger is Sarah learning that Eloise understood materially more of this than she disclosed and chose to continue.
Sarah’s argument should therefore centre on trust, not merely dual-use technology.
The underlying charge:
You knew this was relevant. You knew what those results might mean. You let me continue without telling me.
Eloise has room to defend herself:
	•	Project Kebab has genuinely achieved interdimensional communication;
	•	the remote event remains poorly understood;
	•	the old work never produced an operational weapon;
	•	understanding the phenomenon is necessary to control it;
	•	suppressing the research would leave humanity ignorant of a capability that demonstrably exists.
The argument escalates because both women can make a case.
Sarah also has the personal complication that the increasingly coherent information has been reaching her through her dreams/pregnancy. Eloise’s willingness to continue experimentation therefore implicates Sarah and Little Clinton in a way an ordinary project dispute would not.
The accumulated stress of the confrontation causes Sarah’s waters to break.
Critical pacing principle: Christmas is the first point at which Sarah possesses all three elements together:
	1	capability — Kebab can produce a remote physical effect;
	2	precedent — Eloise previously pursued related work that Fridge Force considered militarily significant;
	3	choice/concealment — Eloise recognised the connection and continued without fully informing Sarah.
Any earlier scene may provide one or two of these. None should provide all three.
