A small change in any one of them moved what the material called “the region of maximum effect”, and that region was in no way limited to three dimensions.
She was beginning to understand why it spooked Chip so much, and why he asserted that stasis fields should never be allowed to interact with one another. It struck her that all the mathematical and engineering documentation was no more than hand-waving over hard-won superstition. To Hayley, it was less science and more a religious injunction that dragons should never be allowed to kiss after midnight.

Somewhere along the way, musicians had discovered that spinning Gyges discs beneath the audible frequencies could persuade dry rock to participate. Canyon walls hummed. Loose stones moved. The point at which the surrounding landscape joined in became known as the Drop.
Over time, jokes about what was causing the various noises acquired enough repetition to become theology. Ludwig presided over the beat and anything sufficiently percussive to feel like thunder. Zildjian favoured crashes, metallic violence and sudden structural excitement. Rickenbacker occupied the frequencies beneath hearing and had particular responsibility for vibration travelling through the ground.
By the time the Order of the Perpetual Drop formalised matters, a full service required three Gyges discs.
One for each god.
Hayley reread that part.
The Order eventually abandoned the American desert after quake-trance became sufficiently commercial that authentic geological experiences could be booked at regular intervals, and relocated to a bankrupt trance ashram in Goa whose previous operators had failed to monetise enlightenment.
There the music became wet quake-trance.
The distinction turned out to matter.
The old desert performances worked against dry exposed rock. Goa offered saturated ground, groundwater, mud, estuaries and the Arabian Sea, all of which proved considerably more willing to pass vibrations around. The services became deeper, stranger and increasingly successful. The Order attributed this to Rickenbacker being more communicative in the tropics.
Fridge Force eventually found another explanation.
During one of the Order’s largest services, all three discs entered an unusually coherent state. The effects around the Goa site were impressive enough for the worshippers to consider the evening a success.
At Rotherhithe, several thousand kilometres away, a substantial part of London began behaving as though the same stasis event were happening there.
Hayley slowed down.
There was no corresponding equipment in London. No Gyges installation. No laboratory. Fridge Force spent nearly a year eliminating explanations which had the advantage of sounding sane before accepting the one supported by the telemetry.
The three fields in Goa and the destruction at Rotherhithe constituted a single multidimensionally colocated resonant concurrency event.
Hayley went back to the earlier explanation and made another attempt at it.
Nothing had travelled from Goa to Rotherhithe in any ordinary sense. For the duration of the resonance, the Gyges fabric had treated both locations as parts of the same event.
That made Chip’s phrase considerably less reassuring.
The Order had its own explanation. Rickenbacker governed distant vibration and, according to doctrine, did not need to travel beneath the world because he was already beneath every part of it.
Hayley preferred that explanation. It had fewer syllables.
