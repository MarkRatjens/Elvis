**Locations**: Faircloud Apartments; Halo’s apartment, unit 2564; building forecourt; garbage room; service dock.

**Tone and Voice Sentence**: "No job is worth this taxing shit."

**Synopsis**:
At Faircloud Apartments, Hayley’s investigation into Halo’s unit is met with suspicion by the concierge, Jody, whose panic under questioning reveals a covert communication with a hidden party claiming to send a team in five minutes. Exploiting Jody’s hasty departure, Hayley acquires a maintenance keycard and accesses Halo’s residence, only to find it uninhabited and devoid of personal belongings. As she inspects the empty space, a PAPER Tactical Response hover-copter attacks the apartment, leaving it riddled with damage. Hayley narrowly evades capture by fleeing to the garbage room and escaping through the service dock.
