**Locations**: Kathy's medical examination room; equipped with advanced monitoring devices, including the SARA machine.

**Tone and Voice Sentence**: "I need people who see my child as a person, not a specimen."

**Synopsis**:
During an advanced prenatal check-up, Kathy uses the SARA (Superconducting Array for Reproductive Assessment) machine to examine the neural activity of Sarah’s unborn child, Little Clinton. While initial ultrasounds show normal development, SARA reveals unprecedented patterns of neural activity—geometric sequences and purposeful rhythms resembling a language or signal, mirroring the patterns Sarah has seen in her dreams and linking back to prophetic symbols found in the Gyges Disc experiments.

Sarah experiences a vivid mental flash while in the SARA machine’s cradle, solidifying her belief that Little Clinton is not only developing but "preparing" for something extraordinary. Kathy, equally alarmed and fascinated by the unprecedented coherence of the readings, reassures Sarah of Little Clinton’s health while concealing her own mounting anxieties about the implications of the data. 

Fearful of Eloise’s potential exploitation of her baby, Sarah demands Kathy keep the findings secret, framing Little Clinton as a person, not an experimental subject. Kathy hesitates, recognizing Eloise’s likely involvement in this situation from the beginning, but ultimately resolves to conceal the results, understanding the stakes of protecting Sarah and the child. Turning off the machine and applying a patch for clarity, Kathy prepares herself to lie convincingly to Eloise, committing her loyalty to Sarah’s trust and humanity over scientific or political gain.
