4. Chip / final catastrophe
This should be the least explanatory of all because the reader already owns most of the machinery.
Chip sees the final apparatus.
What has physically changed?
The harnesses are moving around the ellipse while the discs rotate inside them.
He notices first.
He wants Johnny to stop it.
Then he recognises something from an appendix nobody else had reason to care about.
The explanation of closed resonance should therefore arrive under severe pressure and in fragments. Chip should not suddenly become a lecturer because the novel has reached the chapter containing the answer.
The strongest sequence is probably:
Chip sees movement → understands → tries to stop Johnny → Johnny refuses because the machine is working → Chip supplies only enough of the Goa anomaly to explain why working is the problem.
Return before source.
Remote locus coincident with generator.
Fixed Goa geometry could not hold it.
This machine changes geometry continuously.
Enough.
Then get back to what the machine is doing.
