The Domain — the meritocracy's own ground-level playground down at the harbour, the paddock that has held kings and mobs and galas. The Elvi's crew arrives incognito, under the classical-concert cover story on the permit, and raises the stage over several days without alerting the authorities. The place's history and exclusivity land as the covert build grinds on. Ends on the tip into the concert: the ground taken by stealth and rigged for rock and roll — that was for the weekend. A rough scale only — hundreds of thousands, perhaps half of them Airborne teenagers indistinguishable from the Cloudborne — not a pinned figure.
