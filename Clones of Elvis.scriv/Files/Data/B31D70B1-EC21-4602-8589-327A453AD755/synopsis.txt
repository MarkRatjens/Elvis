Hayley brings the surviving material to Sarah but refuses to examine it on Shakti equipment. Sarah produces her antique holoscreen and reveals, almost incidentally, that it belonged to her mother. Its isolation from the modern Shakti environment makes it useful now. Sarah and Hayley examine the material together. Hayley supplies the investigation; Sarah supplies the technical/security reading of what matters. Between them they recognise uncomfortable parallels between the old project and Project Kebab's increasing use of interacting discs. Nothing proves that Eloise intends to weaponise Kebab, but Sarah realises Eloise possessed directly relevant experience—and relevant warnings—which she has never disclosed. Sarah decides that this is no longer something she can simply leave Hayley to watch.
