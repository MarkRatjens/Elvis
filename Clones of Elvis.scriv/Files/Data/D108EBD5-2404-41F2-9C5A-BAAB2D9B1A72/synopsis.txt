**Locations**: Suburban backseat, location shifting throughout urban settings.

**Tone and Voice Sentence**: “I must find Sarah at all costs, even if it costs me my exceedingly valuable bachelorhood..”

**Plot Summary**:
- Tony awakens in the backseat of a suburban vehicle, ambushed and facing Alexander Shakti. Their interaction quickly descends into verbal sparring, as Alexander channels his disdain and ulterior motives with elegant manipulation.
- Accusations against Tony pile high—ranging from conspiracy with suspicious Sages, theft of the Gyges Disc, and associations with bombings—while Alexander dials up his leverage, intertwining personal grievances with duty.
- The interrogation derails as Alexander’s true obsession surfaces: a frantic desire to locate Sarah Jacobson, whom he claims is fated to be with him under delusions of romantic destiny and cosmic significance. Alexander pressures Tony for Sarah’s whereabouts, vacillating between threats and affections.
- Tony resists, unsure what’s more alarming: Alexander’s handling of the charges against him or his growing dangerous obsession with Sarah. Alexander exits, leaving Tony’s fate precariously unbalanced between accusation and survival.
