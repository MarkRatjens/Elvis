	•	The welcome becomes confident, even proprietorial. The necks regard themselves as the hosts and victors of the evening. One brief incident can carry their pride in Shakti’s superior equipment or their participation in the broadcast.
	•	Government troops and equipment move inside. The private guards help them get where they need to go, believing their purposes coincide.
	•	End with the UDF admitted and positioned among the Shakti forces. The hosts still have their weapons and expect to retain authority.
