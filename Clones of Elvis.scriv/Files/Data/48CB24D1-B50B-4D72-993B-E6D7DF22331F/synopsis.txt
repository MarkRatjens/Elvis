A note on the socks: In 2009, Fred Mason of Roberts Creek, Australia, sent the message “Have you got all our missing socks? Love, Earth” towards Gliese 581d as part of the Hello from Earth project. I discovered this after writing my own missing-socks message. Terry Pratchett had already declared the phenomenon “a fairly simple little cliché”, so I claim no ownership of humanity’s oldest laundry problem. I am nevertheless delighted that an Australian got the question into space first.
