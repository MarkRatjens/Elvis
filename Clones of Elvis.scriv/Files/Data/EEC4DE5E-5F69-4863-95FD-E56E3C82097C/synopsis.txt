**Locations**: The mansion. Tony’s room; the lobby.

**Tone and Voice Sentence**: "Evolve me tender, Evolve me true."

**Synopsis**:
After Kathy confirms Tony is not the father of Sarah's baby, the Elvi clones begin to force him from the mansion. Tony is dragged from his room while struggling in vain. As they descend the stairs, Sarah intervenes, demanding they stop. The Elvi disregard her, moving to continue ejecting Tony. Cornered, Tony begins singing improvised lyrics, including, “Evolve me tender, Evolve me true.” The clones halt and join in his song, pausing the eviction.
