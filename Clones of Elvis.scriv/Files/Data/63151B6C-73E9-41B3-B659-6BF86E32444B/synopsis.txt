The Elvi organise the mission to steal Gyges discs from mansions in the Mountains.

They drive up the Great Western Goat Track in a convoy of old white Lincoln Continentals (In varying degrees of common disrepair) with blue lights flashing and sirens blaring. 

What ensues is a night of comic mayhem as the Elvi hit one mansion after another with the same modus operandi — a combination of hi-tech thwarting of Fridge Force alarms (possibly involving the playing of Elvi music which somehow disrupts the security —becuase Elvi music is so subversive?) and slapstick small and grab.

Johnny arranges to discreetly meet Tony at a cafe in a mountain village, but is alarmed to find the village high street a full of white Lincoln continentals and the quiet cafe has been forceable commandeered. Inside, he meets Tony and a battalion of Elvi wise guys. 

Johnny asks Tony to hand over the discs, expecting to be handed a package large enough for three, he is startled when an Elvis thumps a guitar case on table and opens it. The case is full of Gyges Discs. 

He is even more startled when give more Elvi slam five more guitar cases on three more tables and reveal, they too are laden with Gyges Discs.
