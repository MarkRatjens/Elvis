**Locations**: The large living area in the recording studio; the kitchen; the bar.

**Synopsis**:
Tony joins the band and crew for breakfast in the luxurious yet chaotic living quarters of the recording studio. While Elvisettes dote on their respective Elvi, Tony wrestles with a growing sense of suffocation and misplaced adoration by followers camping outside. His discomfort is compounded by the pervasive gifts from fans, including a live python and a recently destroyed coffin.

The band presses Tony for new lyrics, but he stalls with vague assurances, deflating the group's energy as they grow eager to record completed tracks. The atmosphere turns raucous when Rusty proposes a comically bad song about his blue suede shoes, prompting Steve to literally shoot a TV screen in frustration. Tony attempts to mediate, reminding the group of their progress under his guidance, but his influence feels tenuous amidst the growing pressure to live up to his "Great Teacher" persona. Resigned, Tony retreats from the room, seeking solace and inspiration, but his struggles to produce new material continue to weigh heavily on him.
