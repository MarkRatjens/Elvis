**Locations**: Mansion under house arrest, luxurious bedroom with oppressive decor.

**Tone and Voice Sentence**: "Sarah morbidly watched the show ever since the Elvi offered her—that is, jailed her in—the mansion, ‘for her own good.’"

**Plot Summary**:
- Sarah, held under luxurious house arrest by the Elvi, wakes up in distressing physical and psychological discomfort, highly unaccustomed to sickness and frailty. Her garish surroundings amplify her misery.
- Each day, the mansion’s screens broadcast episodes of "Curb," showcasing Tony undergoing brutal interrogations by goons under the guise of confessions. Sarah, feeling a tenuous connection to Tony through these broadcasts, struggles to process his resilience, blurred continuity errors, and apparent rapid physical recovery between episodes.
- Battling despair and suspicion, Sarah critically analyzes the broadcasts, suspecting their staging and manipulative editing while deeply disturbed by Tony’s uncanny abilities and the implied violence.
- Amid her reflection, Sarah’s physical and emotional exhaustion culminates in a symbolic moment of rejection when she shoots one of the mansion’s screens, signaling her breaking point and disillusionment with her captors’ imposed narrative.
