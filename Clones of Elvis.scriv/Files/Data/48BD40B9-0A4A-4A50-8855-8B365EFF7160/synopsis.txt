In every Chilli-positive Elvis she had studied, the viral construct remained separate from the host chromosomes. However much of it they carried, it was still Chilli and they were still Elvi.
Chanta’s sequence crossed the boundary.
The software flagged reads that began as human DNA, ran through the Chilli construct, then became human again. The junctions were clean. Chilli was not merely circulating through Chanta’s cells. It was part of his genome.
Kathy stared at that for a while.
The distinction mattered. The Elvi had acquired Chilli from somewhere. Chanta had the machinery written into him.
It did not prove he was where the infection had begun, but it made the question considerably less academic.
The other results supplied arithmetic.
Kathy had already known that the amount of Chilli mattered against Pickle. Now she had Elvi carrying wildly different amounts of it, and, courtesy of the Great Teacher, a rapidly expanding body of information about who had slept with whom.
The highest concentrations clustered among the Elvines who had slept with Chanta himself.
Kathy started asking questions.
Who else had they slept with?
Who had those people slept with?
The first step was easy. The second required more tact. Beyond that, the Humpenings had produced chains that became increasingly difficult to reconstruct, particularly when several participants had enthusiastically taken the Great Teacher’s decree to mean that keeping records would demonstrate a disappointing lack of faith.
There were enough clean chains.
Direct from Chanta, Chilli concentrations were highest.
One lover removed, they were lower.
Another lover removed, lower again.
Kathy stopped sorting the samples by age, symptoms and apparent rejuvenation and sorted them by distance from Chanta.
The further Chilli travelled, the less of it arrived.
That made sense of something else she had been seeing. The Elvi closest to Chanta in the chains were also the ones whose scars were vanishing fastest, whose joints had stopped complaining and whose apparent ages were becoming increasingly detached from the dates on their birth records. Further out, the effects diminished with the Chilli.
Whatever Chilli did inside an Elvis, it did not restore what had been lost in transmission. Chanta could keep producing it. The Elvi could pass it on, but each passage left the next recipient with less.
The Humpenings had not spread a cure through the Elvi population.
They had diluted one.
Chanta had been right about sex.
He had been wrong about arithmetic.
By the sixth degree from the Great Teacher, there was often too little Chilli left to protect anybody from anything.
